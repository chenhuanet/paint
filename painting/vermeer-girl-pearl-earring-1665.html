《戴珍珠耳环的少女》Girl with a Pearl Earring

欧洲Europe·油画（布面）油画（布面）可自由商用Free for commercial use绘画Painting

《戴珍珠耳环的少女》是一幅类型画（类型画），而不是某位特定人物的肖像，更像是对类型和表情的练习。少女回头看向我们，嘴微张；蓝色头巾和黄色衣服形成冷暖对比，珍珠在她的脖子上捕捉到一点高光，就好像它是房间里唯一的光源。背景是一片深色，没有窗户，也没有家具。

维米尔没有赋予她身份；他只呈现了被看到的瞬间，好像她正要开口说话或正要转身离开。正是这种悬而未决，使这幅画在三个多世纪里一直紧绷着观者的视线。

Girl with a Pearl Earring is a tronie, not a portrait of a named sitter but an exercise in type and expression. The girl turns her head back towards us, lips parted; the blue turban and yellow garment set up a cold-warm contrast, and the pearl catches a small highlight at her neck, as if it were the only light source in the room. The background is a dark field with no window, no furniture.

Vermeer does not give her an identity; he gives only the instant of being seen, as if she is about to speak or about to turn away. That suspension has kept the picture taut for more than three centuries.
