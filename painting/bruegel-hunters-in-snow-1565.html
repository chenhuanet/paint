《雪中猎人》Hunters in the Snow (Winter)

欧洲Europe·油画（木板）油画（木板）可自由商用Free for commercial use绘画Painting

《雪中猎人》是老彼得·勃鲁盖尔一年十二个月系列中最寒冷、最宁静的一幅画。猎人和猎狗从右侧山脊下行，他们的足迹在雪地上拖出一条斜线；左边的村庄屋顶被雪压得下沉，远处的池塘上有人滑冰，灰蓝色的山脉封住了天际线。画面没有中心人物，但视线会沿着山脊、树线和冰面一直延伸到远方。

老彼得·勃鲁盖尔描绘的北方冬日空气仿佛可以呼吸：铅灰色的天空、黑色的树木、脏雪。人类的形象只是风景的一部分，这种“小人物、大自然”的视角在后来的荷兰风景画和浪漫主义中得到了呼应。

Hunters in the Snow is the coldest and quietest panel of Bruegel's series of the months. Hunters and dogs descend a ridge from the right, their tracks dragging a diagonal line through the snow; village roofs sag under snow on the left, skaters circle a pond beyond, and grey-blue mountains close the horizon. There is no central figure, yet the eye is led along ridge, treeline and ice into the distance.

Bruegel paints northern winter air as something breathable: leaden sky, black trees, dirty snow. The human figures are only part of the landscape, and this "small people, large nature" view echoes later through Dutch landscape painting and Romanticism.
