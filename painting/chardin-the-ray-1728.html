《鳐鱼》The Ray (La Raie)

欧洲Europe·油画（布面）Oil on canvas可自由商用Free for commercial use绘画Painting

《鳐鱼》是夏尔丹1728年进入皇家学院的接受作品，也是静物画的一次宣言。一条剖开的鳐鱼倒挂在铁钩上，银白的腹部翻出，血色内脏垂落；左侧一只猫踩着牡蛎壳，右侧是酒瓶、陶罐、刀与白布。所有东西都摆在粗糙的石台上，没有神话，没有寓意，只有厨房里真实的气味。

夏尔丹用厚涂的白色高光表现鱼皮的湿润，用暗褐背景把物体推到前景。它让“日常”成为值得凝视的主题，也预告了后来库尔贝与马奈对平凡事物的尊重。

Chardin's *The Ray* was his reception piece for the Royal Academy in 1728, and a manifesto for still life. A gutted ray hangs from a hook, its silver belly opened, red entrails spilling; a cat steps among oyster shells on the left, while bottles, a jar, a knife and a white cloth occupy the right. Everything sits on a rough stone ledge. There is no myth and no allegory, only the smell of a kitchen.

Chardin uses thick white highlights for the wet skin of the fish and a dark brown ground to push the objects forward. He makes the everyday worth looking at, preparing the way for Courbet and Manet's respect for ordinary things.
