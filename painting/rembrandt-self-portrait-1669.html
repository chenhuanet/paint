《双圆自画像》Self-Portrait with Two Circles

欧洲Europe·油画（布面）油画（布面）可自由商用Free for commercial use绘画Painting

《双圆自画像》是伦勃朗晚期自画像中最安静的一幅。他坐在画架前，手握画笔和调色板，转向观众；在他身后的墙上，两圈淡色的圆圈像几何练习或未完成的宣言一样被描绘着。脸部有光照，眼角和嘴角向下，皮肤已经不再年轻。

伦勃朗画了数十幅自画像；这幅没有戏剧性的动作，只有一个人在自己的工作室里平静地面对衰老。那两个圆圈的意义从未得到确定；它们像未解的方程一样留在画面上。

Self-Portrait with Two Circles is the quietest of Rembrandt's late self-portraits. He sits at his easel with brush and palette, turning towards the viewer; on the wall behind, two circles are sketched in pale paint, like a geometry exercise or an unfinished manifesto. The face is lit, the corners of eye and mouth drawn down, the skin no longer young.

Rembrandt painted dozens of self-portraits; this one has no dramatic action, only a man in his studio facing old age calmly. What the two circles mean has never been settled; they leave the picture like an equation still to be solved.
