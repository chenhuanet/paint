《哀悼基督》Lamentation

欧洲Europe·湿壁画Fresco可自由商用Free for commercial use绘画Painting

在帕多瓦的斯克罗维尼教堂，乔托把拜占庭的圣像变成了有重量、有呼吸的身体。玛丽靠近基督的脸，约翰张开双臂，抹大拉的玛利亚握着他的脚；一个斜斜的岩石台阶把目光引向死去的身体，把整个场景压在左下角。在天空中，天使在哭泣，他们的悲伤几乎可见。

乔托把场景放在灰蓝色的岩石和土地上，而不是金色背景里，用体积感、空间深度和可识别的表情代替了象征性的秩序。衣服有了厚度，脸上有了阴影，悲伤也有了不同的层次。艺术史上通常把这一刻视为西方绘画从圣像走向自然的转折点。

In the Scrovegni Chapel in Padua, Giotto turned the Byzantine icon into bodies that weigh and breathe. Mary bends close to Christ's face, John throws his arms wide, Mary Magdalene holds the feet; a diagonal rock ledge drives the eye down to the dead figure and presses the whole group into the lower left corner. In the sky, angels weep, their grief almost visible.

Giotto set the scene on grey-blue rock and earth rather than against a gold ground, replacing symbolic order with volume, spatial depth and recognisable expression. Drapery acquires thickness, faces acquire shadow, and grief acquires different degrees. Art history often treats this moment as the point where Western painting turns from icon towards nature.
