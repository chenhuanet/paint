《红、蓝、黄构图》Composition II in Red, Blue and Yellow

欧洲Europe·油画（布面）Oil on canvas可自由商用Free for commercial use绘画Painting

蒙德里安的《红、蓝、黄构图》只包含黑色线条、白色背景和三个基础色平面。一个大红色矩形占据主导位置；蓝色位于左下角，一个小黄位于右下角；黑色线条不对称移动，这里厚、那里细，追求平衡而不是形成网格。

蒙德里安称这种关系为新造型主义：水平和垂直代表宇宙的两种基本力量，原色代表纯净能量，白色代表空间。没有故事，但画作需要长时间凝视：每次注意力的转移，颜色的重量都会改变。

Mondrian's *Composition with Red, Blue and Yellow* contains only black lines, white ground and three primary planes. A large red rectangle dominates; blue sits at lower left, a small yellow at lower right; the black lines move asymmetrically, thicker here, thinner there, seeking balance rather than executing a grid.

Mondrian called this relationship Neo-Plasticism: horizontal and vertical stand for the two basic forces of the universe, primary colours for pure energy, white for space. There is no story, yet the painting asks for prolonged looking: with every shift of attention, the weight of the colours changes.
