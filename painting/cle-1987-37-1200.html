《月下山景》Mountain Landscape in Moonlight

中国China·hanging scroll; ink on silkhanging scroll; ink on silk可自由商用Free for commercial use绘画Painting

月下山景，1200年。中国。绢本水墨；Image: 54.3 x 36 cm (21 3/8 x 14 3/16 in.); Overall: 139.6 x 41.4 cm (54 15/16 x 16 5/16 in.)。

以山体、路径与雾气组织观看，山不是背景，而是叙事的主体。

Mountain Landscape in Moonlight, 1200s. China, Southern Song dynasty (1127–1279) to Yuan dynasty (1271–1368). Hanging scroll; ink on silk; image: 54.3 x 36 cm (21 3/8 x 14 3/16 in.); overall: 139.6 x 41.4 cm (54 15/16 x 16 5/16 in.). The Cleveland Museum of Art, Mr. and Mrs. William H. Marlatt Fund, 1987.37

Within “Landscape and Nature”, under “山”: Organises looking through mass, path and mist; the mountain is the subject, not the backdrop.
