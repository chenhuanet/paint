《无量寿佛唐卡》Thangka with Amitayus

跨地域Cross-regional·painting on fabricpainting on fabric可自由商用Free for commercial use绘画Painting

这幅唐卡包含一系列来自拉贾斯坦邦的印度教俗家信徒的捐赠者肖像，他们为一座佛教寺庙赞助了这幅画。

这件作品把神圣形象放进可读的构图里：人物、手势、器物与边框共同规定观看的顺序。先看中心，再看两侧与下方，图像因而成为一套可以被重复使用的信仰语言。

This thangka includes a series of donor portraits of Hindu laypeople from Rajasthan, who sponsored this painting for a Buddhist temple.

The work arranges sacred figures inside a legible composition: bodies, gestures, objects and borders prescribe the order of looking. Read the centre first, then the sides and the lower edge; the image becomes a reusable language of faith.
