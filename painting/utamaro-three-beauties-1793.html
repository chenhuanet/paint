《三位美人》Three Beauties of the Present Day

东亚East Asia·木版画Woodblock print可自由商用Free for commercial use绘画Painting

《三位美人》是喜多川歌磨的大头画（大头画）的代表作。画中三位著名女性并排而立：玉屋的富士本丰姬、浪屋喜多，以及高岛大久。如她们的面容、发型、和服花纹与目光各不相同；背景使用云母粉，使深色背景闪闪发光，衬托出她们的面庞。

歌磨画的不是地位，而是名气：这些女性被城市所关注、讨论和注视。大头画的形式将肖像放大至半身，使观者不得不与她们对视，也让浮世绘从叙事转向一种现代的明星形象。

Three Beauties of the Present Day is the representative work of Kitagawa Utamaro's ōkubi-e (large-head pictures). Three celebrated women are placed side by side: Tomimoto Toyohina of the Tamaya, Naniwaya Kita, and Takashima Ohisa. Their faces, hairstyles, robe patterns and glances differ; mica powder in the background makes a shimmering dark ground from which the faces emerge.

Utamaro paints not status but fame itself: women who are consumed, discussed and looked at by the city. The large-head format enlarges the portrait to half-length, forcing the viewer to meet their eyes, and turns ukiyo-e from narrative toward a modern kind of star image.
