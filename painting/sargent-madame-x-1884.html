《X夫人》Madame X (Madame Pierre Gautreau)

北美North America·油画（布面）Oil on canvas可自由商用Free for commercial use绘画Painting

《X女士》描绘的是维珍妮·阿梅莉·阿韦尼奥·戈特罗，一位著名的巴黎美女。她侧身站立，一只手搭在桌子上，头向后仰，露出一张苍白的脸和鼻子；她黑色缎面礼服的肩带滑落，肌肤与布料形成冷峻的对比。这幅画在1884年的沙龙展引起了轰动：不是因为裸露，而是因为姿态太自傲、太不受控制。萨金特后来重新画了肩带，但这种挑衅感并没有消失。它仍然是19世纪末“新女性”最尖锐的肖像之一。

Madame X portrays Virginie Amélie Avegno Gautreau, a celebrated Parisian beauty. She stands in profile, one hand resting on a table, her head thrown back to reveal a pale face and nose; the strap of her black satin dress has slipped, skin and fabric set in cold contrast.

The picture caused a scandal at the 1884 Salon: not for nudity but for a posture too proud, too ungovernable. Sargent later repainted the strap, yet the provocation did not disappear. It remains one of the sharpest portraits of the late-nineteenth-century "new woman."
