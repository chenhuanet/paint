《栖居于龙眠山》Dwelling in the Longmian ("Sleeping Dragon") Mountains

中国China·Handscroll mounted as three album leaves; ink and color on silkHandscroll mounted as three album leaves; ink and color on silk可自由商用Free for commercial use绘画Painting

《龙眠山庄图》，12-13世纪。李公麟（中国，约1049–1106年）。

它不把自然当作背景，而是让山、水、树、雾成为画面的骨架。视线沿路径移动，近景与远峰之间留出呼吸，风景因此有了可以进入的空间。

Dwelling in the Longmian ("Sleeping Dragon") Mountains, 1100s-1200s. Li Gonglin (Chinese, c. 1049–1106).

Nature is not a backdrop here but the armature of the picture. The eye follows paths, pauses between foreground and distant peaks, and the landscape becomes a space one can enter.
