《长颈圣母》Madonna with the Long Neck

欧洲Europe·油画（木板）油画（木板）可自由商用Free for commercial use绘画Painting

《长颈圣母》把圣母、圣婴和一群天使安排在一个不确定的空间里。圣母坐在中央，脖子拉得异常长，右手触碰着圣婴，但她的身体似乎随时会滑出画框；圣婴体型偏大，睡着的姿势几乎像古代雕塑。右侧一排纤细的柱子没有柱头，背景看起来既像舞台布景又像未完成的建筑。

帕尔米贾尼诺故意打破比例和透视，把优雅推向失衡的边缘。这种美学后来被称为“矫饰主义”，它不再依赖文艺复兴的测量标准，而是依赖线条、姿态以及观者的微妙不适感。

Madonna with the Long Neck stages the Virgin, Child and a crowd of angels in an uncertain space. The Virgin sits at the centre, her neck stretched impossibly long, her right hand touching the Child while her body seems ready to slide out of the frame; the Child is oversized, his sleeping pose almost like an antique sculpture. A row of slender columns at the right lacks capitals, and the background reads as both stage set and unfinished architecture.

Parmigianino deliberately violates proportion and perspective, pushing grace to the edge of imbalance. This aesthetic, later called Mannerism, no longer trusts Renaissance measurement; it trusts line, pose and the viewer's unease.
