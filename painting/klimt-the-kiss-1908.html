《吻》The Kiss

欧洲Europe·油画（布面，金箔）Oil on canvas with gold leaf可自由商用Free for commercial use绘画Painting

《吻》展示了两个人物跪在花草繁茂的草地边缘：男人俯身靠向女人，她的脸朝上，闭着眼睛。他们的身体被一件巨大的金色斗篷包裹，斗篷上覆盖着圆形、矩形和眼睛形状；男人的衣服是黑白条纹的，女人的衣服则点缀着彩色圆点。背景是闪烁的金色场面，既像马赛克又像夜空。

克里姆特将维也纳分离派的装饰推到极限：爱不仅是叙事，而是一种被包围的存在状态。金箔让画作减轻了重量，并将这对情侣从现实中提升到永恒的图案中。

The Kiss shows two figures kneeling at the edge of a flower meadow: the man bends over the woman, her face tilted up, eyes closed. Their bodies are wrapped in a vast gold cloak covered with circles, rectangles and eye-like shapes; his garment is striped black and white, hers patterned with coloured dots. The background is a shimmering gold field, mosaic and night sky at once.

Klimt pushes Vienna Secession ornament to its limit: love is not a narrative but a state of being enclosed. Gold leaf removes weight from the picture and lifts the couple out of reality into an eternal pattern.
