《最后的晚餐》The Last Supper

欧洲Europe·湿壁画（蛋彩与油）Tempera and oil on plaster可自由商用Free for commercial use绘画Painting

在米兰圣玛利亚感恩教堂的食堂墙上，列奥纳多用一点透视法布置了《最后的晚餐》。十二位使徒被分成四组，每组三个人，从中心向外延展。基督刚说过：“你们中有一个将背叛我”；他的双手摊开在桌上，而犹大在阴影中抓着钱包，身体向后倾。消失点位于基督的头部，长桌的线条将每一个声音都引向中心。

因为列奥纳多使用干蛋彩和油画而非真正的湿壁画，这幅画完成不久后就开始剥落。现在所保存的是一幅被时间不断修补的图像；其中的人类心理编排仍然是西方叙事绘画的入门课。

On the refectory wall of Santa Maria delle Grazie in Milan, Leonardo stages the Last Supper in a room built by one-point perspective. The twelve apostles are arranged in four groups of three, rippling outward from the centre. Christ has just said, "One of you will betray me"; his hands lie open on the table, while Judas, in shadow, clutches his purse and leans back. The vanishing point sits at Christ's head, and the long table lines gather every voice towards the centre.

Because Leonardo worked in dry tempera and oil rather than true fresco, the painting began to flake soon after completion. What survives is an image endlessly patched by time; its choreography of human psychology remains a primer of Western narrative painting.
