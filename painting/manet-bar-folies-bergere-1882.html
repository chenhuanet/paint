《女神游乐厅的吧台》A Bar at the Folies-Bergère

欧洲Europe·油画（布面）Oil on canvas可自由商用Free for commercial use绘画Painting

《福乐喜剧酒吧》展示了巴黎音乐厅的最后一夜。一个酒吧女侍站在柜台后，双手放在大理石台面上，胸前别着一朵花；她面前摆着酒杯、酒瓶和一盘橘子。她身后的镜子反射出整个大厅：吊灯、观众、包厢，以及一个正和她交谈的男人——这也是观众实际上所处的位置。

马奈画的反射并不完全正确，透视也略有偏差。酒吧女侍的表情空洞，既不欢迎也不拒绝。整幅画成为现代城市生活的标志性形象：人群、光线、消费，以及一个无法接近的人。

A Bar at the Folies-Bergère shows the last night of a Paris music hall. A barmaid stands behind the counter, both hands resting on the marble, a flower at her breast; glasses, bottles and a plate of oranges are arranged in front of her. The mirror behind her reflects the whole hall: chandeliers, audience, boxes, and a man speaking to her — the place where the viewer actually stands.

Manet paints the reflection not quite correctly and lets the perspective slip. The barmaid's expression is blank, neither welcoming nor refusing. The picture becomes a defining image of modern city life: crowd, light, consumption, and a person who cannot be reached.
