《荷拉斯兄弟之誓》Oath of the Horatii

欧洲Europe·油画（布面）Oil on canvas可自由商用Free for commercial use绘画Painting

《霍拉提兄弟的誓言》把罗马历史中的一个瞬间变成了舞台剧。三个兄弟肩并肩站在父亲面前，手臂伸向剑；父亲举起三把剑，背后是拱门和阴影。右侧，女人们一起倒下，母亲和姐妹低下头，知道战争会让她们失去兄弟或丈夫。

大卫用垂直的柱子、硬朗的轮廓和几乎无笔触的画面，把新古典主义的“美德”变成了几何形状。男性的身体是直线，女性的身体是曲线；誓言和悲伤在画布的两侧被区分开来。

Oath of the Horatii turns a moment of Roman history into theatre. Three brothers stand shoulder to shoulder before their father, arms extended towards the swords; the father raises the three blades, an archway and shadow behind him. On the right, the women collapse together, mother and sisters bowing their heads, knowing the war will cost them brothers or husbands.

David uses vertical columns, hard contours and a nearly brushless surface to turn Neoclassical "virtue" into geometry. Male bodies are straight lines, female bodies curves; oath and grief are divided between the two sides of the canvas.
