《下十字架》The Descent from the Cross

欧洲Europe·油画（木板）油画（木板）可自由商用Free for commercial use绘画Painting

鲁本斯的《基督下十字架》，是为安特卫普大教堂创作的三联画的中央面板，展示了基督的身体沿着十字架斜滑下，被白色布料承托。亚利马太的约瑟夫和尼哥底母从上方拉拽布料，约翰在下方接住双足，圣母、抹大拉的马利亚和其他妇女跪在左侧。人物形成一个旋转的菱形，光线沿着布料和肌肉流动。

鲁本斯将巴洛克的重量感、运动感和血腥感推向极致：这不是安静的哀悼，而是仍在进行中的拉拽。白色布料既是裹尸布也是反光板，让所有视线都集中在身体上。

Rubens's Descent from the Cross, the central panel of a triptych for Antwerp Cathedral, shows Christ's body sliding diagonally from the cross, caught in a white sheet. Joseph of Arimathea and Nicodemus haul the cloth from above, John receives the feet below, and the Virgin, Mary Magdalene and other women kneel at the left. The figures form a rotating diamond, light running along the sheet and across muscle.

Rubens pushes Baroque weight, motion and blood to an extreme: this is not quiet mourning but a hauling that is still under way. The white cloth is both shroud and reflector, turning every gaze towards the body.
