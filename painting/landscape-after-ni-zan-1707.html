《仿倪瓒山水图》Landscape after Ni Zan

中国China·Hanging scroll; ink and light color on paperHanging scroll; ink and light color on paper可自由商用Free for commercial use绘画Painting

王原祁是康熙皇帝（1662–1722年在位）朝廷的官员，出生于一个艺术世家。

人物被放在一个可读的姿态里：服饰、手势与目光共同说明身份。画像不只是记录长相，也在安排一个人如何被看见。

Wang Yuanqi, an official in Emperor Kangxi’s (reigned 1662–1722) court, grew up in an artistic family.

The figure is placed in a readable pose: dress, gesture and gaze together declare an identity. A portrait does not merely record a face; it stages how a person is to be seen.
