《夜巡》The Night Watch

欧洲Europe·油画（布面）Oil on canvas可自由商用Free for commercial use绘画Painting

《夜巡》实际上并不是夜晚的场景。一支民兵队伍正从阿姆斯特丹的拱门里出发。队长弗朗斯·班宁克·考克穿着黑色外套，系着红色腰带，正伸手去碰他的中尉威廉·范·鲁伊滕堡的手套，中尉穿着黄色，手指着画框之外的长矛。一些人在打鼓，有些人在升旗，有些人在操纵武器；人群中间被光照到的小女孩格外显眼。

伦勃朗拒绝了传统的按排排列的群像画，而是把整个队伍描绘成一个集结的瞬间。光影在队伍中流动；每个人都在动作中，每个人都被看见。后来被称为“夜巡”，那是因为上过暗色清漆；清洗后才显现出白天出发的场景。

The Night Watch is not actually a night scene. A militia company marches out of an Amsterdam archway. Captain Frans Banninck Cocq, in black with a red sash, reaches towards the glove of his lieutenant, Willem van Ruytenburch, who wears yellow and points a lance beyond the frame. Some men drum, some raise a flag, some handle weapons; a small girl is picked out by light in the middle of the crowd.

Rembrandt refuses the conventional row-by-row group portrait and paints the company as a moment of assembly. Light and shadow move through the crowd; everyone is in motion and everyone is seen. The later title "Night Watch" came from darkened varnish; cleaning revealed a daytime departure.
