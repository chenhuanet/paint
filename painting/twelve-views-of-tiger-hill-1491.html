《虎丘十二景》Twelve Views of Tiger Hill

中国China·Album of twelve leaves; ink or ink and slight color on paperAlbum of twelve leaves; ink or ink and slight color on paper可自由商用Free for commercial use绘画Painting

《虎丘图》描绘的是位于苏州西北方几英里处的一处历史遗迹。

材料决定画面的表面：金、墨、颜料与纸、绢、板的关系，直接改变光线和颜色的走法。先看材质，再看笔触。

Views of Tiger Hill depicts a historic site a few miles northwest of Suzhou.

Material determines the surface: gold, ink, pigment and paper, silk or panel change how light and colour travel. Look at the material first, then at the brushwork.
