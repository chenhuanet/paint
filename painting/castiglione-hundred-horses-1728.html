《百骏图》One Hundred Horses

中国China·绢本设色绢本设色可自由商用Free for commercial use绘画Painting

《百马图》是意大利耶稣会士朱塞佩·卡斯蒂廖内在清朝宫廷绘制的手卷。百余匹马分布在草坡、溪流和树林间：有的站立，有的打滚，有的饮水，有的由牧人牵引。它们的身体通过细腻的明暗塑造，而树木、岩石和地面则保留了中国画的质感笔触和留白。

卡斯蒂廖内将欧洲的透视、解剖学和明暗对比引入宫廷画，同时必须与中国的材料和观画习惯妥协。结果是混合的视觉效果：马是立体的，空间却是平面的；写实与装饰在同一卷轴中并存。

One Hundred Horses is a handscroll painted at the Qing court by the Italian Jesuit Giuseppe Castiglione. More than a hundred horses are dispersed across grassy slopes, streams and trees: standing, rolling, drinking, led by herdsmen. Their bodies are modelled with fine light and shade, while trees, rocks and ground retain Chinese texture strokes and reserved space.

Castiglione brought European perspective, anatomy and chiaroscuro into court painting, and was obliged to compromise with Chinese materials and viewing habits. The result is a hybrid vision: horses in the round, space flat; realism and ornament in the same scroll.
