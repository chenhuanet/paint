南亚South Asia·gum tempera, ink, and gold on papergum tempera, ink, and gold on paper可自由商用Free for commercial use绘画Painting

宰相儿子的妻子与僧侣有染，僧侣坐在虎皮上，身着苦行僧的蓝色长袍。

材料决定画面的表面：金、墨、颜料与纸、绢、板的关系，直接改变光线和颜色的走法。先看材质，再看笔触。

The wife of the vizier’s son is having an affair with the monk, who is seated on tiger skins and wearing the blue robes of a dervish.

Material determines the surface: gold, ink, pigment and paper, silk or panel change how light and colour travel. Look at the material first, then at the brushwork.
