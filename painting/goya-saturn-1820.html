《农神吞噬其子》Saturn Devouring His Son

欧洲Europe·油画（壁画转布面）Oil on plaster transferred to canvas可自由商用Free for commercial use绘画Painting

《萨图恩吞食自己的儿子》是戈雅黑色画作中最令人震惊的一幅。萨图恩看起来像个疯狂的老头，眼睛瞪大，嘴巴张开，双手紧握着一个已经被啃掉头和胳膊的小尸体。背景是黑色的，只有肉体和白布捕捉到光线。

晚年的戈雅把自己关在索尔多庄园里，把这些作品直接画在墙上。它们既不是接受委托也不是为展览而作，更像是一个人在与恐惧斗争的私人记录。神话在这里失去了寓意，只剩下暴力本身。

Saturn Devouring His Son is the most appalling of Goya's Black Paintings. Saturn appears as a mad old man, eyes staring, mouth wide, both hands gripping a small body whose head and arm have already been bitten away. The background is black; only flesh and white cloth catch the light.

In his late years Goya shut himself in the Quinta del Sordo and painted these works directly onto the walls. They were neither commissions nor exhibition pieces, more like a private record of a man fighting fear. Myth loses its allegory here and leaves only violence itself.
