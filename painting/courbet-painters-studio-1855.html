《画室》The Painter's Studio

欧洲Europe·油画（布面）Oil on canvas可自由商用Free for commercial use绘画Painting

《画家的工作室》的副标题是“一个真正的寓言，总结了我七年的艺术和道德生活”。在画面中央，库尔贝坐在画架前画风景画，旁边有裸体模特和一个男孩。右边是他的朋友和支持者——波德莱尔、普鲁东、尚弗勒里等；左边是日常生活中的普通人：工人、农民、乞丐、一位寡妇。库尔贝把“社会”搬进了画室，把艺术创作和政治立场放在同一个空间里。这既是对学院派的挑战，也是现实主义宣言：画家不画神话，而是画他眼中的人。

The Painter's Studio carries the subtitle "A real allegory summing up seven years of my artistic and moral life." At the centre, Courbet sits at his easel painting a landscape, a nude model and a boy beside him. On the right are his friends and supporters — Baudelaire, Proudhon, Champfleury and others; on the left, ordinary people from daily life: workers, peasants, beggars, a widow.

Courbet moves "society" into the studio and puts artistic production and political stance in the same room. It is both a challenge to the Academy and a Realist manifesto: the painter does not paint myth, but the people he sees.
