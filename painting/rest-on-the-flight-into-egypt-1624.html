《逃往埃及途中歇息》Rest on the Flight into Egypt

欧洲Europe·oil on canvasoil on canvas可自由商用Free for commercial use绘画Painting

乔瓦尼·弗朗切斯科·巴尔比耶里（人称“瓜尔奇诺”）创作了《逃往埃及途中的歇息》，这是一幅圆形画，在十六、十七世纪很少使用这种圆形画布。

材料决定画面的表面：金、墨、颜料与纸、绢、板的关系，直接改变光线和颜色的走法。先看材质，再看笔触。

Giovanni Francesco Barbieri (called il Guercino) created Rest on the Flight to Egypt as a tondo, a circular canvas rarely used in the sixteenth and seventeenth centuries except in

Material determines the surface: gold, ink, pigment and paper, silk or panel change how light and colour travel. Look at the material first, then at the brushwork.
