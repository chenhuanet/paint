《虫祭》Festival of Insects

东亚East Asia·Hanging scroll; ink on paperHanging scroll; ink on paper可自由商用Free for commercial use绘画Painting

虫祭，1615年。东亚。纸本水墨；Image: 30.7 x 45 cm (12 1/16 x 17 11/16 in.); Overall: 122.5 x 55.9 cm (48 1/4 x 22 in.)。

让短暂的热闹进入图像，节庆既是娱乐，也是社会秩序的排练。

Festival of Insects, c. 1615–1868. After Motsurin Jōtō (Japanese, d. 1492). Hanging scroll; ink on paper; image: 30.7 x 45 cm (12 1/16 x 17 11/16 in.); overall: 122.5 x 55.9 cm (48 1/4 x 22 in.). The Cleveland Museum of Art, Mr. and Mrs. William H. Marlatt Fund, 1988.16

Within “City and Everyday Life”, under “节庆”: A brief festivity enters the image; celebration is also a rehearsal of social order.
