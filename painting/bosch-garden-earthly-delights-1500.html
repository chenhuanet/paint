《人间乐园》The Garden of Earthly Delights

欧洲Europe·油画（橡木板三联画）油画（橡木板三联画）可自由商用Free for commercial use绘画Painting

《人间乐园》是一幅三联画。左边的画板是伊甸园，上帝把夏娃介绍给亚当；中间是一个挤满成千上万裸体人物的花园，他们在水果、池塘、鸟类和野兽之间玩耍；右边是一个黑暗的地狱，乐器变成折磨人的工具，怪物吞噬人身。合上时，外翼显示的是灰色的创造，世界被包裹在透明球体中。

博斯把中世纪的道德寓言、炼金术符号和民间谚语都塞进了一个视觉机器里，使得快乐与惩罚连成一体。不管这是警告、讽刺，还是对某种被压抑事物的投射，一直没有定论；正是这种难以解读性，使它在五个世纪后仍然吸引观众。

The Garden of Earthly Delights is a triptych. The left panel is Eden, where God presents Eve to Adam; the centre is a garden crowded with thousands of naked figures sporting among fruit, pools, birds and beasts; the right is a dark hell where musical instruments become instruments of torture and monsters devour bodies. Closed, the outer wings show a grey creation, the world enclosed in a transparent sphere.

Bosch packs medieval moral allegory, alchemical signs and folk proverb into one visual machine, so that pleasure and punishment follow nose to tail. Whether it is warning, satire or the projection of something repressed has never been settled; that very resistance to decoding is why it still holds viewers five centuries later.
