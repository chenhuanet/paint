《蒙马特大道·夜色》The Boulevard Montmartre at Night

欧洲Europe·油画（布面）Oil on canvas可自由商用Free for commercial use绘画Painting

《蒙马特大街夜景》是皮萨罗的一系列作品之一，他从巴黎一家酒店的窗口俯视街道。大街斜向右延伸，路灯将湿漉漉的柏油路照成金色的线条；马车、行人和商店橱窗被压缩成短短的笔触。天空深蓝，建筑物是黑色的剪影。

晚年的皮萨罗遭受眼疾困扰，但在这组城市题材作品中找到了新的自由：他不再画乡村风景，而是描绘现代城市的节奏与光影。印象派的“瞬间”变成了大都市的夜脉动。

The Boulevard Montmartre at Night is one of a series Pissarro painted looking down on the street from a Paris hotel window. The boulevard runs diagonally away to the right, streetlamps turning wet asphalt into lines of gold; carriages, pedestrians and shop windows are compressed into short strokes. The sky is deep blue, the buildings black silhouettes.

Pissarro was troubled by eye disease in his late years, yet found a new freedom in this urban series: he no longer painted the countryside but the rhythm and light of the modern city. The Impressionist "instant" becomes the night pulse of a metropolis.
