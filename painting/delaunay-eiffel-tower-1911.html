《埃菲尔铁塔》Eiffel Tower (Tour Eiffel)

欧洲Europe·油画（布面）Oil on canvas可自由商用Free for commercial use绘画Painting

德拉纳伊把埃菲尔铁塔变成了一根旋转的彩色柱子。铁质结构被分解成红棕色、橙色和灰蓝色的面；云层以环形爆开，周围的公寓楼向铁塔倾斜。他没有使用立体主义的单色分析；颜色本身就创造了空间——暖色面向前推进，冷色面后退，铁质格子在其中若隐若现。

1911年的巴黎是一个充满飞机、海报、地铁和电灯的城市。这幅画让“同时性”变成了视觉体验：你无法一次看到整座铁塔，只能同时感受到它的几个面。

Delaunay turns the Eiffel Tower into a rotating column of colour. The iron structure is broken into red-brown, orange and grey-blue facets; clouds burst in rings, and the surrounding apartment blocks tilt toward the tower. He does not use the monochrome analysis of Cubism; colour itself creates space — warm planes advance, cool planes recede, and the iron lattice appears and disappears between them.

Paris in 1911 was a city of aeroplanes, posters, the Metro and electric light. The painting turns 'simultaneity' into visual experience: you cannot see the tower all at once, only feel several of its faces at the same time.
