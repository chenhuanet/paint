俵屋宗达和本阿弥光悦都与日本绘画的琳派的创立有关。

文字与图像共用同一张纸面：书法、边框、插图与留白互相挤压。阅读的顺序既是文字的，也是图像的。

Tawaraya Sotatsu and Hon’ami Koetsu are both associated with the founding of the Rimpa school of Japanese painting.

Text and image share one sheet: calligraphy, borders, illustration and blank space press against one another. The order of reading is both verbal and visual.
