《圣维罗妮卡木刻匣》Coffret with Woodcut of Saint Veronica's Vernicle

欧洲Europe·Wood, leather, iron, textile, woodcut with stenciled coloringWood, leather, iron, textile, woodcut with stenciled coloring可自由商用Free for commercial use版画Print

圣维罗妮卡木刻匣，1504年。欧洲。木刻；Overall: 28 x 18.5 x 10 cm (11 x 7 5/16 x 3 15/16 in.); Sheet with print: 29 x 19 cm (11 7/16 x 7 1/2 in.)。

以刀痕与黑白对比，把复制技术的限制变成风格。

Coffret with Woodcut of Saint Veronica's Vernicle, Coffret: c. 1515; Woodcut: c. 1509. Woodcuts by Hans Burgkmair the Elder (German, 1473–1531). Wood, leather, iron, textile, woodcut with stenciled coloring; overall: 28 x 18.5 x 10 cm (11 x 7 5/16 x 3 15/16 in.); sheet with print: 29 x 19 cm (11 7/16 x 7 1/2 in.). The Cleveland Museum of Art, Purchase from the J. H. Wade Fund, 2020.228

Within “Material and Technique”, under “木刻”: Knife marks and black-white contrast turn the limits of reproduction into style.
