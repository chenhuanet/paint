《宫娥》Las Meninas

欧洲Europe·油画（布面）油画（布面）可自由商用Free for commercial use绘画Painting

《宫娥》将观众置于国王的视线之中。在画面的中央，小公主玛格丽塔由两位宫女照料；右边站着一个侏儒和一只半睡的狗。左边，委拉斯开兹本人站在一幅巨大的画布后面，手持画笔，胸前佩戴圣地亚哥勋章。背墙的镜子中映出了国王和王后，他们正站在观众所在的位置。

通过镜子、门口和多层视线，委拉斯开兹把“谁在看谁”作为画作的主题。这既是一幅宫廷群像，也是关于绘画的画作：画家在画什么，观众站在哪里，都被留在了画框之外。

Las Meninas puts the viewer inside the king's gaze. At the centre, the young Infanta Margarita is attended by two maids of honour; to the right stand a dwarf and a half-awake dog. On the left, Velázquez himself works behind a huge canvas, brush in hand, the Order of Santiago on his chest. In the mirror on the back wall appear the king and queen, standing exactly where the viewer stands.

With a mirror, a doorway and several layers of gaze, Velázquez makes "who is looking at whom" the subject of the picture. It is at once a court group portrait and a painting about painting: what the painter is painting, and where the viewer stands, are both left outside the frame.
