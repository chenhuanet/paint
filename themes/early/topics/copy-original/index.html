复制与真迹：图像的第二条命Copy and Original: The Second Life of an Image

摹本、复制、工坊版本与印刷。复制不是真迹的反面，它常常是图像得以旅行、被记住和被改写的真正方式。Copies, replicas, workshop versions and prints. Reproduction is not the opposite of the original; it is often how an image travels, is remembered and is rewritten.
