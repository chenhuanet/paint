圣母与菩萨Madonna and Bodhisattva

同样是慈悲的形象，欧洲的圣母与南亚、东亚的菩萨如何用不同的脸、手势和光环回答同一个问题：神圣者如何接近人？Both are images of compassion. How do the European Madonna and the South and East Asian bodhisattva answer the same question with different faces, gestures and halos: how does the divine come near?
