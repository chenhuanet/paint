中亚：赫拉特、布哈拉与书页Central Asia: Herat, Bukhara and the Book

从赫拉特到布哈拉，细密画把诗、王权与花园装进书页。这里的“中亚”是帖木儿与布哈拉写本的世界，不是今天的国界。From Herat to Bukhara, miniature painting puts poetry, kingship and the garden inside the book. Here “Central Asia” is the world of Timurid and Bukharan manuscripts, not present-day borders.
