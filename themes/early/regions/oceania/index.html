大洋洲：库克航行的太平洋图像Oceania: Pacific Images of the Cook Voyages

库克航行的版画与港湾图：新西兰、塔纳、汤加与塔希提。它们是欧洲人如何画太平洋的记录，不是岛屿绘画的本地通史。Prints and harbour views from the Cook voyages: New Zealand, Tanna, Tonga and Tahiti. They record how Europeans drew the Pacific, not a local history of island painting.
