东南亚：欧洲版画里的暹罗Southeast Asia: Siam in European Prints

欧洲铜版画与泰文写本里的暹罗：宫廷、御舟、寺院与河港。先看清“谁在画东南亚”，不把欧洲人的图当成当地绘画通史。Siam in European engravings and Thai manuscripts: court, royal barges, monasteries and river ports. First ask who is drawing Southeast Asia; do not mistake European prints for a local history of painting.
