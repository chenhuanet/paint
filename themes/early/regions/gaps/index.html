区域缺口Regional Gaps

计划中的东南亚、中亚、非洲、拉丁美洲与大洋洲五个方向，本轮可用绘画不足 8 件或 3 个时段。这里如实登记，不用器物、地图或复制品充数；下一轮换来源再补。Five planned directions—Southeast Asia, Central Asia, Africa, Latin America and Oceania—do not reach eight paintings or three periods in this round. They are logged here rather than padded with objects, maps or copies.
