元代文人山水Yuan Literati Landscape

元代文人山水是汉族士人在异族统治下建立的精神领地。黄公望、倪瓒、吴镇、王蒙把画当作日记、书信和哲学：山是避难所，水是距离，空白是拒绝。笔墨不再描摹地形，而是留下一个人的性格、师承和疲惫。Yuan literati landscape is a spiritual territory built by Han scholars under foreign rule. Huang Gongwang, Ni Zan, Wu Zhen and Wang Meng use painting as diary, letter and philosophy: mountain as refuge, water as distance, blank silk as refusal. Brush and ink no longer map terrain; they record personality, lineage and fatigue.

三个特征Three traits

  • 笔墨独立：皴法、干湿、浓淡，比地形更重要。
  • 空白为境：不画水，不画云，留白就是水与云。
  • 人格即风格：倪瓒的冷、黄公望的厚、王蒙的密，都是人格。
  • Brushwork independent: texture strokes, wet and dry, dark and light matter more than terrain.
  • Emptiness as place: no water, no cloud — blank silk is water and cloud.
  • Personality as style: Ni Zan's coldness, Huang Gongwang's density, Wang Meng's intricacy are personalities.

怎么看How to look

  • 看笔：干、湿、浓、淡，哪一笔最像画家？
  • 看空白：它是水、云，还是拒绝？
  • 看题跋：谁在画上写字？他们和画家是什么关系？
  • Read the brush: dry, wet, dark, light — which mark is most like the painter?
  • Read emptiness: water, cloud or refusal?
  • Read inscriptions: who writes on the painting, and what is their relation to the painter?
