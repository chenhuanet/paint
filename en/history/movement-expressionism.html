表现主义Expressionism

表现主义把颜色和线条从描述中解放出来，让它们直接传递情绪。蒙克的尖叫穿过天空，席勒的身体像一根绷紧的神经，基尔希纳的街道像一把斜切的刀。它不关心“世界看起来怎样”，而关心“世界让人感觉怎样”。Expressionism frees colour and line from description and makes them carry emotion directly. Munch's scream passes through the sky, Schiele's body is a taut nerve, Kirchner's street is a slanted knife. It does not care how the world looks but how the world feels.

三个特征Three traits

  • 颜色即情绪：红可以是恐惧，蓝可以是窒息，绿可以是病。
  • 线条即神经：斜线、锯齿、扭曲，身体和城市一起紧张。
  • 主观空间：透视被扭曲，画面像从内部向外推。
  • Colour as emotion: red can be fear, blue suffocation, green illness.
  • Line as nerve: diagonals, jagged edges, distortion; body and city tighten together.
  • Subjective space: perspective is twisted; the picture pushes outward from inside.

怎么看How to look

  • 看颜色：它让你身体有什么反应？
  • 看线条：它是稳定的，还是像在发抖？
  • 看空间：画面是向外推，还是向内塌？
  • Read colour: how does your body react?
  • Read line: stable or trembling?
  • Read space: pushing outward or collapsing inward?
