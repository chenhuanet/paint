现实主义Realism

现实主义是一种政治选择：把学院拒绝的人、地点和身体放到画面中心。库尔贝说“我没有画天使”，米勒让拾穗者占据前景，马奈把妓女和野餐者请进沙龙，列宾让十一个纤夫拉着一艘船。它不追求美，而追求“被看见”。Realism is a political choice: put the people, places and bodies the Academy rejected at the center. Courbet says he has never painted an angel, Millet lets gleaners occupy the foreground, Manet brings a courtesan and picnickers into the Salon, Repin harnesses eleven barge haulers to a boat. It does not pursue beauty but visibility.

绘画史Painting History / 流派Movements
先看三个特征，再看三件作品；最后把它放回它所在的章节。Start with three traits, then three works; finally put it back into its chapter.

三个特征Three traits

  • 题材下沉：工人、农民、妓女、纤夫，取代神话和贵族。
  • 构图直白：人物被放在前景，背景不负责美化。
  • 观看伦理：画家不替观众判断，只把场景摆出来。
  • Subject matter moves down: workers, peasants, a courtesan, barge haulers replace myth and aristocracy.
  • Direct composition: figures in the foreground; background does not beautify.
  • Ethics of viewing: the painter does not judge for us, only presents the scene.

怎么看How to look

  • 看谁被放在前景：谁被承认了？
  • 看手和脚：它们在劳动吗？
  • 看画家：他站在什么位置？他有没有替人物说话？
  • See who is in the foreground: who is acknowledged?
  • Read hands and feet: are they working?
  • Read the painter: where does he stand, and does he speak for the figures?
