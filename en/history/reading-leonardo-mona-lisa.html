《蒙娜丽莎》Mona Lisa

微笑为什么没有边界？Why does the smile have no boundary?
约1503–1519 · 列奥纳多·达·芬奇ca.1503–1519 · Leonardo da Vinci精读Close reading
绘画史Painting History / 精读Close readings
一件作品，一个问题；先看，再判断。One work, one question; look first, judge later.

图片为该作品在本站的主图；元数据见下方作品卡。The image is the primary image for this work; metadata follows in the work card.

问题Question

达·芬奇用晕涂让轮廓消失，光、空气和皮肤互相渗透。微笑没有一条明确的线，它随观看角度变化；因此她既在看我们，又不在看我们。

Leonardo uses sfumato to dissolve contour; light, air and skin permeate one another. The smile has no clear line and changes with the angle of looking; she is both looking at us and not.

语境Context

约1503–1519年木板油画，藏于卢浮宫。人物通常被认为是佛罗伦萨商人弗朗切斯科·德尔·乔孔多的妻子丽莎。

Oil on panel, c. 1503–1519, held by the Louvre. The sitter is usually identified as Lisa, wife of the Florentine merchant Francesco del Giocondo.

怎么看How to look

  • 先看嘴角：它真的在笑吗？
  • 再看背景：风景为什么退得那么远？
  • 最后看手：它们为什么交叠？
  • Read the mouth: is it really smiling?
  • Then read the background: why does landscape recede so far?
  • Finally read the hands: why crossed?

为什么重要Why it matters

它把“不确定”变成肖像的核心，而不是缺陷。

It makes uncertainty the core of the portrait, not a defect.
