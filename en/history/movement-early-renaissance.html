早期文艺复兴Early Renaissance

早期文艺复兴不是某一天突然开始的。它更像一场缓慢的谈判：拜占庭的圣像传统、哥特式的线条、古典的比例、城市共和国的赞助人，在同一个画面里争夺位置。杜乔让圣像开始呼吸，乔托让哀悼有了重量，马萨乔把人体放进有空气的空间，波提切利则让神话和诗意重新回到佛罗伦萨。The Early Renaissance did not begin on a single day. It was a slow negotiation between Byzantine icon, Gothic line, classical proportion and the patronage of city republics, all competing inside the same picture. Duccio lets an icon breathe, Giotto gives grief weight, Masaccio puts the body into air-filled space, and Botticelli brings myth and poetry back to Florence.

绘画史Painting History / 流派Movements
先看三个特征，再看三件作品；最后把它放回它所在的章节。Start with three traits, then three works; finally put it back into its chapter.

三个特征Three traits

  • 空间开始有空气：透视还不是精确的科学，但人物已经站在可测量的地面上。
  • 身体开始有重量：衣褶挂在肩膀上，脚趾抓住地面，影子从同一侧落下。
  • 赞助人进入画面：美第奇、行会、修道院决定题材，也决定画家能走多远。
  • Space begins to breathe: perspective is not yet exact science, but figures stand on measurable ground.
  • The body gains weight: drapery hangs from shoulders, toes grip the floor, shadows fall from one side.
  • Patrons enter the picture: Medici, guilds and monasteries decide subjects and how far a painter can go.

怎么看How to look

  • 先看人物的脚：它们是否真的踩在地上？
  • 再看衣褶：它是身体的延伸，还是独立的装饰？
  • 最后看背景：建筑、山、海是否在同一个空间里？
  • Start with feet: do they really stand on the ground?
  • Then read drapery: is it an extension of the body or independent decoration?
  • Finally read the background: do architecture, mountain and sea share one space?
