乔托Giotto di Bondone

乔托被看作“文艺复兴的第一人”。在他之前，拜占庭圣像的人物像漂浮的符号；乔托让他们站到地上，有重量、有表情、有彼此之间的关系。他不是靠解剖学，而是靠观察：母亲抱孩子时肩膀怎样倾斜，哭的人怎样扭过脸。Giotto is often called the first painter of the Renaissance. Before him, Byzantine icons floated as symbols; Giotto puts figures on the ground with weight, expression and relationships. He does not rely on anatomy but observation: how a mother's shoulder tilts when she holds a child, how a mourner turns her face away.

小传Biography

《哀悼基督》把所有人都压进一个浅舞台，岩石斜切画面，把视线引向基督的脸。圣母没有大声哭，她只是低下头，把脸贴近儿子。乔托的克制比夸张更有力量：悲伤不是表演，而是身体的重力。

Lamentation presses everyone into a shallow stage; the diagonal rock directs the eye to Christ's face. The Virgin does not wail; she lowers her head and brings her face close to her son. Giotto's restraint is stronger than exaggeration: grief is not performance but the weight of the body.

看圣母的脸：她为什么不哭出声？看岩石：它把视线推向哪里？Read the Virgin's face: why is she silent? Read the rock: where does it push the eye?
