立体主义Cubism

立体主义把对象拆成多个视点，再在同一张画上重新拼起来。它不再相信单点透视，也不再把颜色当作窗外的光；它把桌子、杯子、脸、报纸和字母变成平面上的结构。格里斯把这种结构画得最清楚：颜色像纸片，形体像建筑。Cubism breaks an object into multiple viewpoints and reassembles them on one canvas. It no longer trusts single-point perspective or colour as outdoor light; table, cup, face, newspaper and letter become structure on a plane. Gris makes that structure clearest: colour like paper, form like architecture.

三个特征Three traits

  • 多视点：一个对象可以同时被从正面、侧面、上面看见。
  • 平面化：深度被压成层，颜色和形状在前景与背景之间移动。
  • 字母与报纸：日常印刷品进入画面，艺术与大众媒体开始混在一起。
  • Multiple viewpoints: an object can be seen from front, side and above at once.
  • Flattening: depth is pressed into layers, colour and shape move between foreground and background.
  • Letters and newspapers: everyday print enters the picture, art and mass media begin to mix.

怎么看How to look

  • 先找同一个对象的不同面：它们如何被拼在一起？
  • 再看字母：它是签名、报纸，还是纯粹的形？
  • 最后看颜色：它是光，还是纸片？
  • Find different faces of the same object: how are they joined?
  • Then read letters: signature, newspaper, or pure shape?
  • Finally read colour: light, or paper?
