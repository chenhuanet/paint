战争War

战争画很少真的在画战争，它更多在画战争之后：尸体、幸存者、审判、纪念。这条小径从枪口走到纪念碑。War painting rarely paints war itself; it paints what comes after: bodies, survivors, judgment, memorial. This trail moves from rifle muzzles to monuments.

绘画史Painting History / 小径Trails
按停靠顺序走；每一站只留一条线索，最后回到收束句。Follow the stops in order; each stop leaves one thread, and the closing sentence ties them together.

沿途Along the trail

1814 · 弗朗西斯科·戈雅1814 · Francisco Goya
《1808年5月3日》The Third of May 1808

枪口贴着脸，白衬衫是唯一的灯。Rifles press against faces; a white shirt is the only lamp.

1793 · 雅克-路易·大卫1793 · Jacques-Louis David
《马拉之死》The Death of Marat

政治谋杀被整理成一张干净的圣像。Political murder arranged into a clean icon.

1830 · 欧仁·德拉克罗瓦1830 · Eugène Delacroix
《自由引导人民》Liberty Leading the People

自由举旗跨过街垒，战争被画成一场向前推进的群众运动。Liberty raises the flag over the barricade; war becomes a mass movement pushing forward.

1819–1823 · 弗朗西斯科·戈雅1819–1823 · Francisco Goya
《农神吞噬其子》Saturn Devouring His Son

权力被画成一个正在吃孩子的巨神。Power painted as a god eating his child.

走完之后After the walk

战争画最难的，不是画暴力，而是拒绝让暴力变得好看。The hardest thing in war painting is not to depict violence but to refuse to make it beautiful.
