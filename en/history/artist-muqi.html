牧溪Fachang Muqi

牧溪是南宋末年的禅僧，法名法常，活动于杭州一带。他的画在中國文人系统里评价不高，却被日本室町时代视为水墨典范；这提醒我们，“重要性”从来不是单中心的历史。Muqi was a Chan monk at the end of the Southern Song, ordained as Fachang and active around Hangzhou. His painting was little valued in the Chinese literati system but became a model of ink painting in Muromachi Japan — a reminder that 'importance' is never a single-centre history.

小传Biography

《柳树与喜鹊》《燕子与莲花》用极少的墨、极快的笔，把鸟、枝、花、风留在纸上；《龙虎》则把一对猛兽画成两团会呼吸的墨。牧溪不追求形似，他追求的是对象在瞬间留下的气场。

Willow and Magpie and Swallow and Lotus use very little ink and very fast brushwork to keep bird, branch, flower and wind on paper; Dragon and Tiger turns a pair of predators into two breathing ink masses. Muqi does not seek likeness but the aura an object leaves in an instant.

看墨的浓淡：哪一笔是鸟，哪一笔只是风？Read the ink: which stroke is the bird, and which is only wind?
