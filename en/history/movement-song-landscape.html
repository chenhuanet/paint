两宋山水Song Landscape

两宋山水不是一种风格，而是一套关于“人在自然里站在哪里”的回答。北宋把主峰放在中央，让山成为秩序的中心；南宋把山推到边角，让空白承担距离。范宽、巨然、马远、牧溪各自给出了不同的尺度：山可以压住人，也可以被人从一角观看。Song landscape is not one style but a set of answers to where humans stand in nature. Northern Song places the main peak at the centre and makes the mountain the centre of order; Southern Song pushes it to a corner and lets blank space carry distance. Fan Kuan, Juran, Ma Yuan and Muqi give different scales: the mountain can weigh on a person, or be watched from a corner.

三个特征Three traits

  • 中心与边角：北宋的主峰是秩序，南宋的边角是选择。
  • 皴法即地质：雨点皴、披麻皴、斧劈皴不是笔触装饰，而是石头的结构。
  • 空白参与叙事：雾、水、天、距离都由未画之处承担。
  • Centre and corner: the Northern Song peak is order; the Southern Song corner is choice.
  • Texture strokes are geology: raindrop, hemp-fibre and axe-cut strokes are stone structure, not decoration.
  • Blank space narrates: mist, water, sky and distance are carried by what is not painted.

怎么看How to look

  • 先看主峰的位置：它在中央、边角，还是被切掉？
  • 再看皴法：山是土坡、石壁，还是被雨水冲出的沟？
  • 最后看人：人是行旅、渔夫、观瀑者，还是几乎看不见？
  • Start with the main peak: centre, corner, or cut off?
  • Then read texture strokes: earthen slope, stone wall, or rain-cut gully?
  • Finally read the human: traveller, fisherman, waterfall watcher, or almost invisible?
