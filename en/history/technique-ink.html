水墨Ink painting

水墨用毛笔、墨、水和纸绢。墨分五色：焦、浓、重、淡、清；水让同一笔墨在纸上产生无穷变化。水墨不追求覆盖，而追求“留”和“让”：哪里下笔，哪里空白，都是判断。Ink painting uses brush, ink, water and paper or silk. Ink has five tones: burnt, dense, heavy, light, clear; water makes a single stroke infinitely variable. Ink does not seek coverage but 'leaving' and 'yielding': where to put the brush and where to leave blank are both judgments.

绘画史Painting History / 技法Techniques
先看三步做法，再看作品；技法只有在具体的画里才成立。Start with the three steps, then the works; a technique only exists inside a specific picture.

怎么做How it is done

  • 磨墨、调水，控制浓淡。
  • 用中锋、侧锋、干笔、湿笔制造质感。
  • 留白：不画的地方也是画。
  • Grind ink and mix water to control tone.
  • Use centered, side, dry and wet brush for texture.
  • Reserve blank: the unpainted area is also painting.
