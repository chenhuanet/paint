俵屋宗达Tawaraya Sōtatsu

俵屋宗达是江户初期的画家与画师，被视为琳派（Rinpa）的源头之一。他把金银箔、矿物颜料和“垂水”（tarashikomi）技法用在一起，让装饰性不再是表面的华丽，而是一种会流动的材料。Tawaraya Sōtatsu was an early Edo painter and picture-maker, one of the sources of the Rinpa tradition. He combined gold and silver foil, mineral pigments and 'tarashikomi' (dripping ink) so that decoration became a flowing material rather than surface glitter.

小传Biography

他为《新敕撰和歌集》所作的和歌卡，把海滨松树的图案压成极简的线面；另一幅《小山站的铁路道口》则把人物与风景切成装饰性的色块。宗达的页面常被书法、纸张和图案共同占据，图像不是独立的，它和文字一起呼吸。

His poem-card for the Shinkokin wakashu reduces the design of pines on a beach to a few lines and planes; the other leaf turns figures and landscape into decorative colour blocks. Sōtatsu's pages are shared by calligraphy, paper and pattern: the image is not independent but breathes with the text.
