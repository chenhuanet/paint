宋代风俗画Song Genre Painting

宋代风俗画把普通人、儿童、商贩、船夫和节庆放进画面，不是作为背景，而是作为主角。它和宫廷、文人、宗教画共享同一套笔墨，却把注意力放在一天里发生了什么：谁在桥上，谁在卖东西，谁在追一只跑掉的猫。Song genre painting puts ordinary people, children, pedlars, boatmen and festivals into the picture, not as background but as subject. It shares brush language with court, literati and religious painting, yet attends to what happens in a day: who is on the bridge, who is selling, who is chasing a runaway cat.

先看三个特征，再看三件作品；最后把它放回它所在的章节。Start with three traits, then three works; finally put it back into its chapter.

三个特征Three traits

  • 城市作为机器：桥、船、店铺、城门被组织成可运行的交通系统。
  • 人物作为社会关系：谁看谁、谁让路、谁被挤到边缘，比脸相更重要。
  • 细节作为证据：招牌、货物、衣帽、牲畜都在替时代作证。
  • The city as machine: bridges, boats, shops and gates form a working transport system.
  • Figures as social relations: who looks, who yields, who is pushed aside matters more than likeness.
  • Detail as evidence: signs, goods, clothes, animals testify to the period.

怎么看How to look

  • 先找交通：人、船、车、驴如何互相避让？
  • 再看交易：谁在卖，谁在买，谁只是看？
  • 最后看边缘：画面最边上的人是谁？
  • Start with traffic: how do people, boats, carts and donkeys avoid one another?
  • Then read exchange: who sells, who buys, who only watches?
  • Finally read the edge: who is at the very edge of the picture?
