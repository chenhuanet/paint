象征主义Symbolism

象征主义不相信画应该解释世界。它相信画应该像梦一样，给出气氛、颜色和暗示，而不是答案。勃克林画死之岛，莫罗画朱庇特与塞墨勒，蒙克画一声穿过自然的尖叫，克里姆特把身体包进金色图案。它把神话、欲望、恐惧和死亡重新请回画面。Symbolism does not believe painting should explain the world. It believes painting should work like a dream, offering atmosphere, colour and suggestion rather than answers. Böcklin paints the Isle of the Dead, Moreau paints Jupiter and Semele, Munch paints a scream passing through nature, Klimt wraps the body in gold pattern. Myth, desire, fear and death return to the picture.

绘画史Painting History / 流派Movements
先看三个特征，再看三件作品；最后把它放回它所在的章节。Start with three traits, then three works; finally put it back into its chapter.

三个特征Three traits

  • 气氛优先：颜色、雾、光、寂静，比故事更重要。
  • 神话作为心理：神不是信仰对象，而是欲望和恐惧的化身。
  • 装饰与危险：金箔、花纹、藤蔓，美得让人不安。
  • Atmosphere first: colour, mist, light and silence matter more than story.
  • Myth as psychology: gods are not objects of faith but embodiments of desire and fear.
  • Decoration and danger: gold, pattern, vines — beautiful and unsettling.

怎么看How to look

  • 看气氛：颜色让你感到什么？
  • 看身体：它是完整的，还是正在变形、消失？
  • 看细节：哪些符号重复出现？它们在暗示什么？
  • Read atmosphere: what does colour make you feel?
  • Read the body: intact, transforming or disappearing?
  • Read detail: which symbols repeat, and what do they suggest?
