提香Titian (Tiziano Vecellio)

提香是威尼斯画派的中心人物，也是16世纪欧洲最抢手的肖像画家。他不相信佛罗伦萨的“素描优先”，他相信颜色：皮肤、丝绸和盔甲都由半透明的色层叠出来，边缘融进阴影，人物因此有温度和重量。Titian is the central figure of the Venetian school and the most sought-after portraitist in sixteenth-century Europe. He did not believe in Florentine 'drawing first'; he believed in colour: skin, silk and armour are built from translucent layers, edges dissolve into shadow, and figures gain temperature and weight.

小传Biography

他画宗教题材像在搭剧场：光从云层落下，人物在暗处等待；他也画肖像，让赞助人的权力、疲倦和欲望都留在颜色里。

He staged religious subjects like theatre: light falls from clouds, figures wait in shadow; he also painted portraits, leaving patrons' power, fatigue and appetite in colour.

看颜色：它是在描述物体，还是在制造温度？Read colour: does it describe an object, or create warmth?
