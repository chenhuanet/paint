《哀悼基督》Lamentation

悲伤为什么不需要大声？Why does grief not need to be loud?
约1305 · 乔托ca.1305 · Giotto di Bondone精读Close reading
绘画史Painting History / 精读Close readings
一件作品，一个问题；先看，再判断。One work, one question; look first, judge later.

图片为该作品在本站的主图；元数据见下方作品卡。The image is the primary image for this work; metadata follows in the work card.

问题Question

乔托把所有人压进一个浅舞台，岩石斜切画面，把视线引向基督的脸。圣母没有大声哭，她只是低下头，把脸贴近儿子。

Giotto presses everyone into a shallow stage; the diagonal rock directs the eye to Christ's face. The Virgin does not wail; she lowers her head and brings her face close to her son.

语境Context

帕多瓦阿雷纳礼拜堂湿壁画，约1305年。乔托被看作文艺复兴绘画的起点。

Fresco in the Arena Chapel, Padua, c. 1305. Giotto is often seen as the starting point of Renaissance painting.

怎么看How to look

  • 先看圣母的脸：她为什么不哭出声？
  • 再看岩石：它把视线推向哪里？
  • 最后看背对我们的人：她为什么转身？
  • Read the Virgin's face: why silent?
  • Then read the rock: where does it push the eye?
  • Finally read the figure with her back to us: why turn away?

为什么重要Why it matters

它让宗教故事第一次有了身体的重力。

It gives the religious story bodily gravity for the first time.
