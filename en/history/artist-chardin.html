让-巴蒂斯特-西梅翁·夏尔丹Jean-Baptiste-Siméon Chardin

夏尔丹是十八世纪最会画“物”的画家。他画厨房、餐桌、铜锅、面包、鱼、白布和猫，不画神话，也不画宫廷。他把静物画抬到可以被认真观看的高度，让日常拥有尊严。Chardin is the great painter of things in the eighteenth century. He paints kitchens, tables, copper pots, bread, fish, white cloth and cats; no myth, no court. He raises still life to the level of serious looking and gives the everyday dignity.

小传Biography

《鳐鱼》里，剖开的鳐鱼倒挂在铁钩上，银白腹部翻出，血色内脏垂落；猫踩着牡蛎壳，刀和酒瓶在右侧。夏尔丹用厚涂高光表现鱼皮的湿润，用暗褐背景把物体推到前景。它没有寓意，只有真实的气味。

In The Ray, a gutted fish hangs from a hook, silver belly opened, red entrails spilling; a cat steps on oyster shells, a knife and bottle at right. Chardin uses thick highlights for wet skin and a dark brown ground to push objects forward. There is no allegory, only the smell of reality.

看表面：鱼皮是湿的吗？看猫：它在偷什么？Read the surface: is the fish skin wet? Read the cat: what is it stealing?
