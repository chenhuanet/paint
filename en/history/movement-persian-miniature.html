波斯细密画Persian Miniature

波斯细密画把诗、历史、爱情和宫廷礼仪放进一个高度装饰化的平面。它不追求单点透视，而用颜色、金箔、花园和建筑把故事分层；人物可以在同一张纸上同时出现在不同的时间。Persian miniature puts poetry, history, love and court etiquette onto a highly decorated plane. It does not seek single-point perspective; colour, gold, garden and architecture stratify the story, and figures can appear at different times on the same page.

绘画史Painting History / 流派Movements
先看三个特征，再看三件作品；最后把它放回它所在的章节。Start with three traits, then three works; finally put it back into its chapter.

三个特征Three traits

  • 时间可以折叠：同一人物在同一页上出现两次，表示不同时刻。
  • 颜色有等级：金、蓝、红的位置决定谁被看见。
  • 诗画互文：图像不是文字的插图，而是文字的另一半。
  • Time can fold: the same figure appears twice on one page to mark different moments.
  • Colour has hierarchy: where gold, blue and red sit determines who is seen.
  • Poem and image interlace: the picture is not an illustration but the other half of the text.

怎么看How to look

  • 先找重复的人物：他/她为什么出现两次？
  • 再看颜色等级：金色给了谁？
  • 最后看边框：故事在哪里被切断？
  • Find the repeated figure: why appear twice?
  • Read colour hierarchy: who gets gold?
  • Finally read the frame: where is the story cut?
