石版画Lithography

石版画用油水相斥的原理：在石版上画油性图像，用水润湿空白处，再上油墨印刷。它比铜版更自由，能保留笔触和纸面的质感；19世纪的报刊、海报和艺术版画都靠它把图像送进城市。Lithography works on the repulsion of oil and water: draw an oily image on stone, wet the blank areas, then ink and print. It is freer than copperplate and preserves brushwork and paper texture; nineteenth-century newspapers, posters and art prints used it to put images into the city.

怎么做How it is done

  • 在石版上用油性笔或蜡笔画图。
  • 用水润湿版面，空白处不接受油墨。
  • 上墨印刷；笔触和纸纹被保留下来。
  • Draw on the stone with an oily crayon or ink.
  • Wet the surface so blank areas reject ink.
  • Ink and print; brushwork and paper grain survive.
