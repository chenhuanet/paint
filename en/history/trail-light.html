光Light

画家不画光本身，他们画光落在什么上面。这条小径从烛光、圣光走到日光、火光和电光，看光如何从神的属性变成画家的工具。Painters do not paint light itself; they paint what light falls on. This trail moves from candle, holy light and sunlight to fire and electric light, tracing how light becomes a painter's instrument.

沿途Along the trail

1434 · 扬·凡·艾克1434 · Jan van Eyck
《阿尔诺芬尼夫妇像》The Arnolfini Portrait

窗边的自然光与镜中的反射，让房间像被擦亮的玻璃。Window light and mirror reflection make the room like polished glass.

1599–1600 · 卡拉瓦乔1599–1600 · Caravaggio
《圣马太蒙召》The Calling of Saint Matthew

一束斜光从暗处切进来，照亮税吏，也照亮召唤。A shaft of light cuts in from darkness, illuminating the tax collector and the call.

1642 · 伦勃朗1642 · Rembrandt van Rijn
《夜巡》The Night Watch

光不是平均的，它让一群人有了各自的性格。Light is not even; it gives each figure a separate character.

1839 · 约瑟夫·马洛德·威廉·特纳1839 · J. M. W. Turner
《被拖去解体的无畏号》The Fighting Temeraire

夕阳把旧战舰烧成一个剪影，光成了时代的隐喻。Sunset burns the old warship into a silhouette; light becomes a metaphor of an era.

1872 · 克劳德·莫奈1872 · Claude Monet
《印象·日出》Impression, Sunrise

太阳只是一枚橙点，水面用冷暖关系把它托起来。The sun is an orange dot; warm-cold water relations lift it into view.

走完之后After the walk

下次看画，先问：光从哪里来？它为什么选择照这个人？Next time, ask first: where does light come from, and why does it choose this person?
