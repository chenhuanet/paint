扬·凡·艾克Jan van Eyck

扬·凡·艾克不是油画的“发明者”，但他是最早把油彩用到极致的人。他用层层透明罩染，让金属、毛皮、木头和皮肤拥有不同的反光；他画的世界像被擦亮的玻璃，每一个小物件都在说话。Jan van Eyck did not invent oil painting, but he brought it to an extreme. Layers of transparent glaze give metal, fur, wood and skin different reflections; his world is like polished glass, and every small object speaks.

小传Biography

《阿尔诺芬尼夫妇像》里，镜子反射出房间和两个看不见的见证人，狗代表忠诚，蜡烛代表神意，脱下的鞋代表圣地。凡·艾克把一场婚礼画成一份合同：画面不只是像，它还是证据。

In the Arnolfini Portrait, the mirror reflects the room and two invisible witnesses; the dog stands for fidelity, the candle for divine presence, the discarded shoes for holy ground. Van Eyck turns a wedding into a contract: the picture is not only likeness but evidence.

找镜子：它在反射谁？找鞋：它们为什么被脱掉？Find the mirror: whom does it reflect? Find the shoes: why are they removed?
