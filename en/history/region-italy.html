意大利Italy

从乔托的帕多瓦到卡拉瓦乔的罗马，意大利把绘画变成身体、透视和光的实验室。本区域页按“城市—赞助—作品”组织，不把意大利等同于文艺复兴。From Giotto's Padua to Caravaggio's Rome, Italy turned painting into a laboratory of body, perspective and light. This hub is organised by city, patronage and work; it does not reduce Italy to the Renaissance.

三个特征Three traits

  • 城市竞争：佛罗伦萨、威尼斯、罗马各自发明自己的画法。
  • 赞助网络：教会、行会、宫廷决定尺度和题材。
  • 素描与颜色：佛罗伦萨重轮廓，威尼斯重色层。
  • City rivalry: Florence, Venice and Rome each invent their own painting.
  • Patronage: church, guild and court determine scale and subject.
  • Drawing and colour: Florence favours contour, Venice favours colour layers.
