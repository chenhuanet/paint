Caravaggio

1571–1610

Biography

  • Caravaggio is the most dangerous painter of the Baroque. He paints saints as poor people from the street and light as a knife cut out of darkness. His pictures have no elegant distance: blood sprays onto pillows, feet are dirty, tax collectors count money.
  • In *The Calling of Saint Matthew*, Christ's hand enters from the dark; a shaft of light falls on the tax table. Matthew points at himself, as if asking 'me?' Light is not natural but a choice: the illuminated are called, those left in shadow keep counting.

Note

Find the light: why does it illuminate one person? Read the hands: who calls, who answers?
