伊比利亚Iberia

西班牙把宫廷、宗教和暴力画在同一张画布上。委拉斯开兹的镜子、戈雅的黑画和《1808年5月3日》、格列柯的瘦长身体，都在处理权力如何看见和被看见。Spain paints court, religion and violence on the same canvas. Velázquez's mirror, Goya's Black Paintings and *Third of May 1808*, El Greco's elongated bodies all deal with how power sees and is seen.

三个特征Three traits

  • 宫廷与观看：镜子、门和画布把观者卷进权力链。
  • 宗教身体：瘦长、扭曲、发光的身体表达信仰紧张。
  • 暴力历史：处决、战争和怪物化成为历史画的新语言。
  • Court and looking: mirror, door and canvas draw the viewer into power.
  • Religious body: elongated, distorted, luminous bodies express devotional tension.
  • Violent history: execution, war and monstering become a new language of history painting.
