History · Movements

Art Nouveau

1890–1910

Note

Klimt and others turn the body into a decorative surface of gold, curves and pattern.

Summary

Art Nouveau brought curve, plant, female body and decorative plane into painting, posters, architecture and furniture. It opposed academic stiffness and industrial ugliness, wanting modern life to look like a flowing line. Klimt's *The Kiss* turns that curve into two people on gold.

Traits

  • Line is life: plants, hair, drapery and architectural edges share one curve.
  • Decoration is content: gold, pattern and flat plane are the surface of feeling, not background.
  • Total art: painting, poster, furniture and architecture share one visual language.

Look

  • Start with line: where does it begin, where does it entangle the body?
  • Then read gold: does it warm the picture, or make it colder?
  • Finally read the bodies: one shape, or two?
