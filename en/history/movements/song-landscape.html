History · Movements

Northern Song landscape

960–1279

Note

Centered on Fan Kuan's *Travelers among Mountains and Streams*: the main peak dominates, travelers are tiny.

Summary

Song landscape is not one style but a set of answers to where humans stand in nature. Northern Song places the main peak at the centre and makes the mountain the centre of order; Southern Song pushes it to a corner and lets blank space carry distance. Fan Kuan, Juran, Ma Yuan and Muqi give different scales: the mountain can weigh on a person, or be watched from a corner.

Traits

  • Centre and corner: the Northern Song peak is order; the Southern Song corner is choice.
  • Texture strokes are geology: raindrop, hemp-fibre and axe-cut strokes are stone structure, not decoration.
  • Blank space narrates: mist, water, sky and distance are carried by what is not painted.

Look

  • Start with the main peak: centre, corner, or cut off?
  • Then read texture strokes: earthen slope, stone wall, or rain-cut gully?
  • Finally read the human: traveller, fisherman, waterfall watcher, or almost invisible?
