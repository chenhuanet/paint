文字、长卷与观看Text, Scroll, and Looking

在长卷与册页里，文字不是说明，而是画面的一部分。阅读的顺序、留白的节奏、印章与边框，共同决定你什么时候停、在哪里看。In handscrolls and albums, text is not a caption but part of the picture. The order of reading, the rhythm of blank space, seals and borders decide when you stop and where you look.
