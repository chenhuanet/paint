PERIOD / 1930–1945PERIOD / 1930–1945

现代主义的政治化Modernism politicised

这十五年里，现代主义不再是画廊里的形式实验。经济大萧条、法西斯主义、战争和流亡把画家推回历史与身体：徐悲鸿用写实油画重述中国历史，伍德把美国乡村画成一张国家面孔，蒙德里安仍相信格子可以给混乱世界一种秩序。本馆藏这一时段只有三件，属于收藏缺口，不用其他时期作品填充。In these fifteen years modernism was no longer only a gallery experiment. Depression, fascism, war and exile pushed painters back toward history and body: Xu Beihong retold Chinese history in realist oil, Wood turned an American farm into a national face, Mondrian still believed the grid could give a chaotic world order. The archive holds only three works in this band; this is an acquisition gap, not filled with works from other periods.

三个关键词Three keywords

  • 历史画回潮：战争与国家危机让“大画”重新出现。
  • 国家面孔：乡村、农民、英雄被做成可识别的纪念碑。
  • 抽象仍继续：格子、原色、平面在政治危机旁保持自己的秩序。
  • History painting returns: war and national crisis bring the 'big picture' back.
  • National face: countryside, farmers and heroes become recognisable monuments.
  • Abstraction continues: grid, primary colour and plane keep their order beside political crisis.
