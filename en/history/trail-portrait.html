肖像Portrait

肖像不是脸的照片，它是一份关于“这个人为什么值得被记住”的提案。这条小径从皇帝、商人、画家自己走到一个回头的少女。A portrait is not a photograph of a face; it is a proposal about why this person deserves to be remembered. This trail moves from emperor and merchant to the painter himself and a girl turning her head.

按停靠顺序走；每一站只留一条线索，最后回到收束句。Follow the stops in order; each stop leaves one thread, and the closing sentence ties them together.

沿途Along the trail

1500 · 阿尔布雷希特·丢勒1500 · Albrecht Dürer
《自画像（二十八岁）》Self-Portrait at the Age of 28

画家把自己画成正面、直视、穿皮草的绅士。The painter paints himself frontal, direct, in fur.

1656 · 迭戈·委拉斯开兹1656 · Diego Velázquez
《宫娥》Las Meninas

画家把自己画进宫廷，却让镜子拆穿权力。The painter enters the court, while the mirror exposes power.

约1665–1669 · 伦勃朗ca.1665–1669 · Rembrandt van Rijn
《双圆自画像》Self-Portrait with Two Circles

晚年自画像不表演，只把一张疲惫的脸放在画布上。The late self-portrait does not perform; it places a tired face on canvas.

约1665 · 约翰内斯·维米尔ca.1665 · Johannes Vermeer
《戴珍珠耳环的少女》Girl with a Pearl Earring

不是肖像，是一个正在回头的瞬间。Not a portrait but an instant of turning.

1912 · 埃贡·席勒1912 · Egon Schiele
《与中国灯笼花自画像》Self-Portrait with Chinese Lantern Plant

身体像一根绷紧的神经，自画像变成情绪诊断。The body like a tightened nerve; the self-portrait becomes an emotional diagnosis.

走完之后After the walk

好肖像让你记住一个人，伟大的肖像让你记住“人”本身。A good portrait makes you remember a person; a great portrait makes you remember 'person' itself.
