《庐山高图》Lofty Mount Lu

一幅祝寿画为什么要画成一座山？Why paint a birthday picture as a mountain?
1467 · 沈周1467 · Shen Zhou精读Close reading
绘画史Painting History / 精读Close readings
一件作品，一个问题；先看，再判断。One work, one question; look first, judge later.

图片为该作品在本站的主图；元数据见下方作品卡。The image is the primary image for this work; metadata follows in the work card.

问题Question

沈周把师承、祝寿和山水放进同一幅立轴：庐山高而密，笔墨却松。山既是自然，也是一个人的德行和寿命。

Shen Zhou joins lineage, birthday dedication and landscape in one vertical scroll: Mount Lu is high and dense, yet the brush remains loose. The mountain is both nature and a person's virtue and longevity.

语境Context

明代吴门画派纸本设色立轴，藏于台北故宫博物院。沈周是苏州文人画的核心，学生与友人称他为“石田先生”。

A Ming Wu school hanging scroll in ink and colour on paper, held by the National Palace Museum, Taipei. Shen Zhou was the centre of Suzhou literati painting; students and friends called him 'Master Shitian'.

怎么看How to look

  • 先看山脚：人从哪里进入？
  • 再看山顶：它像石头还是云？
  • 最后看题跋：谁被祝寿？
  • Start at the foot: where does one enter?
  • Then read the summit: stone or cloud?
  • Finally read the inscription: who is being honoured?

为什么重要Why it matters

它把“关系”画进山水：一幅画同时是礼物、师承和自画像。

It paints relationship into landscape: a picture is at once gift, lineage and self-portrait.
