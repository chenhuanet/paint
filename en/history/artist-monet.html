克劳德·莫奈Claude Monet

莫奈是印象主义最坚定的画家。他画同一座教堂、同一片干草堆、同一座桥在不同时刻的颜色，像科学家一样记录光的变化。他晚年画睡莲，眼睛几乎失明，颜色却越来越自由。Monet is the most committed Impressionist. He paints the same cathedral, haystacks and bridge at different hours, recording changes of light like a scientist. In late life he painted water lilies, almost blind, and colour became freer than ever.

小传Biography

《印象·日出》画勒阿弗尔港的早晨：太阳是一枚橙红的点，水面是灰蓝的横线，工厂的烟和船影混在一起。莫奈没有画轮廓，他用颜色的冷暖关系让物体出现。评论家的嘲笑，最后变成了流派的名字。

Impression, Sunrise shows Le Havre harbour in the morning: the sun an orange-red dot, water grey-blue horizontals, factory smoke and masts merged. Monet does not draw contours; he makes objects appear through warm and cool colour. A critic's mockery became the name of a movement.

看同一主题的不同时刻：颜色变了，物体还在吗？Compare the same subject at different hours: colour changes — does the object remain?
