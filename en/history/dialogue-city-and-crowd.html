城市与人群：张择端与勃鲁盖尔City and Crowd: Zhang Zeduan and Bruegel

一个画汴京的桥与船，一个画巴别塔的工地。两幅画都把“人群”当作主角，但一个在记录城市如何运转，一个在记录人类如何失败。One paints the bridge and boats of Bianjing, the other the building site of Babel. Both make the crowd the protagonist, but one records how a city works, the other how humanity fails.

不判断谁更好；只把两张画放在一起，看它们各自回答了什么问题。No verdict on who is better; put two pictures together and see what each answers.

人群是合作，还是失控？Is the crowd cooperation or loss of control?

《清明上河图》Along the River During the Qingming Festival约1085–1145 · 张择端ca.1085–1145 · Zhang Zeduan
《巴别塔》The Tower of Babel1563 · 老彼得·勃鲁盖尔1563 · Pieter Bruegel the Elder

《清明上河图》的人群有分工：船夫、商人、轿夫、守卫，各在自己的位置上。巴别塔的工人也在分工，但塔顶永远完不成；合作本身变成了惩罚。

The crowd in Qingming has roles: boatman, merchant, bearer, guard, each in place. The workers at Babel also have roles, but the top can never be finished; cooperation itself becomes punishment.

收束Closing

城市画不是“人多”，而是看人群如何被组织、被观看、被失败。A city picture is not 'many people' but how a crowd is organised, watched and failed.
