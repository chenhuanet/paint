卡济米尔·马列维奇Kazimir Malevich

马列维奇是至上主义的创始人。他要从绘画里删掉一切“多余”的东西：对象、故事、透视、颜色。最后剩下一个黑方块，放在白底上，像关闭了旧世界，也像打开了一个新空间。Malevich founded Suprematism. He wanted to delete everything 'extra' from painting: object, story, perspective, colour. What remains is a black square on white, like a world switched off and a new space switched on.

小传Biography

《黑方块》不是“少画”，而是一次宣言：绘画可以只依靠形和空无。马列维奇把它挂在展厅的角落，像一幅圣像。观众被迫面对一个没有内容的画面，也就被迫面对自己的观看习惯。

Black Square is not 'painting less' but a manifesto: painting can rely only on form and emptiness. Malevich hung it in the corner of the exhibition like an icon. The viewer is forced to face a picture with no content, and therefore to face his own habits of looking.

看边：黑方块是完美的正方形吗？看角落：它为什么像圣像？Read the edge: is the black square perfect? Read the corner: why does it behave like an icon?
