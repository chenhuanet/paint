瓦西里·康定斯基Wassily Kandinsky

康定斯基是抽象艺术的先驱。他相信颜色和声音可以通感：黄色像喇叭，蓝色像大提琴，黑色像关闭的门。他把绘画从“画什么”推向“怎么组织看不见的力量”。Kandinsky is a pioneer of abstraction. He believed colour and sound could cross-sense: yellow like a trumpet, blue like a cello, black like a closing door. He pushed painting from 'what' to 'how to organise invisible forces'.

小传Biography

《构成第七号》没有可辨认的对象，却有强烈的事件感：黑色线条像爆炸，色块像碎片，画面中心有一个看不见的引力。康定斯基的抽象不是随意，而是作曲：每一块颜色都有位置和重量。

Composition VII has no recognisable object but a strong sense of event: black lines explode, colour planes fragment, an invisible gravity holds the center. Kandinsky's abstraction is not arbitrary; it is composition: every colour has position and weight.

听颜色：哪一种颜色像声音？看中心：什么在吸引画面？Hear colour: which one sounds? Read the center: what pulls the picture together?
