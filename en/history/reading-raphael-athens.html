《雅典学院》The School of Athens

一间厅堂如何装下整个知识界？How does one room hold the whole Republic of Letters?
1509–1511 · 拉斐尔1509–1511 · Raphael精读Close reading
绘画史Painting History / 精读Close readings
一件作品，一个问题；先看，再判断。One work, one question; look first, judge later.

图片为该作品在本站的主图；元数据见下方作品卡。The image is the primary image for this work; metadata follows in the work card.

问题Question

拉斐尔把柏拉图、亚里士多德、毕达哥拉斯、欧几里得和许多同代艺术家安排进同一间厅堂。中心是两位哲学家，手势指向天与地；画面因此成为一张知识地图。

Raphael places Plato, Aristotle, Pythagoras, Euclid and many contemporary artists in one room. At the centre two philosophers point to heaven and earth; the picture becomes a map of knowledge.

语境Context

梵蒂冈使徒宫签字厅湿壁画，1509–1511年。拉斐尔与米开朗基罗、达·芬奇并称盛期文艺复兴三杰。

Fresco in the Stanza della Segnatura, Apostolic Palace, Vatican, 1509–1511. Raphael is paired with Michelangelo and Leonardo as a High Renaissance master.

怎么看How to look

  • 先看中心：谁指天，谁指地？
  • 再看人群：谁在争论，谁在计算？
  • 最后看建筑：透视把视线引向哪里？
  • Read the centre: who points up, who down?
  • Then read the crowd: who argues, who calculates?
  • Finally read the architecture: where does perspective lead?

为什么重要Why it matters

它把“知识”画成一种可被安排的社会秩序。

It paints knowledge as a social order that can be arranged.
