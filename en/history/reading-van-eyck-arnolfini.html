《阿尔诺芬尼夫妇像》The Arnolfini Portrait

一幅婚礼肖像如何变成合同？How does a wedding portrait become a contract?
1434 · 扬·凡·艾克1434 · Jan van Eyck精读Close reading
绘画史Painting History / 精读Close readings
一件作品，一个问题；先看，再判断。One work, one question; look first, judge later.

图片为该作品在本站的主图；元数据见下方作品卡。The image is the primary image for this work; metadata follows in the work card.

问题Question

镜子反射出房间和两个看不见的见证人，狗代表忠诚，蜡烛代表神意，脱下的鞋代表圣地。凡·艾克把一场婚礼画成一份证据。

The mirror reflects the room and two invisible witnesses; the dog stands for fidelity, the candle for divine presence, the discarded shoes for holy ground. Van Eyck turns a wedding into evidence.

语境Context

1434年木板油画，藏于伦敦国家美术馆。画中人物通常被认为是意大利商人阿尔诺芬尼及其妻子。

Oil on panel, 1434, held by the National Gallery, London. The figures are usually identified as the Italian merchant Arnolfini and his wife.

怎么看How to look

  • 先找镜子：它在反射谁？
  • 再看鞋：它们为什么被脱掉？
  • 最后看手：他们在握手，还是在发誓？
  • Find the mirror: whom does it reflect?
  • Then read the shoes: why removed?
  • Finally read the hands: clasping, or swearing?

为什么重要Why it matters

它把物质细节变成法律与信仰的双重证据。

It turns material detail into evidence of both law and faith.
