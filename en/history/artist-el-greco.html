埃尔·格列柯El Greco

埃尔·格列柯生于克里特，先在威尼斯学画，后定居托莱多。他把拜占庭圣像的正面性、威尼斯色彩和西班牙宗教情感放在一起，人物被拉长，光像从云里劈下来。El Greco was born in Crete, trained in Venice and settled in Toledo. He combined the frontality of Byzantine icons, Venetian colour and Spanish religious intensity; figures are stretched and light seems to split the clouds.

小传Biography

《托莱多风景》不是地图，而是一座正在被风暴推挤的城市；《耶稣死在十字架上》把身体拉成一条向上的火焰。格列柯的比例不“正确”，但情绪是准确的：他画的是信仰中的重力。

View of Toledo is not a map but a city being pushed by a storm; Christ on the Cross stretches the body into an upward flame. El Greco's proportions are not 'correct', but the emotion is exact: he paints the gravity of faith.
