History · Trails

Body

Stops

1. `michelangelo-creation-of-adam-1511` — The body as architecture: muscle is arch, finger is contact. 2. `rubens-descent-from-cross-1614` — The body is heavy; moving it takes a whole team. 3. `courbet-painters-studio-1855` — On the left, bodies are labouring bodies, not mythical ones. 4. `munch-the-scream-1893` — The body is emptied by sound, reduced to a twisted contour.

Introduction

The body is painting's oldest subject and its hardest honesty. This trail moves from divine body to labouring, desiring and distorted body.

Closing

In painting the body is never only a body: it is evidence of an order, a desire or a fear.
