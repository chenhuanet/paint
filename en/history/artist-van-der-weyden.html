罗希尔·凡·德·魏登Rogier van der Weyden

魏登是15世纪布鲁塞尔的官方画家，作品以宗教祭坛画和肖像为主。他不像凡·艾克那样痴迷于物质的反光，而更关心身体的姿态和眼泪的方向：人物常常被压进一个很浅的空间，像舞台上的群像。Weyden was the official painter of fifteenth-century Brussels, known for religious altarpieces and portraits. Unlike Van Eyck's obsession with material reflection, he cares about posture and the direction of tears: figures are pressed into a shallow space like a stage group.

小传Biography

《下十字架》把基督的身体、圣母的昏厥和围观者的哭声组织成一条向下的曲线；每个人的手都在做不同的动作，却没有一个动作是多余的。魏登的宗教画因此不是图解，而是一场被安排得很精确的悲痛。

The Descent from the Cross organises Christ's body, the Virgin's swoon and the crowd's grief into a downward curve; every hand does something different, and no gesture is redundant. Weyden's religious painting is not diagram but grief arranged with precision.

看手：哪只手在接，哪只手在推？Read the hands: which one receives, which one pushes?
