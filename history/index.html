1800 年前的十个展览Ten exhibitions before 1800

不是按世纪切块，而是按问题重排：山水、信仰、另一条传统、文字、材料、日常、港口、死亡、女性与金和光。Not centuries in boxes, but questions rearranged: landscape, faith, another tradition, text, material, everyday life, ports, death, women, and gold and light.

山水与自我：中国绘画 4—18 世纪Landscape and the Self: Chinese Painting, 4th–18th Century

14 件14 works

从佛教石窟的残片到文人长卷，中国绘画把自然、身体与文字放在同一张绢或纸上。这里不按朝代排队，而是看山水如何成为一个人可以走进去的空间。From a Buddhist cave fragment to a scholar's handscroll, Chinese painting puts nature, body and text on the same silk or paper. This exhibition does not queue dynasties; it asks how landscape became a space a person could enter.

信仰与图像：欧洲 5—15 世纪Faith and Image: Europe, 5th–15th Century

10 件10 works

中世纪的图像不是窗，而是一套要被人读懂的句子。祭坛、手抄本与壁画把神圣故事拆成手势、器物和边框，让观看成为一次缓慢的辨认。The medieval image was not a window but a sentence to be read. Altarpieces, manuscripts and murals break the sacred story into gestures, objects and borders, making looking a slow act of recognition.

另一条传统：伊斯兰与南亚Another Tradition: Islamic and South Asian Painting

14 件14 works

细密画把文字、边框和人物放进同一页。莫卧儿的宫廷、波斯的诗集与耆那教的手抄本，让我们看到另一种“观看的组织方式”。Miniatures put text, border and figure on one page. Mughal courts, Persian poetry and Jain manuscripts show another way of organising looking.

文字、长卷与观看Text, Scroll, and Looking

14 件14 works

在长卷与册页里，文字不是说明，而是画面的一部分。阅读的顺序、留白的节奏、印章与边框，共同决定你什么时候停、在哪里看。In handscrolls and albums, text is not a caption but part of the picture. The order of reading, the rhythm of blank space, seals and borders decide when you stop and where you look.

材料与技法：金、墨、壁画Material and Method: Gold, Ink, Mural

14 件14 works

金箔反光，墨会渗进纸里，湿壁画必须趁灰泥未干画完。材料不是背景条件，它直接决定线条、颜色和光在画面上的走法。Gold leaf reflects, ink sinks into paper, fresco must be painted before the plaster dries. Material is not a background condition; it decides how line, colour and light travel across the surface.

早期世界的城市与日常City and Everyday Life in the Early World

14 件14 works

市场、宫廷、厨房与节庆，把“日常生活”变成可以观看的图像。人物在做什么、手里拿着什么，往往比故事本身更能说明一个时代。Markets, courts, kitchens and festivals turn everyday life into an image. What people do and what they hold often says more about a period than the story itself.

港口与市场：城市怎样被画成相遇Ports and Markets: Painting the City as Encounter

9 件9 works

从地中海港口到中国山市，从波斯细密画到江户版画，市场与码头把远方带进画面。交易的路线，也是图像传播的路线。From Mediterranean harbours to Chinese mountain markets, Persian miniatures to Edo prints, markets and wharves bring distance into the picture. Trade routes are also image routes.

死亡与记忆：葬礼、墓碑与纪念Death and Memory: Funeral, Grave and Memorial

11 件11 works

死亡不是终点，而是一组需要被安排、被观看、被重复的仪式。葬礼、墓碑与纪念图像，把失去变成可以共享的画面。Death is not an endpoint but a set of rites to be arranged, watched and repeated. Funerals, graves and memorial images turn loss into a shareable picture.

女性：可见与不可见Women: Visible and Invisible

11 件11 works

肖像、劳动与宗教中的女性，常被画在画面边缘，却常常是画面真正的原因。看她们手里拿什么、站在哪里、被谁观看。Women in portraits, labour and religion are often placed at the edge, and often are the real reason for the picture. Look at what they hold, where they stand, and who watches them.

金与光：材料怎样制造神圣Gold and Light: How Material Makes the Sacred

10 件10 works

金箔反光，烛光晃动，圣像的眼睛在暗处发亮。材料与光共同制造神圣感，也共同暴露观看的条件。Gold leaf reflects, candlelight flickers, icon eyes shine in the dark. Material and light together make sanctity, and expose the conditions of looking.

流派Movements

从早期文艺复兴到表现主义。From Early Renaissance to Expressionism.

早期文艺复兴Early Renaissance

5 件5 works

早期文艺复兴不是某一天突然开始的。它更像一场缓慢的谈判：拜占庭的圣像传统、哥特式的线条、古典的比例、城市共和国的赞助人，在同一个画面里争夺位置。杜乔让圣像开始呼吸，乔托让哀悼有了重量，马萨乔把人体放进有空气的空间，波提切利则让神话和诗意重新回到佛罗伦萨。The Early Renaissance did not begin on a single day. It was a slow negotiation between Byzantine icon, Gothic line, classical proportion and the patronage of city republics, all competing inside the same picture. Duccio lets an icon breathe, Giotto gives grief weight, Masaccio puts the body into air-filled space, and Botticelli brings myth and poetry back to Florence.

盛期文艺复兴High Renaissance

5 件5 works

盛期文艺复兴相信，画面可以同时做到三件事：空间可信、身体完美、意义清楚。达·芬奇用晕涂法让轮廓消失，米开朗基罗让身体变成建筑，拉斐尔把整个知识界安排进一间厅堂，提香则用颜色证明威尼斯不需要佛罗伦萨的素描。它只持续了大约三十年，却成了后来所有“古典”的参照。The High Renaissance believed a picture could do three things at once: make space credible, make the body perfect, make meaning clear. Leonardo dissolves contour with sfumato, Michelangelo turns the body into architecture, Raphael arranges the whole Republic of Letters in one room, and Titian proves with colour that Venice does not need Florentine drawing. It lasted only about thirty years, yet became the reference for everything later called 'classical'.

北方文艺复兴Northern Renaissance

6 件6 works

北方文艺复兴的画家不在意大利的阳光下工作，他们在低地国家的雨、石墙、羊毛和账本里工作。油彩让他们把世界画得像玻璃一样透明：镜子、窗、硬币、毛皮、远处的雪。宗教画因此没有被抛弃，而是被日常细节填满；每一个物件都可能是信仰的证据，也可能是道德的警告。Northern Renaissance painters did not work in Italian sunlight; they worked in the rain, stone walls, wool and ledgers of the Low Countries. Oil paint let them make the world transparent as glass: mirrors, windows, coins, fur, distant snow. Religious painting was not abandoned but filled with everyday detail; every object could be evidence of faith or a moral warning.

矫饰主义Mannerism

5 件5 works

矫饰主义是盛期文艺复兴的“过度自觉”。当比例、透视和人体都被解决之后，画家开始故意拉长、扭曲、堆叠，让画面变得优雅而不安。帕米贾尼诺把圣母的脖子拉长，埃尔·格列柯把托莱多画成一场闪电，颜色变得发疯，空间变得不稳定。它是对“完美”的怀疑，也是现代绘画第一次公开表演“风格”。Mannerism is the 'over-consciousness' of the High Renaissance. Once proportion, perspective and the body had been solved, painters began to stretch, twist and pile up forms, making the picture elegant and unsettled. Parmigianino lengthens the Virgin's neck, El Greco paints Toledo as lightning, colour goes wild and space becomes unstable. It is doubt about 'perfection', and the first time modern painting openly performs 'style'.

巴洛克Baroque

9 件9 works

巴洛克把画面变成舞台：光从黑暗中切下来，人物在动作的最高点停住，观众被安排在舞台正前方。它服务于两种权力：教堂和宫廷。卡拉瓦乔用光审判，鲁本斯用身体旋转，伦勃朗用光回忆，委拉斯开兹用镜子拆穿权力，维米尔把剧场缩小到一间安静的房。The Baroque turns the picture into a stage: light cuts through darkness, figures freeze at the peak of action, and the viewer is placed at the front. It served two powers: church and court. Caravaggio uses light to judge, Rubens uses bodies to rotate, Rembrandt uses light to remember, Velázquez uses mirrors to unmask power, and Vermeer shrinks the theatre to one quiet room.

洛可可Rococo

6 件6 works

洛可可是十八世纪宫廷和城市客厅的艺术：曲线、粉色、金色、丝绸、秋千、花园和爱情游戏。它常被骂成轻浮，但它其实在发明一种新的观看者：有闲暇、有隐私、有好奇心的市民。华托的忧郁、弗拉戈纳尔的顽皮、夏尔丹的物，都是同一个世纪的不同侧面。Rococo is the art of the eighteenth-century court and drawing room: curves, pink, gold, silk, swings, gardens and love games. It is often dismissed as frivolous, but it invents a new viewer: a citizen with leisure, privacy and curiosity. Watteau's melancholy, Fragonard's mischief and Chardin's objects are different faces of the same century.

新古典主义Neoclassicism

6 件6 works

新古典主义把身体变成公民，把画面变成宣言。它相信轮廓、义务、理性和古代德性，反对洛可可的轻浮。大卫的《荷拉斯兄弟之誓》让三只手同时举剑，《马拉之死》把政治谋杀整理成圣像。安格尔继承了大卫的线条，却把线条变成一种冷而性感的技术。Neoclassicism turns the body into a citizen and the picture into a manifesto. It believes in contour, duty, reason and ancient virtue, and rejects Rococo frivolity. David's *Oath of the Horatii* raises three hands toward the swords at once; *The Death of Marat* arranges political murder into an icon. Ingres inherits David's line but turns it into a cold, sensual technique.

浪漫主义Romanticism

8 件8 works

浪漫主义相信风暴、废墟、孤独和不可控制的自然。它把个体放到画面中心，也把个体放到自然的边缘。戈雅记录屠杀，弗里德里希让背影面对雾海，透纳把夕阳画成一场燃烧，德拉克洛瓦让自由举旗，艾瓦佐夫斯基让海浪高过船。Romanticism believes in storm, ruin, solitude and uncontrollable nature. It puts the individual at the center and at the edge of nature. Goya records massacre, Friedrich sets a back against the sea of fog, Turner paints sunset as combustion, Delacroix raises the flag of liberty, Aivazovsky makes the wave taller than the ship.

现实主义Realism

6 件6 works

现实主义是一种政治选择：把学院拒绝的人、地点和身体放到画面中心。库尔贝说“我没有画天使”，米勒让拾穗者占据前景，马奈把妓女和野餐者请进沙龙，列宾让十一个纤夫拉着一艘船。它不追求美，而追求“被看见”。Realism is a political choice: put the people, places and bodies the Academy rejected at the center. Courbet says he has never painted an angel, Millet lets gleaners occupy the foreground, Manet brings a courtesan and picnickers into the Salon, Repin harnesses eleven barge haulers to a boat. It does not pursue beauty but visibility.

印象主义Impressionism

6 件6 works

印象主义把画架搬到户外，也把“完成”搬到户外。它画光、天气、都市瞬间和日常娱乐：火车站、林荫大道、舞厅、阳台、摇篮。颜色不再描述物体，而是记录光的关系；轮廓让位给笔触，构图让位给裁切。Impressionism moves the easel outdoors and 'finished' outdoors with it. It paints light, weather, urban moments and everyday leisure: railway stations, boulevards, dance halls, balconies, cradles. Colour no longer describes objects but records relationships of light; contour gives way to touch, composition to cropping.

后印象主义Post-Impressionism

5 件5 works

后印象主义是四个不同的答案：修拉用点彩把光变成科学，梵高用旋转的笔触把情感变成运动，高更用平涂把文明退回神话，塞尚用几何把风景重新组装。他们都不再满足于“画下眼睛看到的”，而是问：画面能不能自己成立？Post-Impressionism is four different answers: Seurat turns light into science with dots, Van Gogh turns emotion into motion with swirling strokes, Gauguin flattens colour to return to myth, Cézanne reassembles landscape with geometry. None is satisfied with 'painting what the eye sees'; each asks whether the picture can stand on its own.

象征主义Symbolism

4 件4 works

象征主义不相信画应该解释世界。它相信画应该像梦一样，给出气氛、颜色和暗示，而不是答案。勃克林画死之岛，莫罗画朱庇特与塞墨勒，蒙克画一声穿过自然的尖叫，克里姆特把身体包进金色图案。它把神话、欲望、恐惧和死亡重新请回画面。Symbolism does not believe painting should explain the world. It believes painting should work like a dream, offering atmosphere, colour and suggestion rather than answers. Böcklin paints the Isle of the Dead, Moreau paints Jupiter and Semele, Munch paints a scream passing through nature, Klimt wraps the body in gold pattern. Myth, desire, fear and death return to the picture.

元代文人山水Yuan Literati Landscape

5 件5 works

元代文人山水是汉族士人在异族统治下建立的精神领地。黄公望、倪瓒、吴镇、王蒙把画当作日记、书信和哲学：山是避难所，水是距离，空白是拒绝。笔墨不再描摹地形，而是留下一个人的性格、师承和疲惫。Yuan literati landscape is a spiritual territory built by Han scholars under foreign rule. Huang Gongwang, Ni Zan, Wu Zhen and Wang Meng use painting as diary, letter and philosophy: mountain as refuge, water as distance, blank silk as refusal. Brush and ink no longer map terrain; they record personality, lineage and fatigue.

浮世绘Ukiyo-e

5 件5 works

浮世绘是江户的商业艺术：出版商委托，画师起稿，雕版师刻板，印刷师上色，商人卖给市民。它画演员、美人、相扑、茶馆、桥梁、海浪和雨。它不追求唯一性，而追求可复制、可传播、可消费的现代图像。Ukiyo-e is the commercial art of Edo: a publisher commissions, an artist designs, a carver cuts, a printer colours, a merchant sells to townspeople. It pictures actors, beauties, sumo, teahouses, bridges, waves and rain. It does not seek uniqueness but a modern image that can be reproduced, circulated and consumed.

表现主义Expressionism

7 件7 works

表现主义把颜色和线条从描述中解放出来，让它们直接传递情绪。蒙克的尖叫穿过天空，席勒的身体像一根绷紧的神经，基尔希纳的街道像一把斜切的刀。它不关心“世界看起来怎样”，而关心“世界让人感觉怎样”。Expressionism frees colour and line from description and makes them carry emotion directly. Munch's scream passes through the sky, Schiele's body is a taut nerve, Kirchner's street is a slanted knife. It does not care how the world looks but how the world feels.

两宋山水Song Landscape

5 件5 works

两宋山水不是一种风格，而是一套关于“人在自然里站在哪里”的回答。北宋把主峰放在中央，让山成为秩序的中心；南宋把山推到边角，让空白承担距离。范宽、巨然、马远、牧溪各自给出了不同的尺度：山可以压住人，也可以被人从一角观看。Song landscape is not one style but a set of answers to where humans stand in nature. Northern Song places the main peak at the centre and makes the mountain the centre of order; Southern Song pushes it to a corner and lets blank space carry distance. Fan Kuan, Juran, Ma Yuan and Muqi give different scales: the mountain can weigh on a person, or be watched from a corner.

宋代风俗画Song Genre Painting

4 件4 works

宋代风俗画把普通人、儿童、商贩、船夫和节庆放进画面，不是作为背景，而是作为主角。它和宫廷、文人、宗教画共享同一套笔墨，却把注意力放在一天里发生了什么：谁在桥上，谁在卖东西，谁在追一只跑掉的猫。Song genre painting puts ordinary people, children, pedlars, boatmen and festivals into the picture, not as background but as subject. It shares brush language with court, literati and religious painting, yet attends to what happens in a day: who is on the bridge, who is selling, who is chasing a runaway cat.

青绿山水Blue-Green Landscape

2 件2 works

青绿山水用石青、石绿把山画成发光的矿物，而不是水墨的灰。它不是为了“更真实”，而是为了把国土、仙境和王朝想象变成可携带的颜色。王希孟的《千里江山图》让十八岁的画家画出一个他从未走遍的天下。Blue-green landscape uses azurite and malachite to make mountains glow like minerals rather than grey ink. It does not aim at greater realism but turns territory, paradise and dynastic imagination into portable colour. Wang Ximeng's *A Thousand Li of Rivers and Mountains* lets an eighteen-year-old paint a realm he never walked.

吴门文人画Wu School Literati Painting

3 件3 works

吴门文人画把绘画放回一种生活方式：在苏州的园林、书斋、茶席和朋友圈里，画不是商品，而是唱和、题跋、收藏和教养的一部分。沈周、文徵明、唐寅、仇英把山水、人物、花鸟都变成可以交换的纸上时间。Wu school literati painting returns painting to a way of life: in Suzhou gardens, studios, tea gatherings and circles of friends, a picture is not a commodity but part of poetry exchange, colophon, collecting and education. Shen Zhou, Wen Zhengming, Tang Yin and Qiu Ying turn landscape, figure and flower into exchangeable time on paper.

琳派Rinpa

2 件2 works

琳派是日本江户时代最具装饰性的绘画传统，名字来自尾形光琳。它把金银箔、矿物颜料、垂水（tarashikomi）和书法放在同一张纸上，让图案、文字和材料互相渗透。琳派不追求自然主义的深度，它追求平面上的节奏。Rinpa is the most decorative painting tradition of Edo Japan, named after Ogata Kōrin. It places gold and silver foil, mineral pigments, tarashikomi and calligraphy on one sheet, letting pattern, text and material permeate one another. Rinpa does not seek naturalistic depth but rhythm on a flat plane.

莫卧儿细密画Mughal Miniature

4 件4 works

莫卧儿细密画把波斯书的版面、印度的人物传统和欧洲的透视放进同一张纸。它既画宫廷、狩猎、战争和爱情，也画一只羚羊、一棵树和一场梦。细密画的大小要求观看靠近：你必须把眼睛放到纸面上，才能看见权力和诗的细节。Mughal miniature joins Persian book layout, Indian figure traditions and European perspective on one page. It paints court, hunt, war and love, but also an antelope, a tree and a dream. Its scale demands closeness: you must bring your eye to the paper to see the details of power and poetry.

波斯细密画Persian Miniature

5 件5 works

波斯细密画把诗、历史、爱情和宫廷礼仪放进一个高度装饰化的平面。它不追求单点透视，而用颜色、金箔、花园和建筑把故事分层；人物可以在同一张纸上同时出现在不同的时间。Persian miniature puts poetry, history, love and court etiquette onto a highly decorated plane. It does not seek single-point perspective; colour, gold, garden and architecture stratify the story, and figures can appear at different times on the same page.

新艺术运动Art Nouveau

1 件1 works

新艺术运动把曲线、植物、女性身体和装饰性平面带进绘画、海报、建筑和家具。它反对学院派的僵硬，也反对工业品的粗糙，想让“现代生活”看起来像一条会流动的线。克里姆特的《吻》把这种曲线变成金箔上的两个人。Art Nouveau brought curve, plant, female body and decorative plane into painting, posters, architecture and furniture. It opposed academic stiffness and industrial ugliness, wanting modern life to look like a flowing line. Klimt's *The Kiss* turns that curve into two people on gold.

立体主义Cubism

1 件1 works

立体主义把对象拆成多个视点，再在同一张画上重新拼起来。它不再相信单点透视，也不再把颜色当作窗外的光；它把桌子、杯子、脸、报纸和字母变成平面上的结构。格里斯把这种结构画得最清楚：颜色像纸片，形体像建筑。Cubism breaks an object into multiple viewpoints and reassembles them on one canvas. It no longer trusts single-point perspective or colour as outdoor light; table, cup, face, newspaper and letter become structure on a plane. Gris makes that structure clearest: colour like paper, form like architecture.

小径Trails

光、身体、城市、自然、信仰、战争、肖像、日常。Light, body, city, nature, faith, war, portrait, everyday.

光Light

画家不画光本身，他们画光落在什么上面。这条小径从烛光、圣光走到日光、火光和电光，看光如何从神的属性变成画家的工具。Painters do not paint light itself; they paint what light falls on. This trail moves from candle, holy light and sunlight to fire and electric light, tracing how light becomes a painter's instrument.

身体Body

身体是绘画最古老的题目，也是最难诚实的题目。这条小径从神性身体走到劳动身体、欲望身体和变形身体。The body is painting's oldest subject and its hardest honesty. This trail moves from divine body to labouring, desiring and distorted body.

城市The city

城市在画里先是背景，后来变成主角。这条小径从汴京的桥走到巴黎的酒吧，看画家如何把人群、建筑和速度组织成一张图。The city begins as background and becomes protagonist. This trail moves from the Bianjing bridge to a Paris bar, tracing how painters organise crowd, architecture and speed.

自然Nature

自然在画里有时是神的书，有时是人的对立面，有时只是光和天气。这条小径从北宋主峰走到浪漫主义的雾海。Nature in painting is sometimes God's book, sometimes man's opposite, sometimes only light and weather. This trail moves from a Northern Song peak to a Romantic sea of fog.

信仰Faith

从圣像到祭坛画，从敦煌到西斯廷，信仰给了绘画最稳定的题目，也给了它最严格的规则。这条小径看画家如何在规则里加入人的温度。From icon to altarpiece, from Dunhuang to the Sistine, faith gave painting its most stable subject and its strictest rules. This trail looks at how painters add human warmth inside those rules.

战争War

战争画很少真的在画战争，它更多在画战争之后：尸体、幸存者、审判、纪念。这条小径从枪口走到纪念碑。War painting rarely paints war itself; it paints what comes after: bodies, survivors, judgment, memorial. This trail moves from rifle muzzles to monuments.

肖像Portrait

肖像不是脸的照片，它是一份关于“这个人为什么值得被记住”的提案。这条小径从皇帝、商人、画家自己走到一个回头的少女。A portrait is not a photograph of a face; it is a proposal about why this person deserves to be remembered. This trail moves from emperor and merchant to the painter himself and a girl turning her head.

日常The everyday

日常画不靠神话、历史或奇观，它靠一双愿意停下来的眼睛。这条小径从厨房的鱼走到卧室、街道和餐桌。Everyday painting relies on no myth, history or spectacle, only an eye willing to stop. This trail moves from a kitchen fish to bedroom, street and table.

区域入口Regions

八个地方史入口，不是八块国别史。Eight local entries, not eight national histories.

意大利Italy

6 件6 works

从乔托的帕多瓦到卡拉瓦乔的罗马，意大利把绘画变成身体、透视和光的实验室。本区域页按“城市—赞助—作品”组织，不把意大利等同于文艺复兴。From Giotto's Padua to Caravaggio's Rome, Italy turned painting into a laboratory of body, perspective and light. This hub is organised by city, patronage and work; it does not reduce Italy to the Renaissance.

低地国家Low Countries

4 件4 works

尼德兰和荷兰把油画、版画和室内空间推到极致。凡·艾克的镜子、勃鲁盖尔的工地、伦勃朗的暗光、维米尔的珍珠，都在小尺度里处理大问题：信仰、劳动、权力和安静。The Netherlands and Flanders pushed oil, print and interior space to an extreme. Van Eyck's mirror, Bruegel's building site, Rembrandt's darkness and Vermeer's pearl handle large questions at small scale: faith, labour, power and silence.

法国France

4 件4 works

法国从古典主义到印象派，不断重定义“谁可以被画”。库尔贝把工人和画室放进大画，莫奈和毕沙罗把城市、铁路和光拆成笔触；绘画从沙龙走向街头。From classicism to Impressionism, France repeatedly redefined who could be painted. Courbet put workers and the studio into large pictures; Monet and Pissarro broke city, railway and light into brushwork. Painting moved from salon to street.

德语区German-speaking lands

6 件6 works

德语区把版画、表现主义和身体焦虑连成一条线。丢勒用线建立可复制的图像，克里姆特用金箔把身体变成装饰，基尔希纳、贝克曼和席勒则把城市、战争和疾病压进扭曲的人体。German-speaking lands connect print, Expressionism and bodily anxiety. Dürer builds a reproducible image with line; Klimt turns the body into gold decoration; Kirchner, Beckmann and Schiele press city, war and illness into distorted bodies.

英国Britain

4 件4 works

英国绘画在风景、工业和时间之间工作。透纳把海、天和蒸汽画成颜色，惠斯勒把桥和夜拆成色调，博恩把胜利日画成港口里的船与人群。这里没有单一“英国性”，只有不断变化的天气和制度。British painting works between landscape, industry and time. Turner paints sea, sky and steam as colour; Whistler reduces bridge and night to tone; Bone paints Victory Day as boats and crowds in a harbour. There is no single 'Britishness', only changing weather and institutions.

伊比利亚Iberia

4 件4 works

西班牙把宫廷、宗教和暴力画在同一张画布上。委拉斯开兹的镜子、戈雅的黑画和《1808年5月3日》、格列柯的瘦长身体，都在处理权力如何看见和被看见。Spain paints court, religion and violence on the same canvas. Velázquez's mirror, Goya's Black Paintings and *Third of May 1808*, El Greco's elongated bodies all deal with how power sees and is seen.

中国China

6 件6 works

中国绘画把线、墨、空白和装裱连成一套观看制度。顾恺之的线、范宽的峰、张择端的城、黄公望的坡、倪瓒的空亭、徐悲鸿的身体，各自回答“人如何进入世界”。Chinese painting joins line, ink, blank space and mounting into a viewing system. Gu Kaizhi's line, Fan Kuan's peak, Zhang Zeduan's city, Huang Gongwang's slope, Ni Zan's empty pavilion and Xu Beihong's body each answer how a person enters the world.

日本Japan

3 件3 works

日本绘画在屏风、版画和卷轴之间切换尺度。宗达的燕子花、春信的少女、北斋的浪、广重的桥，都把日常、季节和城市折进装饰性的平面。Japanese painting moves between screen, print and scroll. Sōtatsu's irises, Harunobu's girls, Hokusai's wave and Hiroshige's bridge fold everyday life, season and city into a decorative plane.

对读Dialogues

把两张画放在一起，看它们各自回答什么。Put two pictures together and see what each answers.

线与信仰：顾恺之与乔托Line and Faith: Gu Kaizhi and Giotto

对读Dialogue

两个相隔近千年的画家，都用线把人物从背景里拉出来；但一个服务于箴言，一个服务于福音。把《女史箴图》和《哀悼基督》并置，问题不是“谁更写实”，而是线替谁说话。Two painters separated by nearly a millennium both pull figures out of the background with line; one serves maxims, the other the Gospel. Set *Admonitions* beside the *Lamentation* and the question is not 'who is more realistic' but for whom the line speaks.

城市与人群：张择端与勃鲁盖尔City and Crowd: Zhang Zeduan and Bruegel

对读Dialogue

一个画汴京的桥与船，一个画巴别塔的工地。两幅画都把“人群”当作主角，但一个在记录城市如何运转，一个在记录人类如何失败。One paints the bridge and boats of Bianjing, the other the building site of Babel. Both make the crowd the protagonist, but one records how a city works, the other how humanity fails.

空白与黑暗：倪瓒与卡拉瓦乔Blank and Dark: Ni Zan and Caravaggio

对读Dialogue

倪瓒用空白让画面变轻，卡拉瓦乔用黑暗让画面变重。两种极端都在处理同一个问题：当人和世界之间没有直接接触，绘画如何让“距离”可见？Ni Zan uses blank space to make the picture light; Caravaggio uses darkness to make it heavy. Both extremes handle one question: when there is no direct contact between person and world, how does painting make distance visible?
