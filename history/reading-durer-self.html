《自画像（二十八岁）》Self-Portrait at the Age of 28

画家为什么把自己画成基督？Why does the painter paint himself as Christ?
1500 · 阿尔布雷希特·丢勒1500 · Albrecht Dürer精读Close reading
绘画史Painting History / 精读Close readings
一件作品，一个问题；先看，再判断。One work, one question; look first, judge later.

图片为该作品在本站的主图；元数据见下方作品卡。The image is the primary image for this work; metadata follows in the work card.

问题Question

丢勒在1500年把自己画成正面、对称、长发、持貂皮领的形象，像一尊圣像。这不是自大，而是把艺术家的创造能力放进宗教框架：画家也在“造人”。

In 1500 Dürer paints himself frontal, symmetrical, long-haired, in a fur collar, like an icon. This is not arrogance but placing the artist's creative power inside a religious frame: the painter also 'makes man'.

语境Context

1500年木板油画，藏于慕尼黑老绘画陈列馆。丢勒是北方文艺复兴最重要的版画家和画家之一。

Oil on panel, 1500, held by the Alte Pinakothek, Munich. Dürer is one of the most important painters and printmakers of the Northern Renaissance.

怎么看How to look

  • 先看正面性：他为什么直视我们？
  • 再看手：它在指自己，还是指画？
  • 最后看题字：他写了什么？
  • Read frontality: why look straight at us?
  • Then read the hand: pointing to himself, or the picture?
  • Finally read the inscription: what does it say?

为什么重要Why it matters

它把“艺术家”从工匠提升为可以自我神化的人。

It raises the artist from craftsman to a person who can mythologise himself.
