《巴别塔》The Tower of Babel

一座塔如何画出入类的傲慢？How does a tower paint human arrogance?
1563 · 老彼得·勃鲁盖尔1563 · Pieter Bruegel the Elder精读Close reading
绘画史Painting History / 精读Close readings
一件作品，一个问题；先看，再判断。One work, one question; look first, judge later.

图片为该作品在本站的主图；元数据见下方作品卡。The image is the primary image for this work; metadata follows in the work card.

问题Question

勃鲁盖尔把巴别塔画成一座正在施工的巨型建筑：螺旋坡道、脚手架、石匠、吊车，所有细节都在工作。塔顶还没完成，海和城市已经在它脚下变小。

Bruegel paints Babel as a huge building under construction: spiral ramp, scaffolding, masons, cranes, every detail at work. The top is unfinished, sea and city already small beneath it.

语境Context

1563年木板油画，藏于维也纳艺术史博物馆。勃鲁盖尔是16世纪尼德兰最重要的风俗与风景画家。

Oil on panel, 1563, held by the Kunsthistorisches Museum, Vienna. Bruegel is the key Netherlandish painter of genre and landscape in the sixteenth century.

怎么看How to look

  • 先看塔顶：它完成了吗？
  • 再看工人：他们在建，还是在修？
  • 最后看比例：城市有多小？
  • Read the top: is it finished?
  • Then read the workers: building, or repairing?
  • Finally read scale: how small is the city?

为什么重要Why it matters

它把神话变成工程现场，让傲慢看起来像一项正在失败的项目。

It turns myth into a construction site, making arrogance look like a failing project.
