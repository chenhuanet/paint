约翰内斯·维米尔Johannes Vermeer

维米尔一辈子住在代尔夫特，画的多是室内：一扇窗、一张桌、一块桌布、一个女人。他的人物不做什么大事，只是读信、倒奶、戴耳环、站在窗边。维米尔把“安静”画成一种几乎可以听见的东西。Vermeer lived his whole life in Delft and painted mostly interiors: a window, a table, a cloth, a woman. His figures do nothing grand; they read a letter, pour milk, wear an earring, stand by a window. Vermeer paints quiet as something almost audible.

小传Biography

《戴珍珠耳环的少女》不是肖像，而是一个瞬间：少女回头，嘴唇微开，珍珠在暗处发亮。维米尔用极细的笔触和冷蓝、暖黄制造光的颗粒。他留下的作品很少，却让日常拥有了神圣的密度。

Girl with a Pearl Earring is not a portrait but an instant: the girl turns, lips slightly open, the pearl shines in shadow. Vermeer uses minute touches and cool blue/warm yellow to make light granular. He left few works, but gave the everyday a sacred density.

看窗：光从哪里来？看珍珠：它为什么比脸还亮？Read the window: where does light enter? Read the pearl: why is it brighter than the face?
