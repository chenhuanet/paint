青绿山水Blue-Green Landscape

青绿山水用石青、石绿把山画成发光的矿物，而不是水墨的灰。它不是为了“更真实”，而是为了把国土、仙境和王朝想象变成可携带的颜色。王希孟的《千里江山图》让十八岁的画家画出一个他从未走遍的天下。Blue-green landscape uses azurite and malachite to make mountains glow like minerals rather than grey ink. It does not aim at greater realism but turns territory, paradise and dynastic imagination into portable colour. Wang Ximeng's *A Thousand Li of Rivers and Mountains* lets an eighteen-year-old paint a realm he never walked.

三个特征Three traits

  • 颜色是结构：石青、石绿层层罩染，山脊因此有了体积。
  • 长卷是国土：画面不是单点透视，而是可移动、可停留的旅程。
  • 仙境与政治：青绿既像仙境，也像朝廷希望拥有的疆域。
  • Colour is structure: azurite and malachite are layered until ridges gain volume.
  • The handscroll is territory: not single-point perspective but a journey one can move through and pause in.
  • Paradise and politics: blue-green is both a paradise and the territory a court wants to possess.

怎么看How to look

  • 先看颜色层：石青在什么地方最亮？
  • 再看山脊线：它是轮廓，还是建筑的边界？
  • 最后看长卷的停顿：哪里可以住人，哪里只能经过？
  • Start with colour layers: where is azurite brightest?
  • Then read ridges: contour, or architectural boundary?
  • Finally read the scroll's pauses: where could one live, where only pass through?
