詹姆斯·麦克尼尔·惠斯勒James McNeill Whistler

惠斯勒是美国人，长期在伦敦工作。他反对把画当成故事或道德课，主张“为艺术而艺术”，用音乐术语给画命名：协奏曲、夜曲、 arrangement。Whistler was American but worked for decades in London. He rejected painting as story or moral lesson, arguing for 'art for art's sake' and naming works with musical terms: concerto, nocturne, arrangement.

小传Biography

《灰与黑的协奏曲：画家母亲肖像》把母亲画成侧面，灰、黑、白被安排得像一首低音曲；《查令十字铁路桥》则用石版画的细线把桥、河和雾切成平面。惠斯勒的“抽象”不是去掉对象，而是让对象服从色调。

Arrangement in Grey and Black No. 1 paints his mother in profile, grey, black and white arranged like a bass line; Charing Cross Railway Bridge uses fine lithographic lines to cut bridge, river and mist into planes. Whistler's 'abstraction' does not remove the object; it makes the object obey tone.
