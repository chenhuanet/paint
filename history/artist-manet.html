爱德华·马奈Édouard Manet

马奈是现实主义到现代艺术的桥梁。他拒绝把裸体变成神话，也拒绝把观看变成礼貌。《奥林匹亚》让一个真实的巴黎女人直视观者，《草地上的午餐》让穿衣男人和裸体女人坐在同一片草地上。Manet is the bridge from Realism to modern art. He refuses to turn nudity into myth or looking into politeness. *Olympia* lets a real Parisian woman look directly at us; *Le Déjeuner sur l'herbe* puts clothed men and a nude woman on the same grass.

小传Biography

马奈的画笔很平，颜色很亮，空间被压扁。他不追求“深度”，而追求“事实”：画布是画布，女人是女人，观者是观者。1865年的沙龙骂他“污秽”，但现代绘画从他开始。

Manet's touch is flat, colour bright, space compressed. He does not pursue 'depth' but 'fact': the canvas is canvas, the woman is a woman, the viewer is a viewer. The 1865 Salon called him obscene, but modern painting begins with him.

看目光：谁在看我们？看空间：它为什么被压平了？Read the gaze: who looks at us? Read space: why is it flattened?
