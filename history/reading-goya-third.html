《1808年5月3日》The Third of May 1808

光如何替受害者说话？How does light speak for the victim?
1814 · 弗朗西斯科·戈雅1814 · Francisco Goya精读Close reading
绘画史Painting History / 精读Close readings
一件作品，一个问题；先看，再判断。One work, one question; look first, judge later.

图片为该作品在本站的主图；元数据见下方作品卡。The image is the primary image for this work; metadata follows in the work card.

问题Question

戈雅把行刑画成一场在夜里发生的屠杀：白衬衫的受害者张开手臂，士兵的背影像一堵墙，灯笼把恐惧压在地上。画面没有英雄，只有被照亮的死者和看不见的脸。

Goya paints an execution as a night massacre: the victim in a white shirt opens his arms, soldiers' backs form a wall, a lantern presses terror onto the ground. There is no hero, only the lit dead and invisible faces.

语境Context

1814年布面油画，藏于马德里普拉多博物馆。作品回应1808年5月3日马德里起义者被法军处决的事件。

Oil on canvas, 1814, held by the Museo del Prado, Madrid. It responds to the execution of Madrid insurgents by French troops on 3 May 1808.

怎么看How to look

  • 先看白衬衫：它为什么最亮？
  • 再看士兵：他们有脸吗？
  • 最后看灯笼：光从哪里来？
  • Read the white shirt: why is it brightest?
  • Then read the soldiers: do they have faces?
  • Finally read the lantern: where does light come from?

为什么重要Why it matters

它把历史画从胜利纪念碑变成对暴力的控诉。

It turns history painting from victory monument into indictment of violence.
