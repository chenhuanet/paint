后印象主义Post-Impressionism

后印象主义是四个不同的答案：修拉用点彩把光变成科学，梵高用旋转的笔触把情感变成运动，高更用平涂把文明退回神话，塞尚用几何把风景重新组装。他们都不再满足于“画下眼睛看到的”，而是问：画面能不能自己成立？Post-Impressionism is four different answers: Seurat turns light into science with dots, Van Gogh turns emotion into motion with swirling strokes, Gauguin flattens colour to return to myth, Cézanne reassembles landscape with geometry. None is satisfied with 'painting what the eye sees'; each asks whether the picture can stand on its own.

三个特征Three traits

  • 笔触成为主角：点、漩涡、平涂、色阶，每一种都在替画家思考。
  • 颜色脱离描述：黄色可以是情绪，蓝色可以是夜，红色可以是重量。
  • 画面自我组织：对象还在，但结构、节奏和表面开始独立。
  • Touch becomes protagonist: dots, vortices, flat areas, tonal patches — each thinks for the painter.
  • Colour leaves description: yellow can be emotion, blue night, red weight.
  • The picture organises itself: objects remain, but structure, rhythm and surface become independent.

怎么看How to look

  • 看笔触：它在替你做什么？
  • 看颜色：它是否还服从物体？
  • 看空间：透视还在吗？画面向深处退，还是向表面压？
  • Read the touch: what is it doing for you?
  • Read colour: does it still obey objects?
  • Read space: is perspective present, and does the picture recede or flatten?
