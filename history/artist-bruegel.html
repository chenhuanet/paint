老彼得·勃鲁盖尔Pieter Bruegel the Elder

勃鲁盖尔画农民、雪、节日、盲人、乞丐和塔。他不把农民画成可爱的田园符号，而是画成有重量、有胃口、有脾气的人。他的画面常常很大，人物很小，环境才是主角。Bruegel paints peasants, snow, festivals, blind men, beggars and towers. He does not turn peasants into cute pastoral symbols but into people with weight, appetite and temper. His pictures are often large, his figures small; the environment is the protagonist.

小传Biography

《雪中猎人》让猎人背对观者下山，村庄在雪里，鸟从树上飞起，冰面上有人滑。勃鲁盖尔不解释意义，只把冬天放在你面前；《巴别塔》则把傲慢画成一座螺旋上升的工地，塔顶还没封顶，权力已经分裂。

Hunters in the Snow sends hunters downhill with their backs to us, the village under snow, birds rising, skaters on the ice. Bruegel does not explain; he places winter in front of you. The Tower of Babel paints pride as a spiral building site: the top is unfinished, power already divided.

看环境：人为什么这么小？看雪：它覆盖了什么，又暴露了什么？Read the environment: why are people so small? Read the snow: what does it cover, and what does it expose?
