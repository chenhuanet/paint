现代主义的裂解：1900–1945The Fracture of Modernism: 1900–1945

当颜色、线条和视角都不再服从现实，绘画还剩下什么？When colour, line and viewpoint no longer obey reality, what is left of painting?
1900–19451900–1945第 17 章Chapter 17
绘画史Painting History / 第 17 章Chapter 17
现代主义不是一条直线，而是一连串裂口。克里姆特把身体变成金箔图案，席勒把身体拉成紧张的线，基尔希纳把城市切成斜角，康定斯基让颜色脱离对象，蒙德里安把世界压成格子，格里斯把对象拆成多视点。每一个都在问：如果画不再像世界，它还能做什么？Modernism is not a straight line but a series of fractures. Klimt turns the body into gold pattern, Schiele stretches it into tense line, Kirchner cuts the city into diagonals, Kandinsky detaches colour from object, Mondrian presses the world into a grid, Gris breaks the object into multiple viewpoints. Each asks: if painting no longer resembles the world, what can it do?

身体：从金箔到伤口The body: from gold to wound

克里姆特的《吻》把两个人裹进金箔和图案，身体几乎消失在装饰里；席勒的自画像却把身体拉回骨头和神经，眼睛直视观者。一个用装饰保护身体，一个把保护撕开。现代主义对“人”的想象，就在这两极之间摆动。

Klimt's The Kiss wraps two bodies in gold and pattern until the body almost disappears into decoration; Schiele's self-portrait pulls the body back to bone and nerve, eyes straight at the viewer. One uses decoration to protect the body, the other tears the protection open. Modernism's imagination of 'the human' swings between these poles.

抽象：不是没有对象，而是对象退场Abstraction: not no object, but object withdrawing

康定斯基让颜色和线像音乐一样组织，蒙德里安把树、海和建筑压成水平与垂直，马列维奇把一切都收进一个黑方块。抽象不是“随便画”，而是把绘画的元素——颜色、线、面、边界——变成主角。

Kandinsky organises colour and line like music, Mondrian reduces tree, sea and building to horizontals and verticals, Malevich pulls everything into a black square. Abstraction is not 'anything goes'; it makes painting's elements — colour, line, plane, edge — the protagonists.

历史、纪念碑与街头History, monument and street

徐悲鸿的《田横五百士》把中国历史画成一群有体积的人，英雄在中间，群众向两边退开；伍德的《美国哥特式》把两个农民放在木屋前，叉子和窗框像纪念碑。两幅画都在制造“国家的脸”。波萨达的版画则走另一条路：骷髅、政客和灾难为廉价印刷品而作，在街头被一眼读懂。纪念碑与复制，是现代主义同时向外扩张的两端。

Xu Beihong's Tianheng and His Five Hundred Followers paints Chinese history as volumetric bodies, hero at the centre, crowd withdrawing; Wood's American Gothic places two farmers before a wooden house, pitchfork and window like a monument. Both manufacture a 'national face'. Posada's prints take another route: skulls, politicians and disasters made for cheap broadsheets, read at a glance in the street. Monument and reproduction are the two outward expansions of modernism.

怎么看How to look

  • 先看对象还剩下多少：脸、身体、桌子、城市？
  • 再看颜色：它是在描述光，还是自己在发声？
  • 最后看边界：画面是被切开，还是被压平？
  • Start with how much object remains: face, body, table, city?
  • Then read colour: describing light, or speaking for itself?
  • Finally read the edge: cut open, or flattened?
