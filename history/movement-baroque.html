巴洛克Baroque

巴洛克把画面变成舞台：光从黑暗中切下来，人物在动作的最高点停住，观众被安排在舞台正前方。它服务于两种权力：教堂和宫廷。卡拉瓦乔用光审判，鲁本斯用身体旋转，伦勃朗用光回忆，委拉斯开兹用镜子拆穿权力，维米尔把剧场缩小到一间安静的房。The Baroque turns the picture into a stage: light cuts through darkness, figures freeze at the peak of action, and the viewer is placed at the front. It served two powers: church and court. Caravaggio uses light to judge, Rubens uses bodies to rotate, Rembrandt uses light to remember, Velázquez uses mirrors to unmask power, and Vermeer shrinks the theatre to one quiet room.

先看三个特征，再看三件作品；最后把它放回它所在的章节。Start with three traits, then three works; finally put it back into its chapter.

三个特征Three traits

  • 强对比：亮部与暗部之间几乎没有过渡，光成为一个角色。
  • 动作的最高点：人物在即将发生或刚刚发生的瞬间被冻结。
  • 观众的位置：画面假定你站在舞台前，甚至站在画中人的视线里。
  • Strong contrast: almost no transition between light and dark; light becomes a character.
  • The peak of action: figures are frozen at the moment just before or after something happens.
  • The viewer's position: the picture assumes you stand at the stage, even inside a gaze.

怎么看How to look

  • 找光源：它从哪里来？为什么只照亮一个人？
  • 看动作：这是开始、进行还是结束？
  • 看观众：谁在看我们？我们站在谁的位置？
  • Find the light: where does it enter, and why does it illuminate one person?
  • Read the action: beginning, middle or end?
  • Read the viewer: who looks at us, and whose position do we occupy?
