城市The city

城市在画里先是背景，后来变成主角。这条小径从汴京的桥走到巴黎的酒吧，看画家如何把人群、建筑和速度组织成一张图。The city begins as background and becomes protagonist. This trail moves from the Bianjing bridge to a Paris bar, tracing how painters organise crowd, architecture and speed.

沿途Along the trail

约1085–1145 · 张择端ca.1085–1145 · Zhang Zeduan
《清明上河图》Along the River During the Qingming Festival

桥是城市的关节：船、人、货、看客在这里交换方向。The bridge is the city's joint: boats, people, goods and spectators exchange directions.

1563 · 老彼得·勃鲁盖尔1563 · Pieter Bruegel the Elder
《巴别塔》The Tower of Babel

城市变成一座永远建不完的塔。The city becomes a tower that can never be finished.

1872 · 克劳德·莫奈1872 · Claude Monet
《印象·日出》Impression, Sunrise

港口、工厂和船影在晨雾里混成一片，城市从水面上升起。Harbour, factories and masts merge in morning mist; the city rises from the water.

1882 · 爱德华·马奈1882 · Édouard Manet
《女神游乐厅的吧台》A Bar at the Folies-Bergère

酒吧里的镜子把人群变成一场闪烁的表演。The bar mirror turns the crowd into a flickering performance.

1930 · 格兰特·伍德1930 · Grant Wood
《美国哥特式》American Gothic

城市之外，农舍和干草叉也是国家形象。Outside the city, farmhouse and pitchfork are also national images.

走完之后After the walk

城市画不是地图，它是人群如何相互看见的方式。A city painting is not a map; it is a way people see one another.
