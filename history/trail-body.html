身体Body

身体是绘画最古老的题目，也是最难诚实的题目。这条小径从神性身体走到劳动身体、欲望身体和变形身体。The body is painting's oldest subject and its hardest honesty. This trail moves from divine body to labouring, desiring and distorted body.

绘画史Painting History / 小径Trails
按停靠顺序走；每一站只留一条线索，最后回到收束句。Follow the stops in order; each stop leaves one thread, and the closing sentence ties them together.

沿途Along the trail

1511–1512 · 米开朗基罗1511–1512 · Michelangelo Buonarroti
《创造亚当》The Creation of Adam

身体像建筑：肌肉是拱，手指是接点。The body as architecture: muscle is arch, finger is contact.

1612–1614 · 彼得·保罗·鲁本斯1612–1614 · Peter Paul Rubens
《下十字架》The Descent from the Cross

身体很重，搬动它需要一整个团队。The body is heavy; moving it takes a whole team.

1855 · 居斯塔夫·库尔贝1855 · Gustave Courbet
《画室》The Painter's Studio

左边的身体是劳动的身体，不是神话的身体。On the left, bodies are labouring bodies, not mythical ones.

1893 · 爱德华·蒙克1893 · Edvard Munch
《呐喊》The Scream

身体被声音抽空，只剩一个扭曲的轮廓。The body is emptied by sound, reduced to a twisted contour.

走完之后After the walk

身体在画里从不只是身体：它总是某种秩序、欲望或恐惧的证据。In painting the body is never only a body: it is evidence of an order, a desire or a fear.
