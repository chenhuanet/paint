内心的剧场：象征、表现与新艺术The Inner Theatre: Symbolism, Expressionism and Art Nouveau

梦、焦虑与装饰如何进入现代绘画？How did dream, anxiety and decoration enter modern painting?
1883–19131883–1913第 10 章Chapter 10
绘画史Painting History / 第 10 章Chapter 10
当印象主义还在画阳光，另一批画家已经把画面转向内部：勃克林画死之岛，莫罗画朱庇特与塞墨勒，蒙克画一声穿过自然的尖叫，克里姆特把身体变成金箔与图案，席勒把身体画成紧绷的神经，基尔希纳把街道画成倾斜的刀。象征主义、表现主义和新艺术运动共享一个信念：颜色和线条可以像梦一样工作。While Impressionism was still painting sunlight, another group of artists turned the picture inward: Böcklin paints the Isle of the Dead, Moreau paints Jupiter and Semele, Munch paints a scream passing through nature, Klimt turns the body into gold and pattern, Schiele paints the body as a taut nerve, Kirchner paints the street as a slanted knife. Symbolism, Expressionism and Art Nouveau share one belief: colour and line can work like a dream.

梦不是故事，是一种颜色Dream is not a story; it is a colour

《死之岛》把死亡画成一座被柏树围住的孤岛，白色的小船正把一口棺材送进去。没有恐怖，也没有安慰，只有一种静止的、几乎过分的安静。勃克林不解释死后世界，他给你一个可以走进去的布景。

莫罗的《朱庇特与塞墨勒》更华丽：朱庇特以真身出现，塞墨勒在金光中消失，身体边缘长出藤蔓和眼睛。象征主义把神话当成心理地图：神不是信仰对象，而是欲望、恐惧和毁灭的化身。

Isle of the Dead paints death as an island ringed by cypresses, a white boat carrying a coffin inward. There is no horror and no consolation, only a stillness that is almost excessive. Böcklin does not explain the afterlife; he gives you a set you can walk into.

Moreau's Jupiter and Semele is more ornate: Jupiter appears in his true form, Semele dissolves in gold light, vines and eyes grow from the edge of her body. Symbolism uses myth as a psychological map: the god is not an object of faith but an embodiment of desire, fear and destruction.

焦虑的线条，装饰的表面Lines of anxiety, surfaces of decoration

《呐喊》里，天空是红的，水是蓝黑的，桥栏斜切画面，尖叫的人捂住耳朵。蒙克不是画“一个人在喊”，而是画“自然在喊”：声音变成了波浪，穿过整个空间。表现主义把颜色从描述中解放出来，让它直接传递情绪。

克里姆特的《吻》把两个身体包进金色图案，像一枚圣物盒。新艺术运动相信装饰可以改变生活：线条从建筑流到海报、家具、珠宝和绘画。但装饰也有阴影：席勒的身体太瘦，基尔希纳的街道太斜，惠斯勒的灰与黑太安静。美和焦虑在同一个房间里。

In The Scream, the sky is red, the water blue-black, the bridge rail cuts diagonally, and the screaming figure covers its ears. Munch does not paint 'a person screaming' but 'nature screaming': sound becomes a wave passing through space. Expressionism frees colour from description and makes it carry emotion directly.

Klimt's The Kiss wraps two bodies in gold pattern like a reliquary. Art Nouveau believed decoration could change life: lines flow from architecture to posters, furniture, jewellery and painting. But decoration has shadows: Schiele's bodies are too thin, Kirchner's streets too slanted, Whistler's grey and black too quiet. Beauty and anxiety share the same room.

怎么看How to look

  • 看线条：它是装饰的、神经质的，还是像刀一样斜切？
  • 看颜色：颜色是否在描述物体，还是在描述情绪？
  • 看身体：身体是被包裹、被拉长，还是被拆开？它还有 skin 吗？
  • Read the line: decorative, nervous, or slashing like a knife?
  • Read colour: is it describing objects or emotions?
  • Read the body: wrapped, elongated or dismantled? Does it still have skin?
