保罗·塞尚Paul Cézanne

塞尚是“现代绘画之父”。他不满足于印象派的光，他要画光的结构：山、树、房子由一块块色阶组成，透视被轻微错开，画面像从多个角度看同一片风景。Cézanne is the 'father of modern painting'. He was not satisfied with Impressionist light; he wanted its structure: mountain, tree and house built from patches of tone, perspective slightly shifted, the picture seen from several angles at once.

小传Biography

《圣维克多山》里，山不是一座远山，而是一堆互相咬合的色块；天空和地面用同一种笔触，空间被压成表面。塞尚的“几何”不是抽象，而是让画面自己站稳。他直接启发了立体主义和抽象艺术。

In Mont Sainte-Victoire, the mountain is not a distant object but a stack of interlocking colour patches; sky and ground share the same touch, space compressed toward surface. Cézanne's 'geometry' is not abstraction but a way to make the picture stand by itself. He directly prepared Cubism and abstraction.

看色块：山由几块组成？看透视：它为什么对不齐？Read the patches: how many make the mountain? Read perspective: why doesn't it align?
