文艺复兴的发明The invention of the Renaissance

十四到十五世纪，欧洲绘画重新发现身体、空间和古代。乔托让哀悼有重量，马萨乔让透视成为叙事，凡·艾克让油彩透明，波提切利让神话回到佛罗伦萨。中国元代文人则把山水变成笔墨的人格。In the fourteenth and fifteenth centuries European painting rediscovered body, space and antiquity. Giotto gives grief weight, Masaccio makes perspective narrative, Van Eyck makes oil transparent, Botticelli brings myth back to Florence. In Yuan China, literati turn landscape into the personality of brush and ink.

三个关键词Three keywords

  • 透视：线性透视让画面有了可测量的深度。
  • 人体：解剖、比例、重量，身体不再是符号。
  • 赞助：美第奇、行会、修道院，决定画什么和怎么画。
  • Perspective: linear perspective gives the picture measurable depth.
  • The body: anatomy, proportion and weight replace the symbol.
  • Patronage: Medici, guilds and monasteries decide what and how to paint.
