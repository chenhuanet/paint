小传Biography

《下十字架》把一具死去的身体从右上斜降到左下，红色、白色和肉色在暗背景里互相推挤；《伊莎贝拉·布兰特肖像》则把亲密画得很安静。鲁本斯的力量不在夸张，而在重量：每一块肌肉都像真的被地心吸住。

The Descent from the Cross drops a dead body diagonally from upper right to lower left, red, white and flesh pushing against a dark ground; the Portrait of Isabella Brant makes intimacy quiet. Rubens's power is not exaggeration but weight: every muscle seems held by gravity.
