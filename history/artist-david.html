雅克-路易·大卫Jacques-Louis David

大卫是新古典主义的旗手，也是法国大革命的艺术导演。他相信绘画应该教育公民：身体要像雕塑，姿态要像誓言，颜色要让位给轮廓。David is the standard-bearer of Neoclassicism and the art director of the French Revolution. He believed painting should educate citizens: the body like sculpture, the gesture like an oath, colour subordinate to contour.

小传Biography

《荷拉斯兄弟之誓》让三个儿子和父亲举剑，女人在后景哭泣；《马拉之死》把政治谋杀整理成圣像，木箱上的字条是证据，刀在地上。大卫的理性并不平静，它可以把暴力整理成一张干净的图。

The Oath of the Horatii raises three sons and a father toward the swords, women weeping behind; The Death of Marat arranges political murder into an icon, the note on the block as evidence, the knife on the floor. David's reason is not calm; it can arrange violence into a clean image.

看手势：它在发誓吗？看颜色：它为什么让位给轮廓？Read the gesture: is it swearing? Read colour: why does it yield to contour?
