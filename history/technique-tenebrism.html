暗绘Tenebrism

暗绘把画面大部分压进黑暗，只用一束强光把人物、手势和表情从黑里挖出来。卡拉瓦乔不是单纯“画得黑”，他让黑暗成为叙事的一部分：光从哪里来，谁被照到，谁留在暗处，都是权力和信仰的问题。Tenebrism presses most of the picture into darkness and uses a single strong light to cut figures, gestures and expressions out of black. Caravaggio does not simply 'paint dark'; darkness becomes part of the narrative: where light comes from, who is lit, who stays in shadow are questions of power and faith.

怎么做How it is done

  • 用深褐或黑铺底，先把暗部画完。
  • 从一侧打光，确定人物和手势的明暗边界。
  • 用少量厚涂提亮最亮处，保留大片暗部。
  • Lay a dark brown or black ground and finish the shadows first.
  • Light from one side to fix the boundary of figures and gestures.
  • Use sparse impasto at the brightest points, keeping large dark areas.
