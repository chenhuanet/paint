伦勃朗Rembrandt van Rijn

伦勃朗是光影的心理学家。他画肖像、圣经、自画像和城市民兵，最擅长的不是“照亮”，而是让光从暗处慢慢渗出来。他晚年破产、丧亲，自画像却越来越安静：不表演，只把一张疲惫的脸放在画布上。Rembrandt is a psychologist of light. He painted portraits, Bible scenes, self-portraits and militia groups; his gift is not 'lighting' but letting light seep out of darkness. In later life, bankrupt and bereaved, his self-portraits become quieter: no performance, only a tired face on canvas.

小传Biography

《夜巡》把群像画成一场正在散开的行动：队长举手，副官握矛，鼓手、枪手和小孩从阴影里涌出。没有人站成一排，光也不平均。伦勃朗让“集体”变成一群各自有事的个体。

The Night Watch turns a group portrait into an action already dispersing: the captain raises his hand, the lieutenant grips a spear, drummers, musketeers and a child emerge from shadow. No one stands in a row, light is uneven. Rembrandt turns a 'group' into individuals each with something to do.

看暗部：里面藏着谁？看晚年自画像：他还在表演吗？Read the shadows: who hides there? Read the late self-portrait: is he still performing?
