战后与当代：抽象、图像与全球Postwar and Contemporary: Abstraction, Image, Global

在战争、印刷、数字和全球市场之后，绘画如何继续作证？After war, print, digital media and the global market, how does painting continue to bear witness?
1945–20261945–2026第 18 章Chapter 18
绘画史Painting History / 第 18 章Chapter 18
这一章必须从“收藏缺口”开始：本馆藏的战后再绘画很少，贝克曼、博恩和赫因斯布鲁克只能提供几个入口，不能假装覆盖全球。因此我们把重点放在三种继续作证的方式：身体与神话（贝克曼）、日常的重建（博恩）、复制与媒介（赫因斯布鲁克）。This chapter must begin with an acquisition gap: the archive holds very few postwar paintings. Beckmann, Bone and Heijnsbroek offer entries, not global coverage. We therefore focus on three ways painting continues to testify: body and myth (Beckmann), everyday reconstruction (Bone), reproduction and medium (Heijnsbroek).

身体与神话：贝克曼的战后舞台Body and myth: Beckmann's postwar stage

贝克曼的《珀耳修斯的最后一役》把希腊英雄画成一个被困住的男人，周围是变形的身体和暗色空间。神话不再是胜利故事，而是暴力的容器。画面挤、重、不安，正好对应流亡和战争后的欧洲。

Beckmann's Perseus's Last Duty turns the Greek hero into a trapped man among distorted bodies and a dark space. Myth is no longer a victory story but a container for violence. The picture is crowded, heavy and uneasy, matching exile and postwar Europe.

日常的重建：博恩的胜利日Reconstructing the everyday: Bone's Victory Day

博恩的《威茅斯湾，胜利日庆典》用水彩、石墨和钢笔把港口、船、旗帜和人群画在一起。它不宣称战争结束是英雄史诗，而是记录一个可辨认的日常重新回来：船还在，海还在，人又聚到岸边。战后绘画不一定抽象，也可以选择恢复观看的秩序。

Bone's Weymouth Bay, Victory Day uses watercolour, graphite and pen to draw harbour, boats, flags and crowd together. It does not claim the war's end as heroic epic; it records a recognisable everyday returning: boats still there, sea still there, people gathering again at the shore. Postwar painting need not be abstract; it can choose to restore an order of looking.

复制与媒介：赫因斯布鲁克的屏幕Reproduction and medium: Heijnsbroek's screen

赫因斯布鲁克的《框》和《交错 3》把颜色、边线和层叠的平面做成图像。它们既像绘画，也像屏幕上的图形：可以被缩放、复制、上传。这里的“当代”不是宏大叙事，而是绘画如何在数字复制中被重新看见。

Heijnsbroek's Frame and Crosslings 3 turn colour, edge and layered planes into images. They look like paintings and like graphics on a screen: scalable, reproducible, uploadable. The 'contemporary' here is not a grand narrative but how painting is seen again inside digital reproduction.

怎么看How to look

  • 先看身体：它是英雄、受害者，还是商品？
  • 再看日常：港口、船和人群是否重新变得可辨认？
  • 最后看复制：这张图像原本给谁看，在哪里被看？
  • Start with the body: hero, victim, or commodity?
  • Then read the everyday: are harbour, boats and crowd recognisable again?
  • Finally read reproduction: for whom was this image made, and where was it seen?
