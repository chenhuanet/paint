阿尔布雷希特·丢勒Albrecht Dürer

丢勒是北方文艺复兴的“全能型”艺术家：画家、版画家、理论家。他把意大利的透视和人体比例带回纽伦堡，也把北方细节的锋利保留下来。他的自画像把自己画成正面、直视、穿皮草的绅士，签上名字和年份，像在宣告画家的尊严。Dürer is the complete Northern Renaissance artist: painter, printmaker, theorist. He brought Italian perspective and human proportion to Nuremberg while keeping the sharpness of Northern detail. His *Self-Portrait* shows him frontal, direct, in fur, signed and dated, announcing the dignity of the painter.

小传Biography

丢勒的版画比油画传播得更远：《启示录》木刻、《骑士、死神与魔鬼》铜版，线条细得像神经。他相信艺术家不是工匠，而是能测量世界、也能解释世界的人。

Dürer's prints travelled farther than his paintings: the Apocalypse woodcuts, Knight, Death and the Devil engraving, lines fine as nerves. He believed the artist was not a craftsman but someone who could measure and interpret the world.
