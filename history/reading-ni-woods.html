《渔庄秋霁图》Woods and Valleys of Mount Yu

空亭在等谁？Whom is the empty pavilion waiting for?
1355 · 倪瓒1355 · Ni Zan精读Close reading
绘画史Painting History / 精读Close readings
一件作品，一个问题；先看，再判断。One work, one question; look first, judge later.

图片为该作品在本站的主图；元数据见下方作品卡。The image is the primary image for this work; metadata follows in the work card.

问题Question

倪瓒画几棵树、一个空亭、一片水、一段远山。他不画人，也不画云；留白就是水与天。空亭像一个邀请，也像一个拒绝。

Ni Zan paints a few trees, an empty pavilion, a stretch of water, a distant ridge. He paints no people and no clouds; blank silk is water and sky. The empty pavilion is an invitation and a refusal.

语境Context

元代纸本水墨立轴，藏于大都会艺术博物馆。倪瓒晚年散尽家财，泛舟太湖。

A Yuan hanging scroll in ink on paper, held by the Metropolitan Museum of Art. Ni Zan dispersed his estate in later life and lived on a boat on Lake Tai.

怎么看How to look

  • 先看亭子：它有门吗？
  • 再看树：它们为什么这么瘦？
  • 最后看远山：它真的存在吗？
  • Start with the pavilion: does it have a door?
  • Then read the trees: why so thin?
  • Finally read the distant ridge: is it really there?

为什么重要Why it matters

它把“拒绝”画成一种可以被进入的空间。

It paints refusal as a space one can enter.
