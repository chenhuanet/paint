《溪山行旅图》Travelers Among Mountains and Streams

山如何让人变小？How does a mountain make a person small?
约1025 · 范宽ca.1025 · Fan Kuan精读Close reading
绘画史Painting History / 精读Close readings
一件作品，一个问题；先看，再判断。One work, one question; look first, judge later.

图片为该作品在本站的主图；元数据见下方作品卡。The image is the primary image for this work; metadata follows in the work card.

问题Question

范宽把主峰放在画面中央，几乎占满上半幅；行旅、驴队和寺庙小到像标点。这不是“人与自然和谐”，而是人在宇宙尺度前的渺小。

Fan Kuan places the main peak at the centre, filling almost the upper half; travelers, donkeys and a temple are as small as punctuation. This is not harmony with nature but human smallness before cosmic scale.

语境Context

北宋山水立轴，绢本水墨，藏于台北故宫博物院。范宽长期隐居终南山、太华山，主张“师造化”。

A Northern Song hanging scroll in ink on silk, held by the National Palace Museum, Taipei. Fan Kuan lived for years in the Zhongnan and Taihua mountains and argued for learning from nature.

怎么看How to look

  • 先看主峰：它为什么压在正中？
  • 再看皴笔：山是石壁还是土坡？
  • 最后看人：你能找到几个？
  • Start with the main peak: why is it pressed into the centre?
  • Then read texture strokes: stone wall or earthen slope?
  • Finally find the people: how many can you see?

为什么重要Why it matters

它定义了北宋山水的尺度：中心有重量，边缘有呼吸。

It defines Northern Song landscape scale: the centre has weight, the edges breathe.
