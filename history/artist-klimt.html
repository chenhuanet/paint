古斯塔夫·克里姆特Gustav Klimt

克里姆特是维也纳分离派的领袖，也是新艺术运动最华丽的画家。他用金箔、马赛克、曲线和花卉把身体包进装饰表面；美得几乎过分，也因此让人不安。Klimt led the Vienna Secession and is the most ornate painter of Art Nouveau. He wraps bodies in gold leaf, mosaic, curves and flowers; beauty becomes almost excessive, and therefore unsettling.

小传Biography

《吻》里，两个身体被金色斗篷包成一个菱形，男人和女人的图案不同：男人是黑白方块，女人是彩色圆点。脸和手是唯一写实的部分，其余都变成图案。克里姆特把爱情画成一枚圣物盒，也画成一场视觉麻醉。

In The Kiss, two bodies are enclosed in a golden cloak shaped like a diamond; the man's pattern is black-and-white rectangles, the woman's coloured circles. Faces and hands are the only realistic parts; the rest becomes pattern. Klimt turns love into a reliquary and a visual anaesthetic.
