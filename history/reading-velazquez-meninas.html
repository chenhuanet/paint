《宫娥》Las Meninas

谁在看谁？Who is looking at whom?
1656 · 迭戈·委拉斯开兹1656 · Diego Velázquez精读Close reading
绘画史Painting History / 精读Close readings
一件作品，一个问题；先看，再判断。One work, one question; look first, judge later.

图片为该作品在本站的主图；元数据见下方作品卡。The image is the primary image for this work; metadata follows in the work card.

问题Question

委拉斯开兹把画家、镜子、门、狗、公主和国王夫妇放进同一张画。画面里的镜子反射出画布，画外的观者也被卷进观看链：这不是肖像，而是一套关于“谁有权被看见”的装置。

Velázquez puts painter, mirror, door, dog, princess and the king and queen into one picture. The mirror reflects the canvas, and the viewer outside is drawn into the chain of looking: this is not a portrait but an apparatus about who has the right to be seen.

语境Context

1656年布面油画，藏于马德里普拉多博物馆。画面中的画家是委拉斯开兹本人，镜子反射出国王腓力四世与王后。

Oil on canvas, 1656, held by the Museo del Prado, Madrid. The painter in the picture is Velázquez himself; the mirror reflects King Philip IV and the queen.

怎么看How to look

  • 先找镜子：它反射了什么？
  • 再看画家：他在画哪一块画布？
  • 最后看门：谁站在光里？
  • Find the mirror: what does it reflect?
  • Then read the painter: which canvas is he painting?
  • Finally read the door: who stands in the light?

为什么重要Why it matters

它把绘画本身变成权力和观看的现场。

It turns painting itself into a site of power and looking.
