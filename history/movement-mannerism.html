矫饰主义Mannerism

矫饰主义是盛期文艺复兴的“过度自觉”。当比例、透视和人体都被解决之后，画家开始故意拉长、扭曲、堆叠，让画面变得优雅而不安。帕米贾尼诺把圣母的脖子拉长，埃尔·格列柯把托莱多画成一场闪电，颜色变得发疯，空间变得不稳定。它是对“完美”的怀疑，也是现代绘画第一次公开表演“风格”。Mannerism is the 'over-consciousness' of the High Renaissance. Once proportion, perspective and the body had been solved, painters began to stretch, twist and pile up forms, making the picture elegant and unsettled. Parmigianino lengthens the Virgin's neck, El Greco paints Toledo as lightning, colour goes wild and space becomes unstable. It is doubt about 'perfection', and the first time modern painting openly performs 'style'.

绘画史Painting History / 流派Movements
先看三个特征，再看三件作品；最后把它放回它所在的章节。Start with three traits, then three works; finally put it back into its chapter.

三个特征Three traits

  • 比例被故意破坏：长颈、长手指、过小的头，优雅变成一种变形。
  • 颜色变得不自然：酸绿、冷粉、铅灰，颜色不再服从物体。
  • 空间被压扁：人物像在舞台上，背景像一块幕布。
  • Proportion deliberately broken: long necks, long fingers, small heads; elegance becomes distortion.
  • Colour turns artificial: acid green, cool pink, lead grey; colour no longer obeys objects.
  • Space is flattened: figures seem on stage, background like a backdrop.

怎么看How to look

  • 找“不对”：哪一处比例让你不舒服？
  • 看颜色：它是否像打翻的调色盘，而不是自然光？
  • 看身体：它是被拉长、扭转，还是被拆成零件？
  • Find the 'wrong': which proportion makes you uncomfortable?
  • Read colour: does it look like a spilled palette rather than natural light?
  • Read the body: stretched, twisted or dismantled?
