居斯塔夫·库尔贝Gustave Courbet

库尔贝是现实主义的宣言者。他拒绝画天使和历史英雄，只画他看得见的人：工人、农民、妓女、朋友、赞助人。他说“我没有画天使，因为我从未见过”。Courbet is the manifestor of Realism. He refuses angels and historical heroes, painting only people he can see: workers, peasants, a prostitute, friends, patrons. He said he had never painted an angel because he had never seen one.

小传Biography

《画室》把自己画在中央，左边是穷人、工人、失业者，右边是朋友、赞助人和知识分子。画面不是神话，而是一张社会关系图；画家坐在中间，像记录者，也像裁判。

The Painter's Studio places him at the center; on the left the poor, workers and unemployed, on the right friends, patrons and intellectuals. The picture is not myth but a social map; the painter sits in the middle, recorder and judge at once.

看左右：谁在左边，谁在右边？看画家：他站在哪一边？Read left and right: who is on each side? Read the painter: which side does he stand on?
