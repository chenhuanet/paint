《吻》The Kiss

两个人为什么变成一个图案？Why do two people become one pattern?
1907–1908 · 古斯塔夫·克里姆特1907–1908 · Gustav Klimt精读Close reading
绘画史Painting History / 精读Close readings
一件作品，一个问题；先看，再判断。One work, one question; look first, judge later.

图片为该作品在本站的主图；元数据见下方作品卡。The image is the primary image for this work; metadata follows in the work card.

问题Question

克里姆特把一对拥抱的人裹进金箔、花毯和几何图案，身体几乎消失在装饰里。装饰不是遮住身体，而是把身体变成一种可以被触摸的表面。

Klimt wraps an embracing couple in gold, flower carpet and geometric pattern until the body almost disappears into decoration. Decoration does not hide the body; it turns the body into a surface one could touch.

语境Context

1908年布面油画，藏于维也纳美景宫。克里姆特是维也纳分离派的核心人物。

Oil on canvas, 1908, held by the Belvedere, Vienna. Klimt was central to the Vienna Secession.

怎么看How to look

  • 先看脸：谁在吻谁？
  • 再看金箔：它让身体更近还是更远？
  • 最后看图案：哪些是衣服，哪些是地面？
  • Read the faces: who kisses whom?
  • Then read gold: does it bring the body closer or push it away?
  • Finally read pattern: what is cloth, what is ground?

为什么重要Why it matters

它把装饰变成情感的语言，而不是简单的华丽。

It makes decoration a language of feeling rather than simple glitter.
