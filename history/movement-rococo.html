洛可可Rococo

洛可可是十八世纪宫廷和城市客厅的艺术：曲线、粉色、金色、丝绸、秋千、花园和爱情游戏。它常被骂成轻浮，但它其实在发明一种新的观看者：有闲暇、有隐私、有好奇心的市民。华托的忧郁、弗拉戈纳尔的顽皮、夏尔丹的物，都是同一个世纪的不同侧面。Rococo is the art of the eighteenth-century court and drawing room: curves, pink, gold, silk, swings, gardens and love games. It is often dismissed as frivolous, but it invents a new viewer: a citizen with leisure, privacy and curiosity. Watteau's melancholy, Fragonard's mischief and Chardin's objects are different faces of the same century.

绘画史Painting History / 流派Movements
先看三个特征，再看三件作品；最后把它放回它所在的章节。Start with three traits, then three works; finally put it back into its chapter.

三个特征Three traits

  • 曲线与不对称：画面像一件家具或一段音乐，轻、转、停。
  • 私密空间：客厅、卧室、花园，而不是教堂或广场。
  • 享乐与时间：爱情、游戏、音乐、下午茶，都带着“会结束”的阴影。
  • Curve and asymmetry: the picture is like furniture or music — light, turning, pausing.
  • Private space: drawing room, bedroom, garden, not church or square.
  • Pleasure and time: love, games, music and tea all carry the shadow of ending.

怎么看How to look

  • 看空间：这是室内还是花园？它有多私密？
  • 看动作：人物在玩耍、犹豫，还是偷看？
  • 看物：丝绸、瓷器、花、秋千，哪一件在替人物说话？
  • Read the space: interior or garden, and how private is it?
  • Read the action: playing, hesitating or peeking?
  • Read the objects: silk, porcelain, flowers, swing — which one speaks for the figures?
