琳派Rinpa

琳派是日本江户时代最具装饰性的绘画传统，名字来自尾形光琳。它把金银箔、矿物颜料、垂水（tarashikomi）和书法放在同一张纸上，让图案、文字和材料互相渗透。琳派不追求自然主义的深度，它追求平面上的节奏。Rinpa is the most decorative painting tradition of Edo Japan, named after Ogata Kōrin. It places gold and silver foil, mineral pigments, tarashikomi and calligraphy on one sheet, letting pattern, text and material permeate one another. Rinpa does not seek naturalistic depth but rhythm on a flat plane.

三个特征Three traits

  • 材料即图像：金箔、银泥、矿物色不是装饰，而是画面结构。
  • 图案化自然：花、水、云被压成重复的形，却不失去生命。
  • 书与画同纸：和歌、题字和图像共享一个版面。
  • Material is image: gold, silver and mineral colour are structure, not decoration.
  • Patterned nature: flowers, water and clouds are reduced to repeated shapes without losing life.
  • Text and image share paper: waka, inscription and design occupy one field.

怎么看How to look

  • 先看材料的反光：哪里是金，哪里是色？
  • 再看图案的重复：它像自然，还是像布纹？
  • 最后看字与画：谁先吸引你的眼睛？
  • Start with reflected material: where is gold, where pigment?
  • Then read repetition: does it look like nature, or fabric?
  • Finally read text and image: which catches your eye first?
