马远Ma Yuan

马远是南宋画院的代表，出身绘画世家，与夏圭并称“马夏”。他擅长把景物推到画面一角，留下大片空白，让观者自己补上山、水、雾和风。这种“边角构图”不是省事，而是把观看变成一次选择：你看见的越少，越要判断空白里发生了什么。Ma Yuan represents the Southern Song academy. From a painting family, he is paired with Xia Gui as 'Ma-Xia'. He pushes scenery into a corner and leaves large areas blank, asking viewers to complete the mountain, water, mist and wind themselves. This 'one-corner composition' is not economy but a choice: the less you see, the more you must judge what the blank contains.

小传Biography

《松下观鹿》里，松树从左上斜出，鹿在下方饮水，中间的水面几乎空着；《月光下小酌》把人物压到一角，月光用淡墨烘出。马远的空白不是没有，而是让时间、空气和距离一起进来。

In Watching the Deer by a Pine-Shaded Stream, the pine slants in from upper left, deer drink below, and the water between them is almost empty; in Drinking in the Moonlight, figures are pushed to a corner and moonlight is washed in pale ink. Ma's blank is not absence; it lets time, air and distance enter.

先看空白：它是什么？再看边角：谁被挤出画面？Read the blank first: what is it? Then read the corner: who has been pushed out?
