TECHNIQUE / 媒介TECHNIQUE / Medium

蛋彩Tempera

蛋彩用蛋黄或全蛋调颜料，干得快、颜色清亮，适合在木板上做细密的层层描绘。中世纪和早期文艺复兴的祭坛画大多用蛋彩；它的表面不像油画那样厚，而像一层发光的皮肤。Tempera binds pigment with egg yolk or whole egg. It dries quickly, keeps colour clear, and suits fine layered work on panel. Medieval and Early Renaissance altarpieces mostly use tempera; its surface is not thick like oil but like luminous skin.

怎么做How it is done

  • 把颜料粉与蛋液调成乳液。
  • 在涂过石膏底的木板上薄薄一层层画。
  • 用金箔和蛋彩结合，制造圣像的光。
  • Mix pigment powder with egg emulsion.
  • Paint thin layers on a gessoed panel.
  • Combine gold leaf with tempera to make the light of an icon.
