列奥纳多·达·芬奇Leonardo da Vinci

达·芬奇是画家、解剖学家、工程师和笔记狂。他相信绘画是一种研究自然的方法：光怎样落在脸上，水怎样流动，肌肉怎样牵引手指。他的“晕涂法”让轮廓消失在空气里，人物因此像从背景中浮现。Leonardo was painter, anatomist, engineer and obsessive note-taker. He believed painting was a way of studying nature: how light falls on a face, how water flows, how muscle pulls a finger. His sfumato lets contour vanish into air, so figures seem to emerge from the background.

小传Biography

《最后的晚餐》把十二个门徒分成四组，每组的动作都像涟漪一样从基督的话向外扩散；《蒙娜丽莎》的背景左右地平线不一致，微笑因此永远停在一半。达·芬奇不给我们答案，他给我们一个可以反复观看的谜。

The Last Supper divides the apostles into four groups, each gesture rippling outward from Christ's words; in the Mona Lisa, the horizon is inconsistent left and right, so the smile stays suspended. Leonardo does not give answers; he gives a puzzle that can be looked at again and again.

看边缘：它是不是在消失？看微笑：它为什么不能确定？Read the edge: is it disappearing? Read the smile: why can't it be fixed?
