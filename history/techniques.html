十二种技法与媒介Twelve techniques and media

技法不是配方，而是决定：先画什么、后画什么、哪里留白、哪里堆厚。它决定一张画能说什么。Technique is not a recipe but a set of decisions: what comes first, what waits, where to leave blank, where to build thick. It decides what a picture can say.
12 种12 techniques媒介 + 技法Media + methods

湿壁画Fresco

媒介Medium

湿壁画是在石灰墙未干时把矿物颜料画上去，颜料与墙面一起固化。它不能反复修改，画家必须一次决定构图、颜色和明暗；因此湿壁画总是带着一种“不可撤回”的果断。Fresco is painted onto wet lime plaster with mineral pigments; the colour sets with the wall. It cannot be easily revised, so the painter must decide composition, colour and modelling in one go. Fresco therefore carries an irrevocable decisiveness.

蛋彩Tempera

媒介Medium

蛋彩用蛋黄或全蛋调颜料，干得快、颜色清亮，适合在木板上做细密的层层描绘。中世纪和早期文艺复兴的祭坛画大多用蛋彩；它的表面不像油画那样厚，而像一层发光的皮肤。Tempera binds pigment with egg yolk or whole egg. It dries quickly, keeps colour clear, and suits fine layered work on panel. Medieval and Early Renaissance altarpieces mostly use tempera; its surface is not thick like oil but like luminous skin.

油画Oil painting

媒介Medium

油画用油调和颜料，干得慢，可以反复罩染、修改、堆厚。它既能画出玻璃般透明的皮肤，也能画出厚到投下阴影的黄色。北方在十五世纪把它推向极致，威尼斯则把它变成颜色的戏剧。Oil painting binds pigment with oil. It dries slowly, allowing repeated glazing, revision and thick build-up. It can paint glassy skin and yellow thick enough to cast a shadow. The North pushed it to an extreme in the fifteenth century; Venice turned it into a drama of colour.

水彩Watercolour

媒介Medium

水彩用透明颜料和水，纸的白就是画面最亮的光。它不能轻易覆盖，因此画家要提前想好哪里留白、哪里叠色。水彩最适合记录天气、雾、海和一次稍纵即逝的观察。Watercolour uses transparent pigment and water; the white of the paper is the brightest light. It cannot easily be overpainted, so the painter must plan where to leave white and where to layer. It suits weather, mist, sea and a fleeting observation.

木版画Woodblock print

媒介Medium

木版画把画、刻、印分成不同工种：画师起稿，雕版师沿轮廓刻，印刷师一版一色。它让图像可以复制、传播、消费，也让线条变得清楚、平面、有力。Woodblock printing divides design, carving and printing: the artist draws, the carver cuts along contours, the printer prints one block per colour. It makes images reproducible, circulable and consumable, and makes line clear, flat and strong.

水墨Ink painting

媒介Medium

水墨用毛笔、墨、水和纸绢。墨分五色：焦、浓、重、淡、清；水让同一笔墨在纸上产生无穷变化。水墨不追求覆盖，而追求“留”和“让”：哪里下笔，哪里空白，都是判断。Ink painting uses brush, ink, water and paper or silk. Ink has five tones: burnt, dense, heavy, light, clear; water makes a single stroke infinitely variable. Ink does not seek coverage but 'leaving' and 'yielding': where to put the brush and where to leave blank are both judgments.

线性透视Linear perspective

技法Technique

线性透视用一条地平线和一个消失点，让平行线在远处汇聚，从而在平面上制造可测量的深度。它不只是技术，也是一种世界观：世界有一个可以被眼睛计算的秩序。Linear perspective uses a horizon and a vanishing point so parallel lines converge in the distance, creating measurable depth on a flat surface. It is not only technique but a worldview: the world has an order the eye can calculate.

空气透视Aerial perspective

技法Technique

空气透视让远处的物体变淡、变蓝、变模糊，因为空气层厚了。它不靠线条，而靠颜色和对比度制造距离。山、雾、海、雪，都最适合用空气透视来画。Aerial perspective makes distant objects lighter, bluer and softer because the layer of air is thicker. It creates distance through colour and contrast rather than line. Mountains, mist, sea and snow are its natural subjects.

明暗对照Chiaroscuro

技法Technique

明暗对照用强烈的亮部和暗部塑造体积与戏剧。它不只是“打光”，而是让光成为叙事角色：谁被照亮，谁留在黑暗里，都是在做判断。Chiaroscuro uses strong light and dark to model volume and drama. It is not merely 'lighting' but makes light a narrative character: who is illuminated and who remains in darkness is a judgment.

罩染Glazing

技法Technique

罩染是在已经干的色层上涂一层透明色，让光穿过上层、从底层反射回来。它像给画面戴上一层有色玻璃：皮肤会发光，阴影会变深，颜色会变得比直接调出来的更复杂。Glazing applies a transparent colour over a dry layer so light passes through and reflects from below. It is like placing coloured glass over the picture: skin glows, shadows deepen, colour becomes more complex than direct mixing.

厚涂Impasto

技法Technique

厚涂把颜料堆到画布上，留下笔触的厚度和阴影。它让绘画变成可触摸的物体：你能看见画家手腕的速度、用力的方向，甚至颜料的重量。Impasto piles paint on the canvas, leaving the thickness and shadow of the stroke. It makes painting a tactile object: you see the speed of the wrist, the direction of pressure, even the weight of paint.

拼贴/照相蒙太奇Collage / Photomontage

技法Technique

拼贴把现成的纸片、报纸、照片、布料贴到画面上，让“真实材料”进入绘画。它打破了绘画必须“手绘”的规则，也让大众印刷品成为艺术语言。照相蒙太奇则把照片剪开、重组，制造新的叙事。Collage pastes found paper, newspaper, photographs and fabric onto the picture, letting 'real material' enter painting. It breaks the rule that painting must be hand-made and makes mass-printed imagery an artistic language. Photomontage cuts and reassembles photographs to create new narratives.
