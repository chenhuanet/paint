《柳树与喜鹊》Willow and Magpie

几笔墨如何留下鸟和风？How do a few strokes of ink leave a bird and wind?
mid-1200s · 法常 牧谿mid-1200s · Fachang Muqi精读Close reading
绘画史Painting History / 精读Close readings
一件作品，一个问题；先看，再判断。One work, one question; look first, judge later.

图片为该作品在本站的主图；元数据见下方作品卡。The image is the primary image for this work; metadata follows in the work card.

问题Question

牧溪用极少的墨、极快的笔，把柳枝、喜鹊和风留在纸上。他不追求形似，而追求对象在瞬间留下的气场。

Muqi uses very little ink and very fast brushwork to keep willow branch, magpie and wind on paper. He does not seek likeness but the aura an object leaves in an instant.

语境Context

南宋禅僧画，纸本水墨，藏于克利夫兰艺术博物馆。牧溪法名法常，活动于杭州一带。

A Southern Song Chan painting in ink on paper, held by the Cleveland Museum of Art. Muqi's dharma name was Fachang; he was active around Hangzhou.

怎么看How to look

  • 先看喜鹊：它要飞走还是刚落下？
  • 再看柳枝：哪一笔是风？
  • 最后看空白：它让画面变轻还是变空？
  • Start with the magpie: leaving or just landed?
  • Then read the willow: which stroke is wind?
  • Finally read the blank: lighter, or emptier?

为什么重要Why it matters

它证明水墨可以比工笔更直接地记录生命。

It proves ink can record life more directly than fine-line painting.
