日本与南亚：装饰、细密与印刷Japan and South Asia: Decoration, Miniature, Print

当图像进入书页、屏风、和纸与木版，绘画的“深度”发生了什么？When images enter books, screens, washi and woodblocks, what happens to pictorial depth?
1600–19001600–1900第 16 章Chapter 16
绘画史Painting History / 第 16 章Chapter 16
这一章不把日本和南亚当成欧洲绘画的“他者”，而是看三种不同的图像经济：琳派把金银和图案放上纸，浮世绘把城市娱乐变成可复制的版画，莫卧儿与波斯细密画把诗、宫廷和花园压进手抄本的页面。它们都不追求单点透视，却各自建立了严格的观看秩序。This chapter does not treat Japan and South Asia as Europe's 'other'. It looks at three image economies: Rinpa puts gold and pattern on paper; ukiyo-e turns urban entertainment into reproducible prints; Mughal and Persian miniature press poetry, court and garden into manuscript pages. None seeks single-point perspective, yet each builds a strict order of looking.

材料先说话：金、纸、木版Material speaks first: gold, paper, woodblock

宗达的和歌卡把海滨松树压成极简的线面，纸、墨和图案一起构成版面；琳派的“装饰”不是附加，而是材料本身在发光。浮世绘则把颜色拆成一块块木版，每一版都要对准纸上的mark，错一点，脸就歪。

细密画更极端：金箔、矿物色和极细的笔线让页面变成一件可以被手指触摸的物体。观看距离越近，权力和诗的细节越多。

Sōtatsu's poem-card reduces pines on a beach to a few lines and planes; paper, ink and pattern together form the page. Rinpa 'decoration' is not additive; the material itself glows. Ukiyo-e separates colour into woodblocks, each must register on the paper; a slip and the face goes wrong.

Miniature is more extreme: gold, mineral colour and fine line make the page an object one could touch. The closer you look, the more details of power and poetry appear.

可复制的图像，改变了“原作”Reproducible images change the 'original'

浮世绘不是一幅画，而是一个版数；同一构图可以印很多张，颜色还会因版次和纸张而变。莫卧儿细密画常是手抄本的一页，文字、边框和图像共同决定阅读顺序。两者都提醒我们：绘画史不能只数“唯一原作”。

An ukiyo-e print is not one painting but an edition; the same composition can be printed many times, and colour shifts with impression and paper. A Mughal miniature is often one page in a manuscript; text, frame and image together determine reading order. Both remind us that painting history cannot count only 'unique originals'.

怎么看How to look

  • 先看材料：金、纸、木版还是绢？
  • 再看版面：文字和图像谁给谁让路？
  • 最后看复制：它是唯一原作，还是一个版次？
  • Start with material: gold, paper, woodblock or silk?
  • Then read the page: which yields to which, text or image?
  • Finally read reproduction: unique original, or an edition?
