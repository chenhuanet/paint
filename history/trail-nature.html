自然Nature

自然在画里有时是神的书，有时是人的对立面，有时只是光和天气。这条小径从北宋主峰走到浪漫主义的雾海。Nature in painting is sometimes God's book, sometimes man's opposite, sometimes only light and weather. This trail moves from a Northern Song peak to a Romantic sea of fog.

沿途Along the trail

约1025 · 范宽ca.1025 · Fan Kuan
《溪山行旅图》Travelers Among Mountains and Streams

山是中心，人是标点。Mountain is the center; people are punctuation.

1347–1350 · 黄公望1347–1350 · Huang Gongwang
《富春山居图》Dwelling in the Fuchun Mountains

自然是可以居住的时间，不是可征服的地图。Nature is inhabitable time, not a map to conquer.

约1818 · 卡斯帕·大卫·弗里德里希ca.1818 · Caspar David Friedrich
《雾海上的漫游者》Wanderer above the Sea of Fog

背影面对雾海，自然大过人。A back faces a sea of fog; nature is larger than man.

1839 · 约瑟夫·马洛德·威廉·特纳1839 · J. M. W. Turner
《被拖去解体的无畏号》The Fighting Temeraire

自然不是背景，它是把旧时代吞掉的光。Nature is not backdrop; it is the light that swallows an old era.

1889 · 文森特·梵高1889 · Vincent van Gogh
《星月夜》The Starry Night

夜空不是风景，是画家内心的运动。The night sky is not scenery but the painter's inner motion.

走完之后After the walk

自然画最终画的是人如何面对比自己大的东西。Nature painting ultimately paints how humans face something larger than themselves.
