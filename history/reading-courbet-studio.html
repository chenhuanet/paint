《画室》The Painter's Studio

画室为什么像一张社会地图？Why does the studio look like a social map?
1855 · 居斯塔夫·库尔贝1855 · Gustave Courbet精读Close reading
绘画史Painting History / 精读Close readings
一件作品，一个问题；先看，再判断。One work, one question; look first, judge later.

图片为该作品在本站的主图；元数据见下方作品卡。The image is the primary image for this work; metadata follows in the work card.

问题Question

库尔贝把自己放在画室中央，左边是普通人和被社会抛弃者，右边是朋友、赞助人和知识分子。画面不是聚会，而是一张阶级分布图；画家在中间，正在画风景。

Courbet places himself at the centre of the studio: on the left ordinary people and the socially excluded, on the right friends, patrons and intellectuals. The picture is not a party but a map of class; the painter is in the middle, painting a landscape.

语境Context

1855年布面油画，藏于巴黎奥赛博物馆。作品副标题为“我七年艺术与道德生活的真实寓言”。

Oil on canvas, 1855, held by the Musée d'Orsay, Paris. Its subtitle is 'A real allegory summing up seven years of my artistic and moral life'.

怎么看How to look

  • 先看画家：他在画什么？
  • 再看左边：谁被放在边缘？
  • 最后看右边：谁在看画家？
  • Read the painter: what is he painting?
  • Then read the left: who is placed at the edge?
  • Finally read the right: who watches the painter?

为什么重要Why it matters

它把现实主义从“画穷人”升级为“画社会关系”。

It upgrades Realism from painting the poor to painting social relations.
