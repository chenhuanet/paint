印象主义Impressionism

印象主义把画架搬到户外，也把“完成”搬到户外。它画光、天气、都市瞬间和日常娱乐：火车站、林荫大道、舞厅、阳台、摇篮。颜色不再描述物体，而是记录光的关系；轮廓让位给笔触，构图让位给裁切。Impressionism moves the easel outdoors and 'finished' outdoors with it. It paints light, weather, urban moments and everyday leisure: railway stations, boulevards, dance halls, balconies, cradles. Colour no longer describes objects but records relationships of light; contour gives way to touch, composition to cropping.

先看三个特征，再看三件作品；最后把它放回它所在的章节。Start with three traits, then three works; finally put it back into its chapter.

三个特征Three traits

  • 光优先：颜色由光的冷暖决定，而不是物体的固有色。
  • 瞬间：人物在动作中，画面像一次快照。
  • 城市中产：舞会、咖啡馆、阳台、郊游，是新的观众和新的题材。
  • Light first: colour is decided by warm and cool light, not local colour.
  • Instant: figures are in motion; the picture feels like a snapshot.
  • Urban middle class: dance, café, balcony and outing are new audiences and subjects.

怎么看How to look

  • 退后：不要找轮廓，找颜色关系。
  • 看时间：这是几点？光从哪来？
  • 看裁切：谁被切掉？为什么？
  • Step back: do not look for contour; look for colour relationships.
  • Read the hour: what time is it, and where does light come from?
  • Read the crop: who is cut off, and why?
