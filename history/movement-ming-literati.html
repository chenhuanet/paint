吴门文人画Wu School Literati Painting

吴门文人画把绘画放回一种生活方式：在苏州的园林、书斋、茶席和朋友圈里，画不是商品，而是唱和、题跋、收藏和教养的一部分。沈周、文徵明、唐寅、仇英把山水、人物、花鸟都变成可以交换的纸上时间。Wu school literati painting returns painting to a way of life: in Suzhou gardens, studios, tea gatherings and circles of friends, a picture is not a commodity but part of poetry exchange, colophon, collecting and education. Shen Zhou, Wen Zhengming, Tang Yin and Qiu Ying turn landscape, figure and flower into exchangeable time on paper.

绘画史Painting History / 流派Movements
先看三个特征，再看三件作品；最后把它放回它所在的章节。Start with three traits, then three works; finally put it back into its chapter.

三个特征Three traits

  • 画与诗、书、印同场：一张画常常是一组文字与图像的合奏。
  • 园林作为画题：可游、可居、可看，比名山大川更日常。
  • 师承可见：仿某家笔意不是抄袭，而是把传统变成可讨论的对话。
  • Painting shares the field with poetry, calligraphy and seals: a picture is often an ensemble of text and image.
  • Garden as subject: visitable, livable, viewable, more everyday than famous mountains.
  • Lineage is visible: 'after the brush idea of X' is not copying but turning tradition into conversation.

怎么看How to look

  • 先读题跋：谁写的，写给谁，和画是什么关系？
  • 再看园林：哪里可以坐，哪里可以看，哪里只是路？
  • 最后看笔法：它像哪位前人，又哪里不像？
  • Read the colophon first: who wrote it, to whom, and how does it relate to the picture?
  • Then read the garden: where can one sit, look, or only pass?
  • Finally read the brush: which predecessor does it resemble, and where does it differ?
