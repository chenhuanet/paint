山水与自我：中国绘画 4—18 世纪Landscape and the Self: Chinese Painting, 4th–18th Century

从佛教石窟的残片到文人长卷，中国绘画把自然、身体与文字放在同一张绢或纸上。这里不按朝代排队，而是看山水如何成为一个人可以走进去的空间。From a Buddhist cave fragment to a scholar's handscroll, Chinese painting puts nature, body and text on the same silk or paper. This exhibition does not queue dynasties; it asks how landscape became a space a person could enter.
