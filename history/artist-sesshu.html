雪舟等杨Sesshū Tōyō

雪舟是日本室町时代的禅僧画家，1468–1469年随遣明使到中国，回到日本后把南宋院体与禅宗水墨改写为更硬的山水。他晚年的《山水长卷》以粗犷的斧劈皴和强烈的空间推进著称。Sesshū was a Zen monk-painter of Muromachi Japan. He travelled to China with a Japanese mission in 1468–1469 and, after returning, rewrote Southern Song academic and Chan ink into a harder landscape. His late *Landscape Scroll* is known for rough axe-cut strokes and strong spatial push.

小传Biography

本页的《鸟语花香》是一幅花鸟题材的绢本设色，笔线细而果断，鸟的姿态和花枝的转折都被压进一个很紧的构图。它提醒我们：雪舟不仅画大山大水，也画很小的生命。

The Birds and Flowers here is a silk colour painting: the line is thin and decisive, and bird posture and flower branches are compressed into a tight composition. It reminds us that Sesshū painted small lives as well as great mountains.

看鸟的转身：它要飞走，还是刚落下？Read the bird's turn: is it leaving, or has it just landed?
