盛期文艺复兴High Renaissance

盛期文艺复兴相信，画面可以同时做到三件事：空间可信、身体完美、意义清楚。达·芬奇用晕涂法让轮廓消失，米开朗基罗让身体变成建筑，拉斐尔把整个知识界安排进一间厅堂，提香则用颜色证明威尼斯不需要佛罗伦萨的素描。它只持续了大约三十年，却成了后来所有“古典”的参照。The High Renaissance believed a picture could do three things at once: make space credible, make the body perfect, make meaning clear. Leonardo dissolves contour with sfumato, Michelangelo turns the body into architecture, Raphael arranges the whole Republic of Letters in one room, and Titian proves with colour that Venice does not need Florentine drawing. It lasted only about thirty years, yet became the reference for everything later called 'classical'.

绘画史Painting History / 流派Movements
先看三个特征，再看三件作品；最后把它放回它所在的章节。Start with three traits, then three works; finally put it back into its chapter.

三个特征Three traits

  • 中心构图：人物被安排在稳定的三角形或圆形里，视线有明确的入口和出口。
  • 理想人体：比例来自古典雕塑，但被呼吸、肌肉和目光重新激活。
  • 知识进入画面：神学、哲学、解剖、透视被放在同一个视觉秩序里。
  • Central composition: figures are arranged in stable triangles or circles, with clear entry and exit for the eye.
  • Ideal body: proportion comes from classical sculpture but is reactivated by breath, muscle and gaze.
  • Knowledge enters the picture: theology, philosophy, anatomy and perspective share one visual order.

怎么看How to look

  • 找中心：画面的视觉重心在哪里？它是人、手势，还是一道光？
  • 看比例：身体是否“太完美”？完美是否在掩饰什么？
  • 看边缘：轮廓是清楚的，还是被晕涂吞掉？
  • Find the center: is the visual weight a person, a gesture or a shaft of light?
  • Read proportion: is the body 'too perfect', and what does perfection hide?
  • Read the edge: is contour clear, or swallowed by sfumato?
