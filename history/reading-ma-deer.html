《松下观鹿》Watching the Deer by a Pine Shaded Stream

空白如何变成水和雾？How does blank space become water and mist?
1127–1279 · 馬遠1127–1279 · Ma Yuan精读Close reading
绘画史Painting History / 精读Close readings
一件作品，一个问题；先看，再判断。One work, one question; look first, judge later.

图片为该作品在本站的主图；元数据见下方作品卡。The image is the primary image for this work; metadata follows in the work card.

问题Question

马远把松树从左上斜出，鹿在下方饮水，中间的水面几乎空着。空白不是没画，而是让时间、空气和距离一起进来。

Ma Yuan slants the pine in from upper left, deer drink below, and the water between them is almost empty. The blank is not absence; it lets time, air and distance enter.

语境Context

南宋院体绢本设色，藏于克利夫兰艺术博物馆。马远与夏圭并称“马夏”，以边角构图著称。

A Southern Song academic painting in ink and colour on silk, held by the Cleveland Museum of Art. Ma Yuan and Xia Gui are paired as 'Ma-Xia', known for one-corner composition.

怎么看How to look

  • 先看空白：它是水、雾，还是天？
  • 再看鹿：它在喝，还是在听？
  • 最后看松：它把画面压向哪边？
  • Read the blank: water, mist, or sky?
  • Then read the deer: drinking, or listening?
  • Finally read the pine: which side does it press?

为什么重要Why it matters

它让“少画”成为一种主动的观看设计。

It makes painting less an active design of looking.
