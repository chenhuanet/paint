明暗对照Chiaroscuro

明暗对照用强烈的亮部和暗部塑造体积与戏剧。它不只是“打光”，而是让光成为叙事角色：谁被照亮，谁留在黑暗里，都是在做判断。Chiaroscuro uses strong light and dark to model volume and drama. It is not merely 'lighting' but makes light a narrative character: who is illuminated and who remains in darkness is a judgment.

绘画史Painting History / 技法Techniques
先看三步做法，再看作品；技法只有在具体的画里才成立。Start with the three steps, then the works; a technique only exists inside a specific picture.

怎么做How it is done

  • 先铺暗部，让画面沉下去。
  • 从单一光源提亮受光面。
  • 保留最黑和最亮的小面积，形成戏剧焦点。
  • Lay in the shadows so the picture sinks.
  • Light the illuminated side from a single source.
  • Keep small areas of deepest black and brightest light for focus.
