湿壁画Fresco

湿壁画是在石灰墙未干时把矿物颜料画上去，颜料与墙面一起固化。它不能反复修改，画家必须一次决定构图、颜色和明暗；因此湿壁画总是带着一种“不可撤回”的果断。Fresco is painted onto wet lime plaster with mineral pigments; the colour sets with the wall. It cannot be easily revised, so the painter must decide composition, colour and modelling in one go. Fresco therefore carries an irrevocable decisiveness.

绘画史Painting History / 技法Techniques
先看三步做法，再看作品；技法只有在具体的画里才成立。Start with the three steps, then the works; a technique only exists inside a specific picture.

怎么做How it is done

  • 在墙上抹一层新鲜石灰膏，趁未干时画。
  • 颜料渗入墙面，干后成为墙体的一部分。
  • 一天只能画一块“giornata”，接缝处会留下痕迹。
  • Spread fresh lime plaster and paint while it is still wet.
  • Pigment penetrates the wall and becomes part of it when dry.
  • Only one 'giornata' can be painted per day; seams remain visible.
