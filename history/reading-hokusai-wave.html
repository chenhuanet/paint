《神奈川冲浪里》The Great Wave off Kanagawa

浪为什么比船更大人？Why is the wave larger than the boats and people?
约1831 · 葛饰北斋ca.1831 · Katsushika Hokusai精读Close reading
绘画史Painting History / 精读Close readings
一件作品，一个问题；先看，再判断。One work, one question; look first, judge later.

图片为该作品在本站的主图；元数据见下方作品卡。The image is the primary image for this work; metadata follows in the work card.

问题Question

北斋把浪画成一只张开的手，船在浪谷里，富士山在远处很小。画面不是灾难记录，而是把自然的力量、人的劳动和日本的标志放进同一个斜角构图。

Hokusai paints the wave as an open hand, boats in its trough, Mount Fuji small in the distance. It is not a disaster record but a diagonal composition joining natural force, human labour and a Japanese sign.

语境Context

约1831年木版画，属于《富岳三十六景》系列。北斋是江户时代最著名的浮世绘画师之一。

Woodblock print, c. 1831, from Thirty-six Views of Mount Fuji. Hokusai is one of the most famous ukiyo-e artists of the Edo period.

怎么看How to look

  • 先看浪尖：它像手还是爪？
  • 再看船：人在做什么？
  • 最后看富士山：它为什么这么小？
  • Read the crest: hand or claw?
  • Then read the boats: what are the people doing?
  • Finally read Fuji: why so small?

为什么重要Why it matters

它把风景画成人和自然的力量对比，而不是明信片。

It paints landscape as a power comparison between humans and nature, not a postcard.
