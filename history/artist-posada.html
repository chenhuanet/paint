何塞·瓜达卢佩·波萨达José Guadalupe Posada

波萨达是墨西哥近代最重要的版画家和插画师，为廉价的印刷品、报纸、 broadsides 和儿童读物工作。他画骷髅、政客、罪犯、灾难和日常新闻，线条粗、黑白强，图像可以在街头被一眼读懂。Posada was Mexico's most important modern printmaker and illustrator, working for cheap prints, newspapers, broadsides and children's books. He drew skulls, politicians, criminals, disasters and daily news; lines are coarse, black and white are strong, and an image can be read at a glance in the street.

小传Biography

《卡特里娜骷髅头》把死亡变成一位戴帽、披羽毛的时髦女性；《赫苏斯·内格雷特》把真实犯罪画成连环画式的轰动故事；《轻浮的青蛙》则用动物讽刺人类。波萨达的现代性不在于“先锋”，而在于图像如何被复制、传播和消费。

Calavera de la Catrina turns death into a fashionable woman with a hat and feathers; Jesús Negrete turns a real crime into a broadsheet sensation; The Frivolous Frog uses animals to satirise humans. Posada's modernity is not 'avant-garde' but how images are copied, circulated and consumed.
