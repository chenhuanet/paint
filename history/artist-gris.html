胡安·格里斯Juan Gris

格里斯是立体主义三人组里最“建筑”的一位。他不像毕加索那样破坏，也不像布拉克那样松散；他把碎片重新搭成结构，让画面像一座有承重墙的房子。Gris is the most 'architectural' of the Cubist trio. He does not destroy like Picasso or loosen like Braque; he rebuilds fragments into structure, making the picture like a house with load-bearing walls.

小传Biography

《毕加索肖像》把正脸、侧脸和俯视同时放在一张脸上，灰蓝、赭石和冷白压成单色和弦。背景的斜线与衣领的三角形互相咬合。格里斯让分析立体主义从“分解物体”走向“建构画面”。

Portrait of Picasso puts front, profile and top view on one face; grey-blue, ochre and cool white form a monochrome chord. Background diagonals interlock with the collar's triangles. Gris moves Analytical Cubism from dismantling objects to constructing a picture.

看结构：哪里是承重墙？看脸：几个角度同时成立？Read structure: where is the load-bearing wall? Read the face: how many angles coexist?
