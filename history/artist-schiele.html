埃贡·席勒Egon Schiele

席勒是维也纳表现主义画家，古斯塔夫·克里姆特的门生。他画自画像、裸体和肖像，身体被拉长、扭曲，线条像被刮过纸面；他的画常被道德警察起诉，也因为太直接而显得脆弱。Schiele was a Vienna Expressionist and a protégé of Gustav Klimt. He painted self-portraits, nudes and portraits; bodies are stretched and twisted, lines scrape the paper, and his work was often prosecuted by morality police yet remains vulnerable for being so direct.

小传Biography

《与中国灯笼花自画像》把脸、手和植物放在一个很窄的画面里，眼睛直视观者，肩膀却缩着。席勒不是在展示“自我”，他是在暴露一个正在发抖的身体。

Self-Portrait with Chinese Lantern Plant puts face, hands and plant into a narrow frame; the eyes look straight at us while the shoulders shrink. Schiele does not display a 'self'; he exposes a body that is trembling.

看手与肩：身体在防御，还是在靠近？Read hands and shoulders: is the body defending, or approaching?
