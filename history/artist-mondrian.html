皮特·蒙德里安Piet Mondrian

蒙德里安从荷兰风景画出发，一步步删掉曲线、透视、阴影和混合色，最后只剩横线、竖线和三原色。他把这种关系称为“新造型主义”：水平与垂直代表宇宙的两种基本力量，颜色代表纯粹能量。Mondrian began with Dutch landscape and gradually deleted curve, perspective, shadow and mixed colour until only horizontals, verticals and three primaries remained. He called this relationship Neo-Plasticism: horizontal and vertical stand for the two basic forces of the universe, colour for pure energy.

小传Biography

《红、蓝、黄构图》里，红块最大，蓝块在左下，黄块很小；黑线粗一处细一处，像在寻找平衡而不是执行网格。画面没有故事，却要求你长时间观看：每一次视线移动，色块的重力都会改变。

In Composition with Red, Blue and Yellow, red is largest, blue at lower left, yellow very small; black lines are thicker and thinner, seeking balance rather than executing a grid. There is no story, yet the painting asks for prolonged looking: with every shift of attention, the weight of colour changes.

看平衡：哪一块颜色最重？哪一条线在支撑它？Read balance: which colour weighs most? Which line supports it?
