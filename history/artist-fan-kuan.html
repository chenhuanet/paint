范宽Fan Kuan

范宽是北宋山水画的代表。他长期隐居终南山、太华山，主张“师造化”，也就是向自然本身学习。他的山不是远看的一团墨，而是由无数短促、坚硬的皴笔堆出来的石质结构；山顶的密林像一顶沉重的帽子，山脚的水气和雾把山托起来。Fan Kuan is a central figure of Northern Song landscape. He lived for years in the Zhongnan and Taihua mountains and argued for 'learning from creation' — studying nature itself. His mountains are not a distant ink wash but stone structures built from countless short, hard texture strokes; dense forest at the summit sits like a heavy cap, and mist at the base lifts the mountain.

小传Biography

《溪山行旅图》把主峰放在画面中央，几乎占满上半幅；行旅、驴队和寺庙小到像标点。范宽不画“人征服山”，他画“山让人变小”。北宋山水因此不只是风景，而是一种宇宙秩序：中心有重量，边缘有呼吸。

Travelers among Mountains and Streams places the main peak at the center, filling almost the entire upper half; travelers, donkeys and a temple are as small as punctuation. Fan Kuan does not paint 'man conquering the mountain' but 'the mountain making man small'. Northern Song landscape is therefore not scenery but cosmic order: the center has weight, the edges breathe.

看皴笔：山是石头还是墨？看比例：人到底有多小？Read the texture strokes: is the mountain stone or ink? Read scale: how small are the people?
