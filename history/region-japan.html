日本Japan

日本绘画在屏风、版画和卷轴之间切换尺度。宗达的燕子花、春信的少女、北斋的浪、广重的桥，都把日常、季节和城市折进装饰性的平面。Japanese painting moves between screen, print and scroll. Sōtatsu's irises, Harunobu's girls, Hokusai's wave and Hiroshige's bridge fold everyday life, season and city into a decorative plane.

三个特征Three traits

  • 装饰性平面：金箔、色块和图案压平空间。
  • 季节与日常：花、雨、桥、旅行成为时间标记。
  • 版画市场：浮世绘把图像变成可复制的城市商品。
  • Decorative plane: gold, colour blocks and pattern flatten space.
  • Season and everyday: flower, rain, bridge and travel mark time.
  • Print market: ukiyo-e turns images into reproducible urban goods.
