金底Gold Ground

金底是在木板或画布上贴金箔，再在上面画人物、衣褶和建筑。金不是“背景颜色”，而是光本身：它不照亮对象，它让对象从神圣的空间里显现。中世纪圣像和早期文艺复兴祭坛画都用它制造一种不属人间的亮度。Gold ground lays gold leaf on panel or canvas, then paints figures, drapery and architecture over it. Gold is not a 'background colour' but light itself: it does not illuminate objects, it makes them appear from a sacred space. Medieval icons and early Renaissance altarpieces use it to produce a brightness not of this world.

绘画史Painting History / 技法Techniques
先看三步做法，再看作品；技法只有在具体的画里才成立。Start with the three steps, then the works; a technique only exists inside a specific picture.

怎么做How it is done

  • 在木板上涂石膏底，打磨平滑。
  • 用红土或胶作底，贴上金箔并压实。
  • 在金箔上打线、压花，再用蛋彩画人物。
  • Prepare a gessoed panel and smooth it.
  • Lay bole or adhesive, apply gold leaf and burnish it.
  • Tool and punch the gold, then paint figures in tempera.
