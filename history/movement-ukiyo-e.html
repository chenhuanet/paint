浮世绘Ukiyo-e

浮世绘是江户的商业艺术：出版商委托，画师起稿，雕版师刻板，印刷师上色，商人卖给市民。它画演员、美人、相扑、茶馆、桥梁、海浪和雨。它不追求唯一性，而追求可复制、可传播、可消费的现代图像。Ukiyo-e is the commercial art of Edo: a publisher commissions, an artist designs, a carver cuts, a printer colours, a merchant sells to townspeople. It pictures actors, beauties, sumo, teahouses, bridges, waves and rain. It does not seek uniqueness but a modern image that can be reproduced, circulated and consumed.

绘画史Painting History / 流派Movements
先看三个特征，再看三件作品；最后把它放回它所在的章节。Start with three traits, then three works; finally put it back into its chapter.

三个特征Three traits

  • 木版分工：画、刻、印、卖，是一条完整的商业链。
  • 平面装饰：线条清楚，颜色平涂，空间被压缩。
  • 城市时尚：发型、腰带、演员、名胜，都是可消费的符号。
  • Woodblock division of labour: design, carving, printing and selling form a commercial chain.
  • Flat decoration: clear lines, flat colour, compressed space.
  • Urban fashion: hair, sash, actors and famous places are consumable signs.

怎么看How to look

  • 看线条：刻出来的线如何组织运动？
  • 看裁切：人物被画框切断了吗？
  • 看文字：题签、印章、出版商标记在哪里？
  • Read the line: how do cut lines organise movement?
  • Read the crop: are figures cut by the frame?
  • Read text: where are title, seals and publisher marks?
