线的宗教与山的发现Religious line and the discovery of mountain

这一千年里，绘画主要服务于信仰：中国佛教壁画、敦煌绢画、欧洲拜占庭圣像和罗马式壁画，都在用线、金箔和平面空间讲述神圣。山水在唐代独立成科，五代北宋达到高峰；欧洲则从圣像走向乔托式的人间舞台。In this millennium painting served faith: Buddhist murals and Dunhuang silk in China, Byzantine icons and Romanesque frescoes in Europe, all using line, gold and flat space to tell the sacred. Landscape became an independent subject in the Tang and peaked in the Northern Song; Europe moved from icon toward Giotto's human stage.

文艺复兴的发明The invention of the Renaissance

十四到十五世纪，欧洲绘画重新发现身体、空间和古代。乔托让哀悼有重量，马萨乔让透视成为叙事，凡·艾克让油彩透明，波提切利让神话回到佛罗伦萨。中国元代文人则把山水变成笔墨的人格。In the fourteenth and fifteenth centuries European painting rediscovered body, space and antiquity. Giotto gives grief weight, Masaccio makes perspective narrative, Van Eyck makes oil transparent, Botticelli brings myth back to Florence. In Yuan China, literati turn landscape into the personality of brush and ink.

盛期与北方High Renaissance and the North

十六世纪上半叶，意大利把盛期文艺复兴推到顶点：达·芬奇、米开朗基罗、拉斐尔、提香。北方则用细节和油彩继续追问日常与道德：丢勒、勃鲁盖尔、博斯。盛期之后，矫饰主义开始怀疑“完美”。In the first half of the sixteenth century Italy pushed the High Renaissance to its peak: Leonardo, Michelangelo, Raphael, Titian. The North continued to interrogate the everyday and the moral with detail and oil: Dürer, Bruegel, Bosch. After the peak, Mannerism begins to doubt 'perfection'.

巴洛克剧场The Baroque theatre

十七世纪把画面变成舞台：光从黑暗中切下来，人物在动作的最高点停住。卡拉瓦乔、鲁本斯、伦勃朗、委拉斯开兹、维米尔，各自用光处理权力、信仰、记忆和日常。The seventeenth century turns the picture into a stage: light cuts through darkness, figures freeze at the peak of action. Caravaggio, Rubens, Rembrandt, Velázquez and Vermeer each use light to handle power, faith, memory and the everyday.

享乐、物与启蒙Pleasure, things and Enlightenment

十八世纪把镜头从教堂和宫廷转向客厅、卧室、餐桌和花园。洛可可的曲线、夏尔丹的物、新古典的轮廓同时存在；沙龙展和艺术批评开始出现，绘画要在陌生人面前证明自己。The eighteenth century moves the camera from church and court to drawing room, bedroom, dining table and garden. Rococo curve, Chardin's objects and Neoclassical contour coexist; salon exhibitions and art criticism appear, and painting must prove itself before strangers.

革命、风暴与现实主义Revolution, storm and Realism

十九世纪前半叶，新古典与浪漫主义争夺画面：大卫画公民，戈雅画屠杀，弗里德里希画孤独，透纳画光。后半叶，现实主义把工人、农民、城市和丑闻推到前景。In the first half of the nineteenth century Neoclassicism and Romanticism fight for the picture: David paints citizens, Goya massacre, Friedrich solitude, Turner light. In the second half, Realism pushes workers, peasants, the city and scandal into the foreground.

光、色彩与内心Light, colour and the inner world

1880年代以后，绘画分裂成多条路：印象主义继续画光，后印象主义拆解结构，象征主义转向梦，表现主义转向焦虑，新艺术运动把身体变成装饰。颜色和线条开始自己说话。After the 1880s painting splits into several paths: Impressionism continues to paint light, Post-Impressionism dismantles structure, Symbolism turns to dream, Expressionism to anxiety, Art Nouveau turns the body into decoration. Colour and line begin to speak for themselves.

拆解与抽象Dismantling and abstraction

二十世纪初，绘画不再假装自己是一扇窗。立体主义把物体拆成多个视点，奥费主义让颜色旋转，抽象艺术让线条脱离对象，至上主义减到黑方块，风格派减到横线、竖线和三原色。At the beginning of the twentieth century painting stops pretending to be a window. Cubism breaks objects into multiple viewpoints, Orphism sets colour spinning, abstract art frees line from objects, Suprematism reduces to a black square, De Stijl to horizontals, verticals and three primaries.
