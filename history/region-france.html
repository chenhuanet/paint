REGION / FRANCEREGION / FRANCE

法国France

法国从古典主义到印象派，不断重定义“谁可以被画”。库尔贝把工人和画室放进大画，莫奈和毕沙罗把城市、铁路和光拆成笔触；绘画从沙龙走向街头。From classicism to Impressionism, France repeatedly redefined who could be painted. Courbet put workers and the studio into large pictures; Monet and Pissarro broke city, railway and light into brushwork. Painting moved from salon to street.

三个特征Three traits

  • 沙龙与反沙龙：官方展览决定声誉，独立展览制造新市场。
  • 城市作为题材：街道、铁路、桥和咖啡馆进入画面。
  • 笔触作为时间：一次落笔记录一个瞬间的光。
  • Salon and anti-Salon: official exhibition makes reputation, independent shows make a new market.
  • City as subject: street, railway, bridge and café enter the picture.
  • Brushstroke as time: one mark records one instant of light.
