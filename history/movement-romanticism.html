浪漫主义Romanticism

浪漫主义相信风暴、废墟、孤独和不可控制的自然。它把个体放到画面中心，也把个体放到自然的边缘。戈雅记录屠杀，弗里德里希让背影面对雾海，透纳把夕阳画成一场燃烧，德拉克洛瓦让自由举旗，艾瓦佐夫斯基让海浪高过船。Romanticism believes in storm, ruin, solitude and uncontrollable nature. It puts the individual at the center and at the edge of nature. Goya records massacre, Friedrich sets a back against the sea of fog, Turner paints sunset as combustion, Delacroix raises the flag of liberty, Aivazovsky makes the wave taller than the ship.

绘画史Painting History / 流派Movements
先看三个特征，再看三件作品；最后把它放回它所在的章节。Start with three traits, then three works; finally put it back into its chapter.

三个特征Three traits

  • 崇高：自然大过人，美让人不舒服。
  • 个体：背影、孤独者、英雄、受害者，都是画面的中心。
  • 运动：风、浪、云、火，画面永远在动。
  • The sublime: nature is larger than man, and beauty is uncomfortable.
  • The individual: back figures, loners, heroes and victims occupy the center.
  • Motion: wind, wave, cloud and fire keep the picture moving.

怎么看How to look

  • 看天空：它占多大比例？
  • 看人物：他是英雄，还是自然里的一个小点？
  • 看运动：画面在往哪个方向走？
  • Read the sky: how much space does it take?
  • Read the figure: hero or a small dot in nature?
  • Read motion: which direction is the picture moving?
