黄公望Huang Gongwang

黄公望是“元四家”之首，年轻时做过小吏，中年入道，晚年隐居富春江。他把山水画成一种可以居住的时间：坡岸、树林、渔村、云雾在长卷里慢慢展开，笔墨的干湿浓淡记录的是画家在山里走了多久，而不是地图上的距离。Huang Gongwang leads the 'Four Masters of the Yuan'. He served as a minor official, entered Daoist orders in middle age, and spent his late years near the Fuchun River. He paints landscape as inhabitable time: banks, woods, fishing villages and mist unfold slowly in a handscroll; wet and dry, dark and light record how long the painter walked, not map distance.

小传Biography

《富春山居图》用披麻皴画土坡，线条松而厚，墨色层层积染。画面不追求奇峰，而追求“平远”：山不高，水不急，人在其中可以呼吸。它后来被烧成两段，一段在浙江省博物馆，一段在台北故宫，成为一件被历史切开的名作。

Dwelling in the Fuchun Mountains uses hemp-fibre texture strokes for earthen slopes, loose but dense, with layered ink. It does not seek spectacular peaks but 'level distance': low mountains, slow water, room to breathe. Later burned into two sections, one in Zhejiang Museum and one in the National Palace Museum, it became a masterpiece cut by history.
