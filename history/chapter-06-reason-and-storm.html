理性与风暴：从新古典到浪漫Reason and Storm: From Neoclassicism to Romanticism

革命、崇高与个体如何进入画面？How did revolution, the sublime and the individual enter the picture?
1784–18501784–1850第 06 章Chapter 06
绘画史Painting History / 第 06 章Chapter 06
从大卫到德拉克洛瓦，欧洲绘画在六十年里经历了两次地震：一次是政治革命，一次是趣味革命。新古典主义相信轮廓、义务和公民德性；浪漫主义相信风暴、废墟、孤独和不可控制的自然。戈雅站在两者之间，用画笔记录屠杀，也记录理性睡着后出现的怪物。From David to Delacroix, European painting went through two earthquakes in sixty years: a political revolution and a revolution in taste. Neoclassicism believed in contour, duty and civic virtue; Romanticism believed in storm, ruin, solitude and uncontrollable nature. Goya stands between them, painting massacre and the monsters that appear when reason sleeps.

新古典：把身体变成公民Neoclassicism: the body as citizen

《荷拉斯兄弟之誓》把三个儿子和父亲摆成一个几何结构：手臂伸直，剑在空中，女人在后景哭泣。大卫不关心心理的复杂，他要的是一种可被模仿的公民姿态。画面里的每一次转折都像雕塑，颜色被压到最低，故事被压成一句口号。

《马拉之死》更冷：马拉死在浴缸里，身体像一尊被切断的古典雕像，木箱上的字条是证据，刀在地上。大卫把政治谋杀变成圣像画，让死者为革命说话。新古典主义的“理性”并不平静，它可以把暴力整理成一张干净的图。

The Oath of the Horatii arranges three sons and a father into a geometry: arms straight, swords in the air, women weeping in the background. David is not interested in psychological complexity; he wants a civic gesture that can be imitated. Every turn is sculptural, colour is reduced, and the story is compressed into a slogan.

The Death of Marat is colder: Marat lies dead in his bath, his body like a severed classical statue, the note on the wooden block is evidence, the knife on the floor. David turns political murder into an icon, letting the dead speak for the Revolution. Neoclassical 'reason' is not calm; it can arrange violence into a clean image.

浪漫：崇高是一种不舒服的美Romanticism: the sublime is an uncomfortable beauty

《雾海上的漫游者》把一个人放在山顶，背对观众，面对一片被雾吞掉的山谷。我们看不到他的脸，只能看到他的姿态：站着，拄杖，像在测量自己与无限的距离。弗里德里希让风景成为内心，而不是背景。

透纳的《被拖去解体的无畏号》把一艘老战舰拖向拆船厂，夕阳把天空烧成金红，船体只剩一个黑色的剪影。浪漫主义不回避衰败，它把衰败画成一种壮丽。戈雅的《1808年5月3日》则拒绝壮丽：枪口贴着人脸，白衬衫上的血是唯一的灯。浪漫主义的“个体”有时是英雄，有时只是下一个被枪杀的人。

Wanderer above the Sea of Fog places a man on a summit, his back to us, facing a valley swallowed by mist. We cannot see his face, only his posture: standing, leaning on a cane, measuring his distance from infinity. Friedrich makes landscape an inner state rather than a backdrop.

Turner's The Fighting Temeraire drags an old warship toward the breaker's yard; sunset burns the sky gold-red, and the hull is reduced to a black silhouette. Romanticism does not avoid decay; it paints decay as grandeur. Goya's The Third of May 1808 refuses grandeur: rifles are pressed to faces, and the blood on a white shirt is the only light. The Romantic 'individual' is sometimes a hero, sometimes simply the next person shot.

怎么看How to look

  • 看轮廓：新古典的身体是否像雕塑？浪漫的身体是否被风、雾或光吞掉？
  • 看天空：天空占多大比例？它是在背景里，还是在统治画面？
  • 看死亡：死者被画成英雄、圣徒，还是只是一个倒下的人？
  • Read contour: is the Neoclassical body sculptural, or is the Romantic body swallowed by wind, fog or light?
  • Read the sky: how much space does it take, and does it frame or rule the picture?
  • Read death: is the dead painted as hero, saint, or simply a person who fell?
