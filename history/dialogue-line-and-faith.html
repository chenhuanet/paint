线与信仰：顾恺之与乔托Line and Faith: Gu Kaizhi and Giotto

两个相隔近千年的画家，都用线把人物从背景里拉出来；但一个服务于箴言，一个服务于福音。把《女史箴图》和《哀悼基督》并置，问题不是“谁更写实”，而是线替谁说话。Two painters separated by nearly a millennium both pull figures out of the background with line; one serves maxims, the other the Gospel. Set *Admonitions* beside the *Lamentation* and the question is not 'who is more realistic' but for whom the line speaks.

线在两张画里是规矩还是重力？Is line rule or gravity in these two pictures?

《女史箴图》Admonitions of the Instructress to the Court Ladies约380–400（唐摹本） · 顾恺之（传）ca.380–400（唐摹本） · Gu Kaizhi (attributed)
《哀悼基督》Lamentation约1305 · 乔托ca.1305 · Giotto di Bondone

顾恺之的线让身体服从礼仪：衣袖、回头和低头都被规范。乔托的线让身体服从重量：肩膀塌下，脸贴向死者。前者把伦理画成姿势，后者把悲伤画成重力。

Gu Kaizhi's line makes the body obey ritual: sleeve, turn and lowered head are regulated. Giotto's line makes the body obey weight: shoulders collapse, a face presses to the dead. The first paints ethics as posture, the second grief as gravity.

收束Closing

线从来不是中性工具；它总是替某种制度、信仰或身体经验工作。Line is never neutral; it always works for an institution, a faith or a bodily experience.
