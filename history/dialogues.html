三组对读Three dialogues

把不同年代、不同地域的作品放在一起，不是为了排名，而是为了看清各自的问题。Put works from different periods and regions together, not to rank them but to see their separate questions.

线与信仰：顾恺之与乔托Line and Faith: Gu Kaizhi and Giotto

对读Dialogue

两个相隔近千年的画家，都用线把人物从背景里拉出来；但一个服务于箴言，一个服务于福音。把《女史箴图》和《哀悼基督》并置，问题不是“谁更写实”，而是线替谁说话。Two painters separated by nearly a millennium both pull figures out of the background with line; one serves maxims, the other the Gospel. Set *Admonitions* beside the *Lamentation* and the question is not 'who is more realistic' but for whom the line speaks.

城市与人群：张择端与勃鲁盖尔City and Crowd: Zhang Zeduan and Bruegel

对读Dialogue

一个画汴京的桥与船，一个画巴别塔的工地。两幅画都把“人群”当作主角，但一个在记录城市如何运转，一个在记录人类如何失败。One paints the bridge and boats of Bianjing, the other the building site of Babel. Both make the crowd the protagonist, but one records how a city works, the other how humanity fails.

空白与黑暗：倪瓒与卡拉瓦乔Blank and Dark: Ni Zan and Caravaggio

对读Dialogue

倪瓒用空白让画面变轻，卡拉瓦乔用黑暗让画面变重。两种极端都在处理同一个问题：当人和世界之间没有直接接触，绘画如何让“距离”可见？Ni Zan uses blank space to make the picture light; Caravaggio uses darkness to make it heavy. Both extremes handle one question: when there is no direct contact between person and world, how does painting make distance visible?
