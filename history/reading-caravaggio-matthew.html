《圣马太蒙召》The Calling of Saint Matthew

光从哪里来，谁被选中？Where does the light come from, and who is chosen?
1599–1600 · 卡拉瓦乔1599–1600 · Caravaggio精读Close reading
绘画史Painting History / 精读Close readings
一件作品，一个问题；先看，再判断。One work, one question; look first, judge later.

图片为该作品在本站的主图；元数据见下方作品卡。The image is the primary image for this work; metadata follows in the work card.

问题Question

基督的手从右侧暗处伸入，指向马太；马太的手指向自己，好像在问“是我吗？”光从基督方向斜切进来，照亮一张正在数钱的脸。

Christ's hand enters from the dark right, pointing at Matthew; Matthew's hand points to himself, as if asking 'me?' Light cuts in from Christ's direction and lands on a face counting money.

语境Context

1600年布面油画，藏于罗马圣路易吉·德·弗朗切西教堂孔塔雷利礼拜堂。卡拉瓦乔是暗绘和巴洛克戏剧性的代表。

Oil on canvas, 1600, in the Contarelli Chapel, San Luigi dei Francesi, Rome. Caravaggio is the representative of tenebrism and Baroque drama.

怎么看How to look

  • 先看手：谁指向谁？
  • 再看光：它照在谁脸上？
  • 最后看桌子：钱还在数吗？
  • Read the hands: who points at whom?
  • Then read light: whose face does it hit?
  • Finally read the table: is the money still being counted?

为什么重要Why it matters

它把神圣召唤放进一个正在营业的税关，让信仰变成突然的打断。

It puts divine calling inside a working tax office, making faith a sudden interruption.
