《女史箴图》Admonitions of the Instructress to the Court Ladies

一根线如何替文字做伦理判断？How does a line make an ethical judgment for a text?
约380–400（唐摹本） · 顾恺之（传）ca.380–400（唐摹本） · Gu Kaizhi (attributed)精读Close reading
绘画史Painting History / 精读Close readings
一件作品，一个问题；先看，再判断。One work, one question; look first, judge later.

图片为该作品在本站的主图；元数据见下方作品卡。The image is the primary image for this work; metadata follows in the work card.

问题Question

《女史箴图》不是插图，而是把张华的箴言拆成十二段身体动作。谁低头、谁侧身、谁把手藏进袖子，线在替文字执行规矩。

Admonitions is not an illustration but a translation of Zhang Hua's maxims into twelve bodily actions. Who lowers the head, who turns aside, whose hand hides in a sleeve — the line enforces the text's rules.

语境Context

现存为唐代摹本，绢本设色，藏于大英博物馆。原作传为东晋顾恺之，文本则是张华写给宫廷女性的箴言。

The surviving version is a Tang copy in ink and colour on silk, held by the British Museum. The original is attributed to Eastern Jin Gu Kaizhi; the text is Zhang Hua's advice to court women.

怎么看How to look

  • 先看眼睛：谁在看谁，谁不敢看？
  • 再看衣袖：手藏在里面，还是在外面？
  • 最后看线：它在哪里断开，又在哪里接上？
  • Start with eyes: who looks at whom, who dares not?
  • Then read sleeves: is the hand hidden or visible?
  • Finally read the line: where does it break, where does it reconnect?

为什么重要Why it matters

它让“道德”变成可以看见的身体姿势，而不是抽象教条。

It turns morality into visible posture rather than abstract doctrine.
