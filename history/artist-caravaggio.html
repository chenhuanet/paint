卡拉瓦乔Caravaggio

卡拉瓦乔是巴洛克最危险的画家。他把圣徒画成街上的穷人，把光画成从黑暗里切下来的一刀。他的画面没有优雅的距离：血会溅到枕头上，脚会脏，税吏会数钱。Caravaggio is the most dangerous painter of the Baroque. He paints saints as poor people from the street and light as a knife cut out of darkness. His pictures have no elegant distance: blood sprays onto pillows, feet are dirty, tax collectors count money.

小传Biography

《圣马太蒙召》里，基督的手从暗处伸进来，一束斜光照在税吏桌上。马太指着自己，好像在问“是我吗？”光不是自然光，它是选择：被照亮的人被召唤，留在暗处的人继续数钱。

In The Calling of Saint Matthew, Christ's hand enters from the dark; a shaft of light falls on the tax table. Matthew points at himself, as if asking 'me?' Light is not natural but a choice: the illuminated are called, those left in shadow keep counting.

找光：它为什么只照一个人？看手：谁在召唤，谁在回应？Find the light: why does it illuminate one person? Read the hands: who calls, who answers?
