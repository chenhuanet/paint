石涛Shitao

石涛是清初“四僧”之一，明宗室后裔，国破后出家，晚年定居扬州，以卖画为生。他主张“搜尽奇峰打草稿”，反对只临摹古人；他的山水常常湿、乱、满，却有自己的呼吸。Shitao was one of the 'Four Monks' of the early Qing, a Ming royal descendant who became a monk after the dynasty fell and later lived in Yangzhou selling paintings. He argued for 'searching all strange peaks to make a draft' and opposed mere copying of ancients; his landscapes are wet, crowded and full, yet breathe.

小传Biography

《黄山三十六峰图》把黄山的峰、云、松压成一团运动的墨，峰与峰之间没有清楚的边界，只有墨色的呼吸。石涛的“个体”不是孤傲，而是不肯把自然写成别人的模板。

Thirty-six Peaks of Mount Huang presses Huangshan's peaks, clouds and pines into a moving mass of ink; there are no clear borders between peaks, only the breathing of ink. Shitao's 'individuality' is not aloofness but a refusal to turn nature into someone else's template.

看峰与云：边界在哪里消失？Read peaks and clouds: where do the boundaries disappear?
