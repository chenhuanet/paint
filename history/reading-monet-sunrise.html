《印象·日出》Impression, Sunrise

一幅港口为什么叫“印象”？Why is a harbour called an 'impression'?
1872 · 克劳德·莫奈1872 · Claude Monet精读Close reading
绘画史Painting History / 精读Close readings
一件作品，一个问题；先看，再判断。One work, one question; look first, judge later.

图片为该作品在本站的主图；元数据见下方作品卡。The image is the primary image for this work; metadata follows in the work card.

问题Question

莫奈不画港口的清楚轮廓，而画晨雾、橙日、蓝水和远处烟囱的混合。标题“印象”原本带贬义，后来却成了整个运动的名称：绘画开始承认自己只是瞬间的观看。

Monet does not paint clear harbour outlines but the mix of morning mist, orange sun, blue water and distant chimneys. The title 'Impression' was originally a slur and became the name of a movement: painting admits it is only an instant of looking.

语境Context

1872年布面油画，藏于巴黎玛摩丹莫奈博物馆。作品在1874年第一届印象派展览中展出。

Oil on canvas, 1872, held by the Musée Marmottan Monet, Paris. It was shown at the first Impressionist exhibition in 1874.

怎么看How to look

  • 先看太阳：它是圆的吗？
  • 再看水：反射了什么颜色？
  • 最后看船：你能数清几条？
  • Read the sun: is it round?
  • Then read water: what colours reflect?
  • Finally read boats: how many can you count?

为什么重要Why it matters

它让“未完成”的外观成为现代绘画的合法语言。

It makes an 'unfinished' look a legitimate language of modern painting.
