张择端Zhang Zeduan

张择端是北宋末年的宫廷画家，生平记载很少，但《清明上河图》让他成为最会画城市的人。这幅长卷从郊野、汴河、虹桥一直画到城门和街市，画面里有船夫、商贩、医生、乞丐、骆驼、轿子、酒旗，几乎是一部北宋汴京的社会百科。Zhang Zeduan was a court painter at the end of the Northern Song. Very little is known of his life, but *Along the River During the Qingming Festival* makes him the great painter of the city. The scroll moves from countryside to the Bian River, Rainbow Bridge, city gate and market; boatmen, traders, doctors, beggars, camels, sedan chairs and wine banners make it an encyclopaedia of Bianjing society.

小传Biography

张择端不靠透视，而靠遮挡、密度和视线方向制造空间。越靠近虹桥，人物越密，动作越急；桥上的人看船，船上的人看桥，画外的我们看所有人。城市在这里不是背景，而是一台正在运转的机器。

Zhang creates space not through perspective but occlusion, density and direction of gaze. Near the Rainbow Bridge figures thicken and gestures quicken; people on the bridge watch the boat, people in the boat watch the bridge, and we watch everyone. The city is not a backdrop but a machine in motion.

找虹桥：谁在看船，谁在看桥，谁在看我们？Find the Rainbow Bridge: who watches the boat, who watches the bridge, who watches us?
