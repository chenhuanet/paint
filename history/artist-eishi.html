鸟文斋荣之Chōbunsai Eishi

荣之出身武士家庭，后来成为浮世绘画师，擅长美人画。他的女性身形修长，衣纹像水一样流动，背景常是寺院、河岸、游船和桥，人物被放在一个可游的江户空间里。Eishi came from a samurai family and later became an ukiyo-e artist, known for bijin-ga. His women are tall, drapery flows like water, and backgrounds are temples, riverbanks, pleasure boats and bridges: figures are placed inside a visitable Edo space.

小传Biography

《浅草绘马堂休憩女子图》与《隅田川新大桥游船图》都把“休息”画成一种社会场景：谁在船上，谁在岸上，谁被看见，谁只是背景。荣之的线条比春信更瘦，空间也更开阔。

Women Resting in the Votive Picture Hall at Asakusa and Pleasure Boats on the Sumida River turn rest into a social scene: who is in the boat, who on the bank, who is looked at, who is only background. Eishi's line is thinner than Harunobu's and his space more open.

看岸与船：谁在移动，谁被固定？Read bank and boat: who moves, who is fixed?
