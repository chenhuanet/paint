安德烈亚·德尔·萨托Andrea del Sarto

萨托是佛罗伦萨盛期文艺复兴的画家，瓦萨里称他“没有错误的画家”。他的构图平衡、色彩柔和、人物姿态优雅，却常有一种安静的忧郁：画面越完美，越像在掩饰什么。Sarto was a Florentine High Renaissance painter whom Vasari called 'the painter without faults'. His compositions balance, colours soften, gestures are elegant, yet a quiet melancholy remains: the more perfect the surface, the more it seems to hide.

小传Biography

《以撒的献祭》把暴力动作停在最后一秒：刀举起，天使的手抓住腕，孩子的身体被扭成一条弧线；《妇女肖像》则把身份、衣料和目光都收得很紧。萨托的“完美”不是冷漠，而是把情绪压到表面之下。

The Sacrifice of Isaac stops violence at the last second: knife raised, angel's hand catching the wrist, the boy's body twisted into an arc; the Portrait of a Woman holds identity, fabric and gaze tightly. Sarto's 'perfection' is not coldness but emotion pressed beneath the surface.

看停顿：动作为什么没有完成？Read the pause: why is the action not completed?
