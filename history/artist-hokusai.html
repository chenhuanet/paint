葛饰北斋Katsushika Hokusai

葛饰北斋是浮世绘最著名的画家，也是最长寿、最多产的一位。他画富士山、海浪、桥、瀑布、幽灵和漫画，晚年自称“画狂老人”。他说自己到七十岁才略懂画理，九十岁才敢说真正会画。Hokusai is the most famous ukiyo-e artist, and the longest-lived and most prolific. He painted Fuji, waves, bridges, waterfalls, ghosts and manga; in old age he called himself 'the old man mad about painting'. He said he began to understand painting at seventy and only dared claim mastery at ninety.

小传Biography

《神奈川冲浪里》把浪画成一只伸爪的巨兽，三艘船在浪谷里，富士山小得像一个背景注脚。北斋用西方透视和荷兰版画的深蓝，却把画面组织成日本式的装饰：浪的弧线、船的斜线、山的三角形互相咬合。

The Great Wave off Kanagawa turns the wave into a clawed beast, three boats in the trough, Fuji a footnote. Hokusai uses Western perspective and the deep blue of Dutch prints, yet organises the picture as Japanese decoration: wave curve, boat diagonals and mountain triangle interlock.

看浪：它是水还是爪子？看富士山：它为什么这么小？Read the wave: water or claw? Read Fuji: why is it so small?
