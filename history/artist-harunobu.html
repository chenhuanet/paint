铃木春信Suzuki Harunobu

铃木春信是江户中期浮世绘的代表，推动了“锦绘”（多色木版画）的成熟。他画的多是年轻女子、主妇、侍女和日常室内，身姿细长，色彩清透，背景常被压成单纯的墙面或帘幕。Suzuki Harunobu represents mid-Edo ukiyo-e and helped bring full-colour 'nishiki-e' to maturity. He mostly painted young women, housewives, attendants and interiors: slender figures, clear colour, backgrounds reduced to a wall or curtain.

小传Biography

《主妇与女仆》《名妓与沉睡侍女》把等级关系画得很轻：谁站、谁坐、谁替谁整理衣角，一眼就能读出，却不用夸张的表情。春信的魅力在于把浮世绘从“名所”和“演员”转向室内的一刻。

Woman and Maid Servant and Courtesan and Sleeping Attendant make hierarchy light: who stands, who sits, who adjusts whose hem is legible at once without exaggerated expression. Harunobu's charm is to turn ukiyo-e from famous places and actors toward a moment inside a room.

看谁站着、谁坐着：权力在谁的身体上？Read who stands and who sits: where is power in the body?
