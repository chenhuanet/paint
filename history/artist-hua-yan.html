华喦Hua Yan

华喦是清代扬州画派的重要画家，福建上杭人，常年卖画为生。他的册页把“诗意”拆成很小的生活动作：树下坐一会儿、倚杖看菊、临溪听水、园中刺绣。人物不再是被崇拜的高士，而是可以走近的普通人。Hua Yan was a key painter of the Qing Yangzhou school, from Shanghang in Fujian, who made his living by selling pictures. His album leaves break 'poetic feeling' into small everyday actions: sitting under trees, leaning on a staff to look at chrysanthemums, listening to a stream, doing needlework in a garden. Figures are no longer remote sages but people one could approach.

小传Biography

这套《仿古诗意册页》用色清而薄，人物只占画面很小一部分，山水、树石和空白却承担了大部分情绪。华喦的厉害之处，是把文人画的“雅”与职业画家的“熟”接在一起：画面好卖，但不甜。

The album uses thin, clear colour; figures occupy only a small part of the leaf, while trees, rocks and blank paper carry most of the mood. Hua's achievement is to join literati 'elegance' with professional fluency: the pictures are marketable without becoming sweet.
