They point to another history: painting no longer happens only in galleries but on screens, in open image libraries and in transnational one-person studios. The page does not pretend to cover global contemporary art; it uses these two works as an entry into medium and reproduction.

三个关键词Three keywords

  • 媒介自觉：画布、像素、印刷和屏幕互相翻译。
  • 复制成为条件：一张图可以在多个版本、尺寸和渠道里出现。
  • 全球工作室：个人可以在不同城市、语言和市场之间工作。
  • Medium awareness: canvas, pixel, print and screen translate one another.
  • Reproduction as condition: an image can appear in multiple versions, sizes and channels.
  • Global studio: one person can work across cities, languages and markets.
