《博士来朝》Adoration of the Magi

威尼斯为什么用颜色而不是轮廓？Why does Venice use colour instead of contour?
1500s · Titian、Workshop1500s · Titian、Workshop精读Close reading
绘画史Painting History / 精读Close readings
一件作品，一个问题；先看，再判断。One work, one question; look first, judge later.

图片为该作品在本站的主图；元数据见下方作品卡。The image is the primary image for this work; metadata follows in the work card.

问题Question

提香让三博士的队列从右侧斜切入画面，人物、马匹、天空和建筑被颜色连成一片。轮廓不再封住身体，颜色自己制造体积和空气。

Titian brings the Magi's procession diagonally in from the right; figures, horses, sky and architecture are joined by colour. Contour no longer seals the body; colour makes volume and air by itself.

语境Context

约1500年布面油画，藏于克利夫兰艺术博物馆。提香是威尼斯画派的核心，晚年把油彩的厚薄与笔触推到极致。

Oil on canvas, c. 1500, held by the Cleveland Museum of Art. Titian is central to the Venetian school and in late life pushed oil's thickness and touch to an extreme.

怎么看How to look

  • 先看队列：它从哪里进入？
  • 再看颜色：哪一块最暖？
  • 最后看天空：它是背景还是主体？
  • Read the procession: where does it enter?
  • Then read colour: which mass is warmest?
  • Finally read the sky: background or subject?

为什么重要Why it matters

它证明威尼斯不需要佛罗伦萨的素描也能建立画面。

It proves Venice could build a picture without Florentine drawing.
