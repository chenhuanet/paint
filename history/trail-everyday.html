日常The everyday

日常画不靠神话、历史或奇观，它靠一双愿意停下来的眼睛。这条小径从厨房的鱼走到卧室、街道和餐桌。Everyday painting relies on no myth, history or spectacle, only an eye willing to stop. This trail moves from a kitchen fish to bedroom, street and table.

沿途Along the trail

1728 · 让-巴蒂斯特-西梅翁·夏尔丹1728 · Jean-Baptiste-Siméon Chardin
《鳐鱼》The Ray (La Raie)

一条剖开的鱼，也有自己的尊严和气味。A gutted fish has its own dignity and smell.

约1665 · 约翰内斯·维米尔ca.1665 · Johannes Vermeer
《戴珍珠耳环的少女》Girl with a Pearl Earring

一个回头的瞬间，被画成永恒。A moment of turning painted as eternity.

1863 · 爱德华·马奈1863 · Édouard Manet
《草地上的午餐》Le Déjeuner sur l'herbe

草地上的午餐：日常休闲被画成一场挑战沙龙的丑闻。A picnic on the grass: ordinary leisure painted as a Salon scandal.

1882 · 爱德华·马奈1882 · Édouard Manet
《女神游乐厅的吧台》A Bar at the Folies-Bergère

酒吧女郎站在镜子前，日常带着表演性。A barmaid before a mirror; the everyday is performative.

1930 · 格兰特·伍德1930 · Grant Wood
《美国哥特式》American Gothic

农舍、干草叉和两张不笑的脸，日常变成国家寓言。Farmhouse, pitchfork and two unsmiling faces: the everyday becomes national fable.

走完之后After the walk

日常画不是“小事”，它是最难画的：因为我们都以为自己知道它长什么样。Everyday painting is not 'small'; it is the hardest, because we all think we know what it looks like.
