新古典主义Neoclassicism

新古典主义把身体变成公民，把画面变成宣言。它相信轮廓、义务、理性和古代德性，反对洛可可的轻浮。大卫的《荷拉斯兄弟之誓》让三只手同时举剑，《马拉之死》把政治谋杀整理成圣像。安格尔继承了大卫的线条，却把线条变成一种冷而性感的技术。Neoclassicism turns the body into a citizen and the picture into a manifesto. It believes in contour, duty, reason and ancient virtue, and rejects Rococo frivolity. David's *Oath of the Horatii* raises three hands toward the swords at once; *The Death of Marat* arranges political murder into an icon. Ingres inherits David's line but turns it into a cold, sensual technique.

先看三个特征，再看三件作品；最后把它放回它所在的章节。Start with three traits, then three works; finally put it back into its chapter.

三个特征Three traits

  • 轮廓优先：身体像雕塑，边缘清楚，颜色被压到最低。
  • 公民姿态：人物不是在做自己，而是在示范一种德性。
  • 历史作为现在：古代故事被用来评论革命、国家和个人。
  • Contour first: the body is sculptural, edges clear, colour reduced.
  • Civic gesture: figures are not being themselves but demonstrating a virtue.
  • History as present: ancient stories comment on revolution, state and individual.

怎么看How to look

  • 看轮廓：身体是否像石膏？
  • 看手势：它是在表演，还是在发誓？
  • 看颜色：为什么颜色这么少？它在为什么让路？
  • Read contour: does the body look like plaster?
  • Read gesture: is it performing or swearing?
  • Read colour: why so little, and what does it make room for?
