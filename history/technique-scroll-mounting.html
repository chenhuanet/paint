卷轴装裱Scroll Mounting

卷轴不是画完就结束：纸或绢被裱在绫、纸上，加天头、地头、隔水和轴，才能被卷起、展开、收藏和题跋。装裱决定观看节奏：手卷要一段段展开，立轴要一次挂起。材料和时间因此进入画面。A scroll is not finished when the painting is finished: silk or paper is mounted with brocade, paper, top and bottom borders, spacers and rollers so it can be rolled, unrolled, stored and inscribed. Mounting determines viewing rhythm: a handscroll is opened section by section, a hanging scroll is seen at once. Material and time enter the picture.

绘画史Painting History / 技法Techniques
先看三步做法，再看作品；技法只有在具体的画里才成立。Start with the three steps, then the works; a technique only exists inside a specific picture.

怎么做How it is done

  • 把画心托在纸上，修正褶皱。
  • 加绫边、隔水、天头和地头。
  • 装轴、系带；手卷按段展开，立轴一次挂起。
  • Line the painting with paper and smooth creases.
  • Add silk borders, spacers, top and bottom panels.
  • Fit rollers and ties; open a handscroll in sections, hang a vertical scroll at once.
