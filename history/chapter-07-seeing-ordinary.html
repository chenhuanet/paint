看见普通人：现实主义Seeing Ordinary People: Realism

劳动、城市与日常如何成为“值得画”的题材？How did labour, the city and daily life become subjects worth painting?
1855–18821855–1882第 07 章Chapter 07
绘画史Painting History / 第 07 章Chapter 07
现实主义不是“画得脏”，而是一种政治选择：把学院拒绝的人、地点和身体放到画面的中心。库尔贝在画室里宣布“我没有画天使”，米勒让拾穗者占据前景，马奈把妓女和野餐者请进沙龙，列宾让十一个纤夫拉着一艘船。Realism is not 'painting dirty'; it is a political choice to put the people, places and bodies the Academy rejected at the center of the picture. Courbet announces in his studio that he has never painted an angel, Millet lets gleaners occupy the foreground, Manet brings a courtesan and picnickers into the Salon, Repin harnesses eleven barge haulers to a boat.

画室是一张社会地图The studio is a social map

《画室》的副标题是“我七年艺术生活的真实寓言”。库尔贝把自己画在中央，左边是穷人、工人、妓女和失业者，右边是朋友、赞助人和知识分子。画面不是虚构的神话，而是一张社会关系图；画家坐在中间，既不偏左也不偏右，像一个记录者。

米勒的《拾穗者》把三个农妇弯成三座低矮的山。她们的脸看不清，手却很大；背景是丰收的田野，前景是掉在地上的麦穗。现实主义的力量不在于细节，而在于构图：谁被放在前景，谁就被承认。

The subtitle of The Painter's Studio is 'a real allegory summing up seven years of my artistic life'. Courbet paints himself at the center; on the left are the poor, workers, a prostitute and the unemployed, on the right friends, patrons and intellectuals. The picture is not a myth but a social map; the painter sits in the middle, neither left nor right, like a recorder.

Millet's The Gleaners bends three peasant women into three low hills. Their faces are unclear, but their hands are large; the background is a harvest field, the foreground the fallen grain. The power of Realism is not detail but composition: whoever is placed in the foreground is acknowledged.

城市、身体与丑闻The city, the body and scandal

马奈的《奥林匹亚》把提香的维纳斯换成一个真实的巴黎女人：她直视观者，黑人女仆抱着花，猫在床尾。1865年沙龙把这张画骂成“污秽”，因为它拒绝把裸体变成神话。马奈不是不会画理想美，他是不愿意再假装。

《草地上的午餐》同样把观者的位置弄得很尴尬：两个穿衣男人和一个裸体女人坐在草地上，另一个女人在河里。光线是剧场的光，人物却像在公园。现实主义从这里开始分裂：一边是库尔贝的社会记录，一边是马奈对“观看”本身的挑衅。

Manet's Olympia replaces Titian's Venus with a real Parisian woman: she looks directly at us, a Black maid holds flowers, a cat stands at the foot of the bed. The 1865 Salon called it obscene because it refused to turn nudity into myth. Manet could paint ideal beauty; he would not pretend anymore.

Le Déjeuner sur l'herbe makes the viewer's position equally awkward: two clothed men and a nude woman sit on the grass, another woman wades in the river. The light is theatrical, but the figures behave as if in a park. Realism splits here: Courbet's social record on one side, Manet's provocation of 'looking' itself on the other.

怎么看How to look

  • 看谁被放在前景：工人、农民、妓女、纤夫，还是英雄和神？
  • 看手和脚：它们是否在做真实的劳动？还是只是摆姿势？
  • 看观者的位置：我们是在街上、在画室里，还是被画中人直视？
  • See who occupies the foreground: workers, peasants, a courtesan, barge haulers — or heroes and gods?
  • Read hands and feet: are they doing real labour, or merely posing?
  • Read your own position: are you in the street, in the studio, or being stared at by the sitter?
