信仰Faith

从圣像到祭坛画，从敦煌到西斯廷，信仰给了绘画最稳定的题目，也给了它最严格的规则。这条小径看画家如何在规则里加入人的温度。From icon to altarpiece, from Dunhuang to the Sistine, faith gave painting its most stable subject and its strictest rules. This trail looks at how painters add human warmth inside those rules.

沿途Along the trail

1308–1311 · 杜乔·迪·博宁塞尼亚1308–1311 · Duccio di Buoninsegna
《圣母登宝座》（《马埃斯塔》）Maestà (Madonna Enthroned with Saints and Angels)

金底与正面性：圣像仍然是圣像，但脸开始有温度。Gold ground and frontality: still an icon, but the face begins to warm.

约1305 · 乔托ca.1305 · Giotto di Bondone
《哀悼基督》Lamentation

哀悼变成身体的重力，而不是手势的表演。Mourning becomes the weight of bodies, not a performance of gestures.

1434 · 扬·凡·艾克1434 · Jan van Eyck
《阿尔诺芬尼夫妇像》The Arnolfini Portrait

婚礼的每一个物件都在为信仰作证。Every object in the wedding serves as witness to faith.

1599–1600 · 卡拉瓦乔1599–1600 · Caravaggio
《圣马太蒙召》The Calling of Saint Matthew

神圣召唤发生在税吏的桌边。The divine call happens at a tax collector's table.

1511–1512 · 米开朗基罗1511–1512 · Michelangelo Buonarroti
《创造亚当》The Creation of Adam

创造被画成一次即将发生的接触。Creation is painted as a contact about to happen.

走完之后After the walk

信仰画最动人的时刻，往往是神性被人性稍微弄脏的时刻。The most moving moment in religious painting is often when divinity is slightly dirtied by humanity.
