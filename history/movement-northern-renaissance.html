北方文艺复兴Northern Renaissance

北方文艺复兴的画家不在意大利的阳光下工作，他们在低地国家的雨、石墙、羊毛和账本里工作。油彩让他们把世界画得像玻璃一样透明：镜子、窗、硬币、毛皮、远处的雪。宗教画因此没有被抛弃，而是被日常细节填满；每一个物件都可能是信仰的证据，也可能是道德的警告。Northern Renaissance painters did not work in Italian sunlight; they worked in the rain, stone walls, wool and ledgers of the Low Countries. Oil paint let them make the world transparent as glass: mirrors, windows, coins, fur, distant snow. Religious painting was not abandoned but filled with everyday detail; every object could be evidence of faith or a moral warning.

绘画史Painting History / 流派Movements
先看三个特征，再看三件作品；最后把它放回它所在的章节。Start with three traits, then three works; finally put it back into its chapter.

三个特征Three traits

  • 细节即证据：镜子、字条、鞋、狗、蜡烛都在参与叙事。
  • 油彩的透明：层层罩染让金属、毛皮、皮肤拥有不同的反光。
  • 日常与道德：厨房、雪景、农民、愚人，都可能是一则寓言。
  • Detail as evidence: mirrors, notes, shoes, dogs and candles all take part in the narrative.
  • Transparent oil: layers of glaze give metal, fur and skin different reflections.
  • Everyday and moral: kitchen, snow, peasants and fools can all be parables.

怎么看How to look

  • 放大找小东西：它们为什么被画得这么清楚？
  • 看光：光是否从画面内部发出，而不是从窗外照进来？
  • 看空间：房间是深的，还是被镜子压成一张平面？
  • Zoom in for small things: why are they painted so precisely?
  • Read light: does it come from inside the picture rather than through a window?
  • Read space: is the room deep, or flattened by a mirror?
