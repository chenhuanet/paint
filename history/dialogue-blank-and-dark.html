空白与黑暗：倪瓒与卡拉瓦乔Blank and Dark: Ni Zan and Caravaggio

倪瓒用空白让画面变轻，卡拉瓦乔用黑暗让画面变重。两种极端都在处理同一个问题：当人和世界之间没有直接接触，绘画如何让“距离”可见？Ni Zan uses blank space to make the picture light; Caravaggio uses darkness to make it heavy. Both extremes handle one question: when there is no direct contact between person and world, how does painting make distance visible?

不判断谁更好；只把两张画放在一起，看它们各自回答了什么问题。No verdict on who is better; put two pictures together and see what each answers.

空与黑，哪个更接近信仰？Which is closer to faith, emptiness or blackness?

《渔庄秋霁图》Woods and Valleys of Mount Yu1355 · 倪瓒1355 · Ni Zan
《圣马太蒙召》The Calling of Saint Matthew1599–1600 · 卡拉瓦乔1599–1600 · Caravaggio

倪瓒的空亭不邀请任何人进入，空白是水、雾和拒绝。卡拉瓦乔的暗室却有一束光突然照进来，召唤打断日常。一个把神留在远处，一个把神压到眼前。

Ni Zan's empty pavilion invites no one in; blank is water, mist and refusal. Caravaggio's dark room is suddenly cut by light; calling interrupts the everyday. One keeps the divine at a distance, the other presses it into the face.

收束Closing

空白和黑暗都不是“没有画”，而是两种不同的观看伦理。Blank and dark are not 'nothing painted' but two different ethics of looking.
