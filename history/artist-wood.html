格兰特·伍德Grant Wood

格兰特·伍德是美国地方主义的代表。他出生在艾奥瓦州，画乡村、农舍、农民和土地。他反对现代主义的世界主义，主张美国画家应该画自己熟悉的美国。Grant Wood is the representative of American Regionalism. Born in Iowa, he painted countryside, farmhouses, farmers and land. He opposed the cosmopolitanism of modernism and argued that American painters should paint the America they knew.

小传Biography

《美国哥特式》让妹妹和牙医站在一座木匠哥特式农舍前，男人握干草叉，女人侧看，两人都不笑。干草叉的三根齿在衣服上重复，窗尖在背景里重复。伍德说他想画“美国乡村的严峻与压抑”，这张画却变成了国家符号。

American Gothic places his sister and dentist before a Carpenter Gothic farmhouse; the man holds a pitchfork, the woman looks sideways, neither smiles. The three tines repeat on his overalls, the window point in the background. Wood said he wanted 'the severity and repression' of rural America; the picture became a national symbol.

看重复：干草叉和窗尖在呼应吗？看表情：他们为什么都不笑？Read repetition: do pitchfork and window echo? Read expressions: why do neither smile?
