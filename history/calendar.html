今日一画：三百六十六天A painting a day: 366 days

这是一份编辑排期，不是创作日期。它不声称“某年某月某日画了这张画”，只安排“今天看哪一件”。历史事件另立“艺术史上的今天”，宁缺毋滥。This is an editorial schedule, not a creation date. It does not claim 'this was painted on this day'; it only decides 'what to look at today'. Historical events belong to a separate 'On This Day in Art', and only when exact.
