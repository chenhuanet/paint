《田横五百士》Tianheng and His Five Hundred Followers

中国历史如何变成一座纪念碑？How does Chinese history become a monument?
1930 · 徐悲鸿1930 · Xu Beihong精读Close reading
绘画史Painting History / 精读Close readings
一件作品，一个问题；先看，再判断。One work, one question; look first, judge later.

图片为该作品在本站的主图；元数据见下方作品卡。The image is the primary image for this work; metadata follows in the work card.

问题Question

徐悲鸿把田横与五百士画成一群有体积、有表情的人，英雄站在中间，群众向两边退开。画面用西方写实的身体来重述中国历史，让“忠义”获得公共纪念碑式的重量。

Xu Beihong paints Tianheng and his five hundred followers as a group of volumetric, expressive bodies, the hero at the centre, the crowd withdrawing. He uses Western realist bodies to retell Chinese history, giving loyalty the weight of a public monument.

语境Context

1930年布面油画，藏于徐悲鸿纪念馆。徐悲鸿早年留学法国，回国后推动写实主义美术教育。

Oil on canvas, 1930, held by the Xu Beihong Memorial Museum. Xu studied in France and later promoted realist art education in China.

怎么看How to look

  • 先看英雄：他为什么在中间？
  • 再看群众：他们在离开还是靠近？
  • 最后看姿态：谁站得最直？
  • Read the hero: why is he centred?
  • Then read the crowd: leaving or approaching?
  • Finally read posture: who stands straightest?

为什么重要Why it matters

它把中国历史画从文人手卷变成公共纪念碑。

It turns Chinese history painting from literati handscroll into public monument.
