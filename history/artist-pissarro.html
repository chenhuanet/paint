卡米耶·毕沙罗Camille Pissarro

毕沙罗是印象派的核心人物，也是唯一参加全部八次印象派展览的画家。他画乡村、果园、市场、城市大道和窗户里的灯，笔触短而碎，颜色却始终有结构。Pissarro was a central Impressionist and the only painter to show in all eight Impressionist exhibitions. He painted countryside, orchards, markets, boulevards and lit windows; strokes are short and broken, but colour always has structure.

小传Biography

《蒙马特大道·夜色》把城市的灯、车、行人和湿路面画成一片移动的光；《巴黎圣拉扎尔街》是石版画，同一座城市被转成黑白线条；《咖啡壶静物》则把印象派的颜色收进桌面。毕沙罗的民主不在题材，而在观看方式：每个人都占同样的笔触。

The Boulevard Montmartre at Night turns lamps, carriages, pedestrians and wet pavement into moving light; Rue Saint-Lazare is a lithograph, the same city translated into black line; Still Life with a Coffeepot pulls Impressionist colour back onto a table. Pissarro's democracy is not subject matter but looking: every person gets the same stroke.

看灯与湿地面：城市的光从哪里反射？Read lamps and wet pavement: where does the city's light reflect?
