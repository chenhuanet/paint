拉斐尔Raphael

拉斐尔是盛期文艺复兴最会“安排”的画家。他不像达·芬奇那样神秘，也不像米开朗基罗那样暴烈；他的天赋是把复杂的人、观点和空间整理成一个让人愿意停留的秩序。Raphael is the great arranger of the High Renaissance. He is less mysterious than Leonardo and less violent than Michelangelo; his gift is to organise complex people, ideas and space into an order where the eye wants to stay.

小传Biography

《雅典学院》把柏拉图、亚里士多德、苏格拉底、毕达哥拉斯、欧几里得等不同时代的人请进同一间厅堂。柏拉手指天，亚里士多德手掌向下，两种哲学在画面中央分开；建筑透视把所有人包进一个理性的穹顶。拉斐尔的“和谐”不是没有冲突，而是让冲突可以同处一室。

The School of Athens brings Plato, Aristotle, Socrates, Pythagoras and Euclid from different eras into one hall. Plato points up, Aristotle holds his palm down; two philosophies divide at the center, while architectural perspective wraps everyone in a rational dome. Raphael's harmony is not the absence of conflict but the ability to let conflict share a room.

看两只手：一只指天，一只向下。看建筑：它把谁包在里面？Read the two hands: one points up, one down. Read the architecture: whom does it enclose?
