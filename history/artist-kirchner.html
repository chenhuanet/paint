小传Biography

《柏林街景》把城市画成一种神经质的节奏：人物的脸像面具，衣服像刀片，街道从两边向中间挤。基尔希纳不是记录柏林，他画的是柏林给人的压迫感。

Street, Berlin turns the city into a nervous rhythm: faces like masks, clothes like blades, the street squeezing in from both sides. Kirchner does not record Berlin; he paints the pressure Berlin exerts.

看街道的斜线：它把人物推向哪里？Read the street's diagonals: where do they push the figures?
