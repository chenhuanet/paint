老卢卡斯·克拉纳赫Lucas Cranach the Elder

克拉纳赫是德国文艺复兴的画家、版画家和印刷商，长期在维滕贝格为萨克森选侯工作，也是宗教改革的重要图像制造者。他画过大量宗教画、肖像、神话和狩猎场景，人物身体细长，色彩鲜亮而平。Cranach was a German Renaissance painter, printmaker and publisher who worked for the Saxon electors at Wittenberg and became a key image-maker of the Reformation. He produced religious pictures, portraits, myths and hunts; bodies are slender, colour bright and flat.

小传Biography

《基督受洗》把神圣事件放在一片可辨识的河岸和树上，约翰的手、天使的衣、基督的脚都清楚；《哈尔滕费尔斯城堡附近的狩猎活动》则把宫廷娱乐画成一张权力地图。克拉纳赫的宗教与世俗并不分裂，它们共用同一套视觉语言。

The Baptism of Christ places a sacred event on a recognisable riverbank with trees; John's hand, the angel's cloth and Christ's feet are all clear. Hunting near Hartenfels Castle turns court entertainment into a map of power. Cranach's sacred and secular worlds are not split; they share one visual language.
