《千里江山图》A Thousand Li of Rivers and Mountains

十八岁的画家如何画出一个没走遍的天下？How does an eighteen-year-old paint a realm he never walked?
1113 · 王希孟1113 · Wang Ximeng精读Close reading
绘画史Painting History / 精读Close readings
一件作品，一个问题；先看，再判断。One work, one question; look first, judge later.

图片为该作品在本站的主图；元数据见下方作品卡。The image is the primary image for this work; metadata follows in the work card.

问题Question

《千里江山图》用石青、石绿把山脊变成发光的矿物。它不是地图，而是一个被想象出来的国土：颜色越亮，王朝越像可以携带的天下。

A Thousand Li of Rivers and Mountains turns ridges into glowing minerals with azurite and malachite. It is not a map but an imagined territory: the brighter the colour, the more the dynasty becomes a portable realm.

语境Context

北宋青绿山水长卷，绢本设色，藏于北京故宫博物院。王希孟在十八岁前后完成此卷，由宋徽宗时期宫廷支持。

A Northern Song blue-green landscape handscroll in ink and colour on silk, held by the Palace Museum, Beijing. Wang Ximeng completed it around the age of eighteen with court support under Emperor Huizong.

怎么看How to look

  • 先看石青：哪里最亮？
  • 再看山脊：它是轮廓还是建筑？
  • 最后看长卷：哪里可以住人？
  • Start with azurite: where is it brightest?
  • Then read ridges: contour or architecture?
  • Finally read the scroll: where could people live?

为什么重要Why it matters

它证明颜色可以承担国土想象，而不只是“好看”。

It proves colour can carry territorial imagination, not merely decorate.
