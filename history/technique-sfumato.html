晕涂Sfumato

晕涂是把颜色和轮廓处理成烟雾一样的过渡，让边缘消失。达·芬奇不是用线把人物封起来，而是让光、空气和皮肤互相渗透；因此《蒙娜丽莎》的微笑没有一条明确的边界，它随着观看角度变化。Sfumato treats colour and contour as smoke-like transitions, letting edges disappear. Leonardo does not seal the figure with line; light, air and skin permeate one another. The smile of the *Mona Lisa* therefore has no clear boundary and changes with the angle of looking.

怎么做How it is done

  • 先铺一层薄薄的中间色。
  • 用极细的笔把明暗交界处反复过渡。
  • 不画硬边，让轮廓在背景里消失。
  • Lay a thin middle tone.
  • Blend the boundary of light and dark with a very fine brush.
  • Avoid hard edges; let contour dissolve into the ground.
