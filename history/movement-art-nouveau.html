新艺术运动Art Nouveau

新艺术运动把曲线、植物、女性身体和装饰性平面带进绘画、海报、建筑和家具。它反对学院派的僵硬，也反对工业品的粗糙，想让“现代生活”看起来像一条会流动的线。克里姆特的《吻》把这种曲线变成金箔上的两个人。Art Nouveau brought curve, plant, female body and decorative plane into painting, posters, architecture and furniture. It opposed academic stiffness and industrial ugliness, wanting modern life to look like a flowing line. Klimt's *The Kiss* turns that curve into two people on gold.

先看三个特征，再看三件作品；最后把它放回它所在的章节。Start with three traits, then three works; finally put it back into its chapter.

三个特征Three traits

  • 线是生命：植物、头发、衣褶和建筑边缘共用同一种曲线。
  • 装饰即内容：金箔、图案、平面不是背景，而是情感的表面。
  • 总体艺术：绘画、海报、家具、建筑共享一套视觉语言。
  • Line is life: plants, hair, drapery and architectural edges share one curve.
  • Decoration is content: gold, pattern and flat plane are the surface of feeling, not background.
  • Total art: painting, poster, furniture and architecture share one visual language.

怎么看How to look

  • 先看线：它从哪里开始，在哪里缠住人？
  • 再看金箔：它让画面更亮，还是更冷？
  • 最后看身体：两个人是一个形，还是两个形？
  • Start with line: where does it begin, where does it entangle the body?
  • Then read gold: does it warm the picture, or make it colder?
  • Finally read the bodies: one shape, or two?
