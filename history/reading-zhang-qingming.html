《清明上河图》Along the River During the Qingming Festival

城市如何被画成一台机器？How is a city painted as a machine?
约1085–1145 · 张择端ca.1085–1145 · Zhang Zeduan精读Close reading
绘画史Painting History / 精读Close readings
一件作品，一个问题；先看，再判断。One work, one question; look first, judge later.

图片为该作品在本站的主图；元数据见下方作品卡。The image is the primary image for this work; metadata follows in the work card.

问题Question

张择端不靠透视，而靠遮挡、密度和视线方向制造空间。越靠近虹桥，人物越密，动作越急；桥上的人看船，船上的人看桥，画外的我们看所有人。

Zhang Zeduan creates space through occlusion, density and gaze rather than perspective. Near Rainbow Bridge figures thicken and gestures quicken; people on the bridge watch the boat, people in the boat watch the bridge, and we watch everyone.

语境Context

北宋风俗长卷，绢本设色，藏于北京故宫博物院。画面从郊野、汴河、虹桥一直画到城门和街市。

A Northern Song genre handscroll in ink and colour on silk, held by the Palace Museum, Beijing. It moves from countryside to the Bian River, Rainbow Bridge, city gate and market.

怎么看How to look

  • 先找虹桥：谁在看船？
  • 再看船：谁在撑篙，谁在指挥？
  • 最后看城门：城市在哪里结束？
  • Find Rainbow Bridge: who watches the boat?
  • Then read the boat: who poles, who directs?
  • Finally read the gate: where does the city end?

为什么重要Why it matters

它把城市从背景变成主角，也让“社会”成为绘画可以处理的对象。

It turns the city from backdrop into protagonist and makes society a subject painting can handle.
