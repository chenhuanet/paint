文森特·梵高Vincent van Gogh

梵高只画了十年，却改变了绘画的方向。他用厚涂、旋转的笔触和纯色，把情绪直接画进画面：麦田会燃烧，夜空会流动，向日葵的黄色厚到可以投下阴影。Van Gogh painted for only ten years, yet changed the direction of painting. He uses impasto, swirling strokes and pure colour to put emotion directly on canvas: wheat fields burn, night skies flow, sunflower yellow is thick enough to cast a shadow.

小传Biography

《星月夜》的星星有燃烧的晕圈，柏树像黑色的火苗，村庄在下面安静地睡。梵高不是画“夜空”，而是画“看见夜空时的我”。他的书信和画一样重要：它们让我们知道，每一笔都是思考过的。

In The Starry Night, stars have burning halos, the cypress is a black flame, the village sleeps below. Van Gogh does not paint 'the night sky' but 'me seeing the night sky'. His letters matter as much as his paintings: they show that every mark was thought.

看笔触：它在旋转吗？看黄色：它为什么像在发光？Read the stroke: is it rotating? Read the yellow: why does it seem to glow?
