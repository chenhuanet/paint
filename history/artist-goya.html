弗朗西斯科·戈雅Francisco Goya

戈雅是宫廷画家，也是战争的目击者。他前半生画贵族肖像和挂毯，后半生画屠杀、疯人、宗教裁判和“理性睡着后出现的怪物”。他让西班牙绘画第一次直视暴力。Goya was a court painter and a witness of war. He spent his early career on aristocratic portraits and tapestries, his later career on massacres, madhouses, the Inquisition and 'the monsters that appear when reason sleeps'. He made Spanish painting look at violence directly.

小传Biography

《1808年5月3日》里，枪口贴着人脸，白衬衫上的血是唯一的灯；《农神吞噬其子》把权力画成一个正在吃孩子的巨神。戈雅不提供安慰，他提供见证。

In The Third of May 1808, rifles press against faces, blood on a white shirt is the only light; Saturn Devouring His Son paints power as a god eating his child. Goya offers no consolation, only witness.

看白衬衫：光为什么落在它上面？看怪物：它在替谁说话？Read the white shirt: why does light fall there? Read the monster: whom does it speak for?
