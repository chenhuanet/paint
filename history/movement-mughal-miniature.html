莫卧儿细密画Mughal Miniature

莫卧儿细密画把波斯书的版面、印度的人物传统和欧洲的透视放进同一张纸。它既画宫廷、狩猎、战争和爱情，也画一只羚羊、一棵树和一场梦。细密画的大小要求观看靠近：你必须把眼睛放到纸面上，才能看见权力和诗的细节。Mughal miniature joins Persian book layout, Indian figure traditions and European perspective on one page. It paints court, hunt, war and love, but also an antelope, a tree and a dream. Its scale demands closeness: you must bring your eye to the paper to see the details of power and poetry.

绘画史Painting History / 流派Movements
先看三个特征，再看三件作品；最后把它放回它所在的章节。Start with three traits, then three works; finally put it back into its chapter.

三个特征Three traits

  • 书与画同体：细密画常是手抄本插图，图像服务于文本，也和文本争空间。
  • 宫廷与自然并置：人物、动物、花园、建筑被组织成可读的符号系统。
  • 近距离观看：画面小，细节密，观看是一种身体动作。
  • Book and image are one body: miniatures illustrate manuscripts, serve the text and compete with it for space.
  • Court and nature together: figures, animals, gardens and architecture form a readable symbolic system.
  • Close viewing: small page, dense detail, looking is a bodily act.

怎么看How to look

  • 先找文本框：图像是被文字包围，还是冲出了边框？
  • 再看人物比例：谁被画得更大，为什么？
  • 最后看动物与花园：它们在装饰，还是在叙事？
  • Find the text frame: is the image contained by words, or escaping it?
  • Read scale: who is painted larger, and why?
  • Finally read animals and garden: decoration, or narrative?
