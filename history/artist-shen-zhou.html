沈周Shen Zhou

沈周是明代吴门画派的中心人物，长居苏州，出身书香与地产之家。他不靠卖画为生，却把文人画从“业余消遣”变成一种可持续的工作方式：写诗、写字、看园林、教学生，也把日常风景画成可以反复展读的册页。Shen Zhou is the central figure of the Ming Wu school in Suzhou. Born into a scholarly landowning family, he did not live by selling paintings; he made literati painting a sustainable way of working — poetry, calligraphy, gardens, teaching, and ordinary scenery painted as albums that could be read again and again.

小传Biography

《庐山高图》把师承、山水与祝寿放进同一幅立轴，山体高而密，笔墨却松；《姑苏台十二景》则把虎丘一带的实景拆成十二个可游的片段。沈周的重要不在于“像”，而在于让观看变成一段可以在朋友之间传阅的时间。

Lofty Mount Lu combines lineage, landscape and a birthday dedication in one vertical scroll: the mountain is high and dense, yet the brush remains loose. Twelve Views of Tiger Hill breaks a real Suzhou site into twelve visitable fragments. Shen matters less for likeness than for turning looking into time passed among friends.

先看山体的“密”与笔的“松”：它为什么既重又轻？Read the dense mountain and the loose brush: why is it both heavy and light?
