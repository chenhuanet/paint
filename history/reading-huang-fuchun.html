《富春山居图》Dwelling in the Fuchun Mountains

长卷如何画“住得下来”的时间？How does a handscroll paint inhabitable time?
1347–1350 · 黄公望1347–1350 · Huang Gongwang精读Close reading
绘画史Painting History / 精读Close readings
一件作品，一个问题；先看，再判断。One work, one question; look first, judge later.

图片为该作品在本站的主图；元数据见下方作品卡。The image is the primary image for this work; metadata follows in the work card.

问题Question

黄公望用披麻皴画土坡，线条松而厚，墨色层层积染。画面不追求奇峰，而追求“平远”：山不高，水不急，人在其中可以呼吸。

Huang Gongwang uses hemp-fibre texture strokes for earthen slopes, loose but dense, with layered ink. He does not seek spectacular peaks but 'level distance': low mountains, slow water, room to breathe.

语境Context

元代纸本水墨长卷，现分藏浙江省博物馆与台北故宫博物院。黄公望晚年隐居富春江。

A Yuan handscroll in ink on paper, now divided between the Zhejiang Museum and the National Palace Museum, Taipei. Huang Gongwang spent his late years near the Fuchun River.

怎么看How to look

  • 先看山势：哪里起，哪里停？
  • 再看树：它们是路标还是居民？
  • 最后看空白：那是水还是雾？
  • Read the mountain: where does it begin and rest?
  • Then read trees: signposts or inhabitants?
  • Finally read blank: water or mist?

为什么重要Why it matters

它把山水从“景观”变成“可以生活的地方”。

It turns landscape from view into a place one could live.
